# W1 Executive Brief — Agent Skills tooling opportunity (evidence as of 2026-09-10)

Source: 9 research angles + completeness critic + 6 gap-fills (16 agents, ~1,500 tool calls). Full digest with URLs: `w1-digest.md` (same directory; ~370K chars, grep it by angle name, e.g. `[platform-absorption-risk] F12`). Citations below use [angle:F#].

## A. Is "skills" durable? YES as a format; the format is a commodity.
- agentskills.io showcase lists 46 clients (Claude, Codex/ChatGPT, Copilot, VS Code, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Kiro, JetBrains, Databricks, Snowflake, Spring AI, Goose, OpenHands…) plus Zed, Devin, Antigravity, Copilot Studio, AWS AgentCore, GitLab. `.agents/skills/` is the cross-client path. Spec repo 25.2k stars. [platform-vendors:F1][open-source:F1][gapfill-6:F11]
- Snyk telemetry (Jun 2026): "1 in 4 developers has at least one skill installed, averaging 18". [gapfill-6:F10]
- The spec is deliberately thin: 6 frontmatter fields; NO version, dependency, eval, telemetry, provenance, composition fields. Two eval formats are forking inside Anthropic (skill-creator evals.json vs `claude plugin eval` cases/graders); neither is in the spec; spec maintainers do merge eval-contract docs. [platform-vendors:F2][gapfill-3:F7]
- Packaging is splitting: Agent Plugins 1.0 (Aug 2026; TSC = Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI, Vercel; Google in GitHub changelog) vs Anthropic's `.claude-plugin`. Tessl migrated tiles→plugins to follow. [platform-vendors:F9][gapfill-5:F7]
- Community consensus on what survives model upgrades: org-specific procedure, exact commands, deterministic scripts, checkable gates. Generic process skills are being pruned ("snake oil" top HN comment, 68 replies; 12/13 r/codex users dropped superpowers). Claude Code creator advises deleting skills every 6 months (secondary source). [practitioner:F6,F7][gapfill-4:F7]

## F. Historical analogies (decision rules)
- Vendor-adjacent authoring/eval/experimentation tooling = most-absorbed category of 2025 (Humanloop, Statsig, marimo, Noteable). Absorption cycle compressed from years to quarters (VS Code bracket colorizer; Cursor /migrate-to-skills). [historical:F1,F2]
- Survivors owned something the platform didn't: cross-platform SDK + data gravity (Sentry $100M rev; Zapier), the OSS core (n8n $5.2B on ~$40M ARR; Chromatic/Storybook), a proprietary workflow artifact (Postman collections), or deep vertical data. None were "authoring/eval tooling for a single vendor's format". Breadth without data gravity died (Kite: 500k devs, $0). [historical:F5,F6]
- Individual developers don't pay; teams pay for workspaces; managers pay for discrete capabilities or compliance. Vendor-run AI stores pay builders nothing (GPT Store 2 years). Registries are cost centers (npm→GitHub, Smithery→Arcade acqui-hire, 4 overlapping MCP registries). [historical:F6,F7,F10]
- Salesforce DevOps analog VERIFIED: native DevOps Center (GA Dec 2022, free) did NOT absorb independents — 2025 survey: DevOps Center 8% devs vs 30.8% third-party vs 37.3% DIY CLI. But the winner was Gearset: bootstrapped, profitable at 1,700 customers before its only $55M round (2022), product-led, developer-loved, compliance as tier upsell; +49% revenue 2024, 3,000+ customers. Copado (compliance-first, $257M raised) grew slower. Survival preconditions — a versioned Metadata API with 3-year support commitment, a vendor that took 3 years to ship a minimal native v1, installed base, adjacent jobs the vendor declined — DO NOT transfer to skills (no governed API, vendor ships eval tooling in weeks, no installed base, adjacent jobs are exactly what the vendor builds). Window in the analog was ~3.5 years and is now closing (Next-Gen DevOps Center Spring '26 bundles tests, DORA, MCP control). Category size: ~$197M revenue (2021) atop a $30B platform. [gapfill-2:F1-F10]
