# Audit: Production archaeology of the golden sample (src/content/blogs/2026-08-19, deepseek-harness-teardown)

> Generated 2026-08-22 by a multi-agent audit of tiles/blog-production and its chain. Evidence cites working-tree file:line at audit time.

## Summary

Nothing in this package is committed (git log shows zero commits touching 2026-08-19; the whole thing is working tree), so the reconstruction is from file mtimes (machine clock is MDT; Aaron was on +03:00, so every "2026-08-16" in the docs is Aug 15 afternoon/evening MDT). The package went idea.md (Aug 15 01:38 MDT) to final video-qa-report.md (Aug 16 01:41 MDT) in 24h03m of essentially continuous agent work, with YouTube upload at ~23:50 MDT. Roughly 9.5h went to the article (13 draft versions, one genuine editorial pivot at v4), 0.5h to images (6 finals, zero re-rolls), ~10h to the video (a 12:42 v1 slide render that was thrown away, a 5.5h bespoke Remotion build, two 10-minute masters), and ~2h to post-upload bookkeeping in which most of the "gates" (director-plan.json + audit, package-state.json, postmortem.md, blog-package-quality.ts changes) were created AFTER the upload. The genuinely strong parts are the evidence discipline (claim-ledger C1-C31, a 22-agent/68-check HEAD recheck, a narration word-stream fidelity gate that caught three silent TTS assembler bugs) and the fast, first-pass-accepted image set. The expensive parts were workflow-caused: the v1 video repeated an art direction rejected on 2026-08-03 because the skill's own "video richness gate" (20-30 images every 15-20s) pushes the slide-deck format; 15 of 23 generated PNGs were wasted on that; TTS was billed 3.5x the final narration length because render-time resegmentation invalidates the cache and a neighbor-invalidation rule regenerates adjacent slides; a one-word factual fix ("a week" -> "a few days") that 13 drafts, three judges, a red-team and a 68-check recheck all missed forced a third audio master, a 27-scene remap, a re-render and a re-upload. The film is 1,582 lines of hand-written scene code plus 1,227 lines of generated data, imports nothing shared, and overlaps the previous film by 112 boilerplate lines; "ledger-editorial-v1" is a 35-line prose doc plus a JSON palette, not code, so the next film will be rebuilt from scratch. The storyboard's "templates" are labels in JSON that no code implements. Of ~50 markdown/JSON artifacts outside the media, Aaron plausibly read 6-8; the rest exist to satisfy gates, several of which contradict each other (director-plan.json names clean-indigo as the style while package-state.json promotes ledger-editorial-v1).

## Strengths

- **Evidence discipline is real and load-bearing, not theater** — claim-ledger.md (21,668 bytes, C1-C31, all repo facts pinned to commit 47f9438); head-recheck-2026-08-15.md: '22 个并行验证 agent ... 共 68 项子检查: 56 VERIFIED / 9 MISMATCH / 3 AMBIGUOUS' with file:line for each, run at 09:59 before article lock (10:23); red-team-review.md R1-R5 all applied with a recorded delta; video-qa-report.md:53 re-verified the release date from GitHub API + Wayback + HN + changelog after Aaron flagged wording.
  - Why it matters: This is the part of the chain that makes the piece 'operator-grade' rather than news. It is also the only part that would survive productization unchanged — the ledger + recheck pattern is already a repeatable script (handoff.md:35 names the replayable workflow script).
- **Narration fidelity gate caught three silent TTS assembler failure modes and became permanent tooling** — audio-generation.md 'Render-Time Regeneration Record' + 'v2 Full-Stream Verification': dropped trailing segment, leaked '---SEGMENT---' delimiter, duplicated tail segments (~2 min of repeated narration in the 12:42 v1). tiles/aaron-video-gen/scripts/verify-narration-fidelity.ts:1-22 documents all three and exits 1 on any word mismatch (new file, 15:30 Aug 15).
  - Why it matters: This is exactly the kind of machine gate that should replace prose gates: it turned an 'inspect by ear' step into a deterministic check that runs after every audio/render pass.
- **Image production is fast and first-pass: 6 finals + 2 alternates in ~15 minutes, zero re-rolls** — imgs/ mtimes: visual-strategy/style/outline/prompts 12:30-12:33; 00-cover candidates 12:36/12:37; body 01-04 12:36-12:41; imgs/web/*.webp 12:43. imgs/visual-postmortem.md: 'all six finals accepted on the first generation pass, zero re-rolls'; PNG 2.4-4.0 MB -> WebP 42-185 KB.
  - Why it matters: Concept-before-style with explicit predicates and an exact-text thumbnail rule produced a thumbnail that YouTube's hqdefault confirmed (video-qa-report.md:65). The blog-illustrate sub-chain is the most productizable piece of the media side.
- **The film has a real visual thesis and the QA record is specific, measured, and honest about accepted variances** — director-memo.md: 'The film is a session log' grammar; video-qa-report.md: 27/27 scene stills inspected, -16.7 LUFS / -4.1 dBTP, last 0.4s at -91 dB, 'Known accepted variances' and 'Deliberate Article/Film Divergence' sections explicitly scoped.
  - Why it matters: The editorial ambition (one grammar that IS the argument) is the thing 老高/小林-class explainers have and slide decks don't. Protect the treatment/memo step; it is the cheapest, highest-leverage 40 minutes in the whole timeline (15:19-16:02).
- **Prototype-slice-before-full-render is the right structure** — video-prototype-ledger-log-chapter.mp4 (82.56s, 5.7 MB) rendered 16:02, before the 21:57 v2 full render; video-treatment.md: 'a 60–90s prototype slice (must contain calm, structured, and the signature scene) for Aaron's review before any full render'.
  - Why it matters: A 10-minute 1080p render takes >10 min (remotion-render.ts timeout raised to 30 min); the prototype gate is what keeps full renders to 2-3 instead of 08-02's six.
