# critic-completeness

> Adversarial critique over all eight audits, 2026-08-22.

## Verdict

The eight audits are thorough on mechanism and almost silent on the operator. Together they propose roughly 70 work items (about 25 of them M or L) for a system whose owner budgets 3-4 hours a week for blog plus video (src/brain/life/routine.md, Weekly Time Budget: "博客 + 视频 ~3-4h"), reviews content in two fixed daily slots (6:30 "YouTube 审核", 23:00 "GG Sync"), and whose self-node says "Don't perform - rhythm beats spikes" and "1 piece of content/week" (src/brain/world/people/aaron.md). The golden sample consumed one 24-hour continuous agent run plus at least five Aaron interventions (v4 rejection, six directives, audio listening, a morning film review that found two defects, a late factual flag that forced a re-upload); no auditor converted that into Aaron-minutes per package or defined the unit of human input. The strategic layer that should decide tiering and video slots is dormant: src/brain/goals/current-quarter.md is an empty Q2 template, weekly reviews stop at 2026-W16, the journal has no entries after June, and x.md still carries February targets nobody checked. The pipeline is not cold-start (two series are queued through 10-07, and the DSH series has the genuinely distinctive rule that each post requires an experiment in Aaron's own stack - handoff.md:44, entry-fee-experiment.md, agent-decisions/rejected/), but the backlog is text-only: 9 scored packages, 1 video, 0 planned video slots. The biggest factual correction to the audits: the benchmark evidence shows 老高 is narration-first with near-zero visuals and 小Lin is PPT-grade charts, so the gap to those channels is topic selection, curiosity arc, delivery prosody and a recurring device, not cut density; the video auditor's "opposite product" conclusion from the 57%-static measurement overreaches, and the calm ledger grammar is compatible with the target. Missing entirely: cost accounting (API spend is small; agent tokens dominate and are untracked, while series B is literally about measuring that); rights closure (Eleven Music rights still "publication-gated" in video-qa-report.md:27 while the film is public; no synthetic-voice disclosure field anywhere in youtube-upload.ts); a calibrated definition of "professional" (no reference video, no benchmark channel named anywhere in the repo); and the conflict between productization and personal voice (the value is Aaron's taste gates and lived experiments; the sellable part is the harness, not the content). Net verdict: protect the editorial evidence discipline and the experiment-prerequisite rule, freeze the gate count, spend the next month on four things only - a tier switch, a cost/time ledger, one reusable film system, and the first real retention readback - and explicitly defer the rest.

## Protect

## Over-engineering risks

- **The combined audit roadmap (~70 items, ~25 M/L: artifacts.json + package-state CLI + per-artifact JSON schemas + telemetry runs[] + golden fixture regression + source-teardown tile + illustration-package-check + encoded-master-audit + preflight-master + contact-sheet + BlogThumbnail composition + editorial primitives extraction + ZH lane + posting automation for 4 channels + repurpose tile + eval runners + doctor.sh + CONTRIBUTING + brand-tokens.json).**
  - Why risky: routine.md budgets 3-4 h/week for content and 5-7 h/week for OrgNext (the stated big bet). Executed in full this is 3-6 months of engineering with zero new published packages, exactly the 'spike' the self-node warns against. The system already grew blog-production 109->352 lines and aaron-video-gen 130->775 lines in ten weeks with no release notes; adding 20 more scripts repeats the pattern that produced the current sprawl.
  - Alternative: One-month freeze on new gates and new prose. Pick four items (tier switch, cost/time ledger, reusable film primitives, first analytics readback) and require that every other proposal be justified by a defect observed in the next two packages, recorded in src/brain/agent-decisions/.
- **Proposals to machine-enforce judgment gates: parse lock lines, count red-team bullets >=5, 70%-per-dimension scorecard rule, 'judge: independent' marker, scorecard hash binding, angle-memo with evidence-density scoring, structural-redraft regeneration of plan/memo on pivot.**
  - Why risky: Archaeology shows the scorecard scored v4 and v12 identically (89) and the decisive signal came from Aaron, not from any artifact. Enforcing more structure on artifacts Aaron does not read (research-dossier cited once, memory-reflection once, editorial-brief twice) raises agent cost and makes pivots more expensive, which is the opposite of what the v4->v5 moment needed.
  - Alternative: Shrink the editorial front half to the artifacts with proven lift: claim ledger, recheck, red-team, one-page argument brief that Aaron approves before drafting. Make the scorecard a 10-line contract diff. Measure 'Aaron review rounds per package' and aim to cut it, not the gate count to raise it.
- **Video auditor's recommendation to build a second 'illustrated-story' treatment family with Ken Burns, 2-layer parallax, character bible, co-host voice, AI b-roll inserts, and a ZH lane with a recorded Mandarin PVC sample set (L + L + M).**
  - Why risky: Misreads the benchmark: 老高's 2026 format is a black screen with captions; 小Lin is host + PPT charts; 回形针's rule is information density, not motion. Building a motion-heavy family to chase a target that does not need it would burn the next two films on renderer work. Also voice-profiles.json already uses eleven_multilingual_v2, which speaks Mandarin from the same clone; a Mandarin PVC recording is not the blocker.
  - Alternative: Keep ledger-editorial as the visual system. Spend the format budget on narrative: a curiosity-gap cold open, escalating-layer structure, a recurring device (a fictional operator firm, like 小Lin's 奶茶店), and delivery markup for prosody. Run one 60-second ZH narration test with the existing voice id before deciding anything about ZH.
- **Distribution automation for X/LinkedIn/Facebook/Beehiiv (L) plus a repurpose tile (L) plus a brand-sales agent renewal.**
  - Why risky: Posting is a 10-minute manual task per week; automating it before a single 7-day readback exists optimizes the cheapest step. The brand-sales agent produced 32 identical no-op reports in 35 days because it had nothing to close the loop on. Automation without measurement produces more of that.
  - Alternative: Manual posting with a 2-minute 'record-post --channel --url' step; one scheduled nightly ingest + postmortem-fill; re-evaluate posting automation only when a channel shows measurable lift over two packages.
- **Making blog-production 'No.1' on skill registries (skills.sh install counts, agentskills.io packaging, plugin marketplace, Tessl evals) as a near-term goal.**
  - Why risky: Registry ranking rewards generic, install-once skills; Aaron's chain is valuable because it is opinionated and coupled to his practice, voice clone, strategy files and brain. Chasing installs pulls the system toward the 'AI slop' median the benchmark auditor describes, and competes with OrgNext for the same 5-7 h/week CEO budget.
  - Alternative: Define the product as a content harness (state machine, validators, audits, Remotion editorial system, research-recheck module) that a second operator configures with their own profile. Prove it with one external user (a friend's blog) before any registry work. Treat 'No.1' as 'the chain Aaron himself runs every week without dread', measured by packages shipped per month.
- **Three separate proposals for a package-manifest/contract file (orchestrator artifacts.json, skillstack package-manifest.json + schemas/*.schema.json, images canonical naming contract) and two competing tier schemes (note/standard/serious/flagship vs light/serious/teardown).**
  - Why risky: If implemented as proposed they become three more sources of truth in a system that already has three (detect table, package-state.json, handoff.md).
  - Alternative: One file: package-state.json with a tier field and an artifacts map; the validator reads it; the SKILL.md table is generated from it. Four tiers max, chosen at idea.md time, with 'note' routed to blog-notes.
