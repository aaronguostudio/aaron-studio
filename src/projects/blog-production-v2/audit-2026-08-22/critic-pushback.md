# critic-pushback

> Adversarial critique over all eight audits, 2026-08-22.

## Verdict

Verified against the working tree: (1) blog-package-quality.ts:353-357 demands artifacts.video.canonical unconditionally, so a text-only package can never pass --require-release; (2) blog-production/SKILL.md:276 (20-30 images, [IMAGE:] switch every 15-20s) directly contradicts aaron-video-gen/SKILL.md:34 and :626 ('There is no image quota') and is what produced the rejected 12:42 v1 slide render; (3) LedgerHarnessFilm.tsx is 1,582 lines with zero imports from the 1,118-line remotion/src/editorial/ engine; (4) editorial-scorecard.md:30 and :55 give 89/100 to both the rejected v4 and the locked v12, and :59 admits v13 shipped unscored; (5) red-team-review.md (06:02, 'against draft v1') and prose-polish-review.md (06:23) predate the v5 pivot (07:47) and the final (08-16 01:26); (6) 08-19 distribution.json uses url/video_id/publish_date while ingest.mjs:150-162 reads channel_post_id/channel_url/published_at, so the golden sample is invisible to the growth DB; (7) ffmpeg scene-detect at 0.03 finds 0 cuts in the 606s film and the video stream is 208,872 b/s; (8) director-plan.json and package-state.json were written 00:42-00:43, 84 minutes after video-v3. The queue is worse than the orchestrator auditor reported: of the 9 packages after 08-19, 8 carry a PASS claim ledger and an 88-95 scorecard, 1 has a cover (08-21, procedural, never viewed), 0 have video, and one is a Greek-vacation essay. Diagnosis: an editorial bureaucracy with a hand-built film bolted on. The auditors are mostly right about symptoms and mostly wrong about cures: five of eight propose new infrastructure (state-machine CLI, artifacts.json, per-artifact JSON schemas, telemetry arrays, posting automation, repurposing tile, second treatment family, ZH narration lane, source-teardown tile) for a one-person shop shipping one package a week into an orchestrator that already cannot be followed. The 80/20 is the opposite: halve the gate count, put the one human stop where Aaron actually intervened on 08-19 (before drafting), turn the golden film's primitives into data-driven components so the next film costs hours not a day, treat the spoken script as the product (every benchmark channel does), pick one visual family and encode it once, and get two retention curves into the repo before building anything else for distribution. Craft and taste are the bottleneck for the 老高/小林说 goal; reusable film code is the bottleneck for productization; everything else is deferrable.
