# Audit: Orchestrator structure — tiles/blog-production

> Generated 2026-08-22 by a multi-agent audit of tiles/blog-production and its chain. Evidence cites working-tree file:line at audit time.

## Summary

blog-production is a 352-line prose orchestrator wrapped around one genuinely good validator (blog-package-quality.ts, 579 lines, 11 passing tests) and two trivial template-copy scripts. It names 23 quality gates; only about 5 are meaningfully machine-checked by its own scripts, another ~5 are delegated to downstream skills' scripts, and the remaining ~13 are prose or existence-only checks — including all three Production Locks, which no script ever parses. State is split across three places that disagree: the "Detect the next step" heuristic table, package-state.json (whose two real instances use incompatible schemas — the 08-21 one fails the validator with 3 errors), and for 08-19 a hand-written 50-line handoff.md that was needed because nothing could resume the package. The artifact contract lists 38 files but omits the 10 video artifacts aaron-video-gen actually requires, and its video gates still describe the legacy `[IMAGE:]` slide renderer that aaron-video-gen tells agents not to fall back to. There is no fast lane: "serious essay" is never defined, and every one of the 9 packages in the queue (including a Greek-travel personal essay) has a claim ledger and a ≥88 scorecard, while only 3 have a cover and 1 has a video — the editorial front half is over-built relative to the media back half. The editorial system itself (editorial-system.md, templates, the 08-19 revision trail) is the strongest asset and should be protected. Productization is blocked less by hard-coded paths (those are mostly confined to publish-to-blog and config) than by three divergent skill registries, state living in prose, and Codex-specific branches in the image path. The human stops are placed late (audio listening, publish approval) while the one intervention that mattered on 08-19 — Aaron rejecting the v4 angle after it had already passed scorecard 89, red-team and prose polish — happened at a point the workflow does not formally stop.

## Strengths

- **blog-package-quality.ts is a real, tested validator, not theater** — tiles/blog-production/scripts/blog-package-quality.ts:232-247 (frontmatter + canonical taxonomy), :339-377 (package-state schema, canonical video existence and sha256), :468-507 (real PNG/WebP header parsing, ≥1200px, 16:9 ±0.03, byte-identical duplicate detection), :279-326 (X main post ≤280 chars, no URL in main post, per-channel utm_source). `bun test` → 11 pass / 0 fail. Running it on 08-19 with all flags passes and correctly warns that video-v3.mp4 and youtube-script.md are older than the article.
  - Why it matters: This is the kernel a productized orchestrator grows from. It proves the pattern Aaron wants: prose intent promoted to a deterministic check with acceptable false-positive cost (editorial-system.md:73-82).
- **The editorial system (contract → ledger → locks → revision delta) demonstrably produced the golden sample** — tiles/blog-production/references/editorial-system.md:7-17 (seven-decision contract), :63-71 (three locks with stale-asset rule), templates/workflow3/claim-ledger.md (fact/inference/judgment table with source and verification dates). 08-19 package: claim-ledger.md 21,668 bytes (C1-C26, Decision: PASS), editorial-scorecard.md 19,812 bytes with three blind judges (line 17) and a v11→v12 fact-precision delta (line 61), revisions/ holds v3/v4/v5/v11/v12 bilingual snapshots.
  - Why it matters: This is what 'professional-grade' looks like in practice and it is reproducible: 8 later packages all carry the same artifacts with scores 88-95. Protect it; do not let a state-machine refactor flatten it.
- **No-overwrite bootstrap and never-overwrite rule are correct defaults** — workflow2-artifacts.ts:42-45 and workflow3-artifacts.ts:38-41 skip existing files; tested in both .test.ts files; SKILL.md:80 'Never overwrite an existing artifact.'
  - Why it matters: Safe re-entry is the precondition for resumability; the scripts honor it even though the orchestrator prose does not yet exploit it.
- **package-state.json is the right idea and already has a typed schema in code** — blog-package-quality.ts:80-96 defines PackageState with phase enum, artifacts.video.canonical+sha256, release.blog (url, production_commit, branch), postmortem.status. SKILL.md:304 declares it 'the authoritative current-state record; handoff.md and historical QA reports are context, not status.'
  - Why it matters: The seed of a real state machine exists; the gap is that nothing writes it and the schema is only enforced under --require-release.
- **Stale-asset invalidation is stated as a rule, not left implicit** — SKILL.md:203 'If the article changes after Article Lock, mark the affected images, script, audio, video, social copy, and published copy stale'; editorial-system.md:71; validator :513-525 implements an mtime heuristic.
  - Why it matters: Most content pipelines silently ship a video narrated from a superseded draft. The 08-19 validator warning shows the check firing on a real package.

## Gaps

### [high/automation/S] 23 named gates; ~13 are prose-only or existence-only, and none of the three Production Locks is machine-read

- Evidence: SKILL.md §4 lines 168-316 names 23 bold gates. Scripted by blog-production's own validator with semantic content: Editorial scorecard (score regex only, :463-464 — the 'no dimension below 70% of weight' rule at SKILL.md:196 is NOT checked), Taxonomy (:244-246), Distribution plan (:279-326), Image quality (dims/ratio/dup only; the 'three concepts, two candidates' rule at SKILL.md:232 is not checked), Package integrity, Package state. Existence-only: Workflow 3 artifacts (:453-456), Red-team ('at least five issues' SKILL.md:192 — never counted), Prose polish, Memory update (canon-note.md exists), Canon alignment. Delegated to other skills' scripts: Anti-AI style (blog-write/scripts/blog-style-quality.ts), Video script audit (aaron-video-gen/scripts/audit-script.ts), Audio manifest, Reinforcement/Pre-publish (scripts/blog-growth.mjs). Pure prose: Editorial contract, Article depth (7 bullets, SKILL.md:181-190), Production lock gate (:198-203), Reusable asset gate, Video adaptation gate, Video richness gate, Publishing gate (browser QA). `grep -c 'Lock: *(PASS|pass)'` finds lock lines in every package's scorecard, but blog-package-quality.ts never greps for 'Argument Lock'/'Article Lock'/'Package Lock'.
- Recommendation: Cheap scriptable wins (all S): (1) parse the three lock lines in editorial-scorecard.md and mirror them into package-state.locks; refuse --require-images when Article Lock != PASS, refuse --require-release when Package Lock != PASS. (2) Enforce the per-dimension 70% rule by parsing the scorecard table (weights are fixed in the template). (3) Count non-empty bullet items under the seven issue headings in red-team-review.md and require ≥5. (4) Require claim-ledger.md 'Verified on:' date ≥ article date and ≥1 table row per external link in the article. (5) Require video-brief.md mtime/hash precede youtube-script.md. Leave Article depth, Canon alignment, Editorial contract as judgment gates but give each a one-line 'Decision: PASS/FAIL + date' footer the validator can check, like claim-ledger already does.

### [high/structure/M] Three sources of truth for state, and the two real package-state.json files use incompatible schemas

- Evidence: (a) SKILL.md:117-151 'Detect the next step' — 29 missing-artifact rows. (b) package-state.json typed at blog-package-quality.ts:80-96 with schema_version, slug, phase enum. (c) 08-19/handoff.md (50 lines, hand-written 2026-08-15) whose line 3 says 'current status is package-state.json; do not treat this file's next steps as todo' — i.e. it went stale the same week. 08-21/package-state.json keys: ['artifacts','gates','locks','phase','post','postmortem_status','production_stack','publish','updated_at'] vs 08-19: ['artifacts','locks','phase','postmortem','release','schema_version','slug','updated_at','visual_style']. 08-21 phase is the free string 'local-published-qa-passed-awaiting-aroon-approval'. Running the validator on 08-21 with --require-release: 3 errors ('must use schema_version 1', "slug 'undefined'", 'must declare artifacts.video.canonical'). Nothing writes package-state.json: grep shows only blog-production SKILL/validator and aaron-video-gen/SKILL.md:730 reference it; publish-to-blog/SKILL.md does not, despite SKILL.md:304 'after every publish'.
- Recommendation: Make package-state.json the only state and generate the other two from it. Concretely: add `scripts/package-state.ts` with `init`, `set-phase`, `record-lock`, `record-release`, `record-artifact` subcommands that validate against a JSON schema (publish schema_version 2 with an explicit `tier` and `video: none|planned|...`). Replace the Detect-the-next-step table with a `next` subcommand that reads state + filesystem and prints the next phase, the skill to load, and the gate to run — the SKILL.md table becomes its documentation. Have publish-to-blog and aaron-video-gen call `record-release`/`record-artifact` rather than editing JSON by hand. handoff.md becomes unnecessary: `next --explain` prints the resume briefing. This is worth it — 08-19 needed a human to write the resume brief, and 08-21 already drifted schema within two days.

### [medium/quality/S] Validator bug: text-only packages can never pass --require-release

- Evidence: blog-package-quality.ts:353-357 errors 'must declare artifacts.video.canonical when a video package exists' unconditionally — there is no check for whether a video exists. 08-21 is a no-video package (package-state 'no video/audio in scope') and fails on exactly this. The 08-21 state file's self-reported 'package_quality: pass' was therefore run without --require-release.
- Recommendation: Only require artifacts.video.canonical when any video*.mp4 exists in the dir or state declares video in scope; add a test for the text-only published package. Also stop treating a self-reported 'pass' string in package-state as evidence — have the validator write its own result block into state.

### [high/structure/M] Orchestrator's artifact contract and video gates describe the legacy slide renderer, not the pipeline that made the golden sample

- Evidence: SKILL.md:29-68 contract lists 38 artifacts and omits fact-pack.json, video-treatment.md, director-plan.json, director-memo.md, asset-decision-log.md, asset-plan.json, video-storyboard.json, video-qa-report.md, director-plan-audit.md, video-storyboard-audit.md — all of which aaron-video-gen/SKILL.md:64-76 requires for a serious essay and all of which exist in 08-19. Detect-the-next-step (:142-149) has zero rows for treatment/director-plan/storyboard. Video richness gate (:276) counts `[IMAGE:]` switches and 'video-only sNN-MM-*.png'; aaron-video-gen/SKILL.md:34 says 'Do not silently fall back to the legacy slide-deck renderer' and :272-274 says youtube-script.md 'does not carry the full directorial plan; video-storyboard.json does'. blog-write/SKILL.md:23 still calls youtube-script.md 'Slide-based script'. Contract row ownership is ambiguous in 5 rows (claim-ledger 'blog-brainstorm / blog-production', youtube-script-audit and youtube-metadata 'blog-write / aaron-video-gen', editorial-scorecard and canon-note 'blog-production / blog-write'). x-teaser.md survives at SKILL.md:322 and blog-brainstorm/SKILL.md:316.
- Recommendation: Move the artifact contract out of prose into `tiles/blog-production/artifacts.json` (name, phase, owner skill, required-for tiers, producing command, validator). Both the validator's SERIOUS/VISUAL/DISTRIBUTION arrays and the `next` command read it; downstream skills import it for their own output tables. Rewrite the video rows of Detect-the-next-step to the Video Workflow 2.0 sequence (video-brief → fact-pack → youtube-script + audit → video-treatment → director-plan + audit → storyboard + audit → audio → prototype → video-vN → video-qa-report). Delete x-teaser.md and 'slide-based' wording. One owner per artifact.

### [high/product/M] No tiering: every post is forced through Workflow 3 and 'serious essay' is never defined

- Evidence: SKILL.md:124-129 conditions on 'serious essay and no X' with no definition anywhere; no flag, no frontmatter field, no state field. All 9 packages from 08-17 to 10-07 have claim-ledger Decision: PASS and Final score 88-95, including 08-17 'a-greek-trip-i-couldnt-take-without-ai' (a personal travel essay) and 09-09 which is still only idea.md. blog-notes (tiles/blog-notes/SKILL.md, 101 lines) exists as a lightweight lane but blog-production never routes to it. Meanwhile only 3/9 packages have imgs/web/00-cover.webp and 1/9 has a video.
- Recommendation: Add `tier` to package-state (note | standard | serious | flagship) chosen at idea.md time. note → route to blog-notes, no ledger/scorecard. standard → content-plan, plan, article EN/ZH, style gate, cover, x-post; ledger optional. serious → current Workflow 3 + distribution + images. flagship → serious + Video Workflow 2.0 + music + postmortem 24h/7d. artifacts.json carries `required_for: [tiers]`; the validator takes `--tier` instead of the ad hoc --serious/--require-images/--require-distribution/--require-release flag stack. This also re-balances effort toward the media back half, which is where the quality gap to the reference YouTubers actually is.

### [medium/craft/S] SKILL.md is not progressively disclosed: all 23 gate bodies are loaded for every phase

- Evidence: SKILL.md: 352 lines; §4 Quality gates alone is lines 164-316 (153 lines, 43%). A phase like 'run blog-illustrate' needs the Image quality gate (:232) and Package integrity command, but the agent also loads audio loudness rules (:265-274), UTM rules (:205), taxonomy (:278-285), and the growth CLI (:216-230). aaron-video-gen/SKILL.md is 775 lines and blog-write 402, so a full chain load is >1,500 lines before any reference file. Three strategy files are mandated at :88-92 regardless of phase.
- Recommendation: Target SKILL.md ≤120 lines: Pipeline, tiers, the `next` command, the per-phase loop (:157-162), completion report, HITL stops. Move gate bodies to `references/gates/<phase>.md` (editorial, media, distribution, release) and have `next` print which reference to read. Move 'Aaron's default blog style' (:106-115) out — it duplicates blog-write/SKILL.md:56 and strategy/blog-writing-language.md. Keep the artifact contract only as a generated table from artifacts.json.

### [high/product/S] Human-in-the-loop stops are late; the decisive intervention on 08-19 happened where the workflow does not stop

- Evidence: Formal stops: SKILL.md:147 (audio listening), :205 and :289 (external publishing), blog-illustrate/SKILL.md:231 (style menu), :162 'Stop after the phase if human review is needed' (undefined). 08-19 editorial-scorecard.md:87: Argument Lock 'REOPENED then re-locked as v5 — Aaron's review judged the market-frame version too surface-level' — this came after v4 had a scorecard of 89 (handoff.md:25), a red-team review and a prose polish. handoff.md:20 records 11 draft rounds. Detect-the-next-step has no row that stops for Aaron before drafting; Argument Lock is recorded in prose after the fact.
- Recommendation: Make two early stops explicit and machine-recorded in package-state: (1) Argument Lock — Aaron approves editorial-brief + argument-memo (thesis, opening, contribution) before any prose is drafted; `next` refuses to route to blog-write until state.locks.argument = 'pass' with an approver and date. (2) Treatment Lock — Aaron picks the video treatment/reference before director-plan and storyboard are built. Keep audio listening and publish approval. Drop the implicit 'stop if review is needed' in favor of a fixed list. Expected effect: fewer full-draft rewrites like v4→v5, which is where the 08-19 days went.

### [medium/structure/S] Three skill registries drift: sync script, tessl.json, config.blogWorkflow

- Evidence: scripts/sync-agent-skills.sh:11-40 maps 28 skills; tessl.json lists 14 deps and omits blog-canon-alignment, blog-prose-editor, asset-library, blog-notes, pattern-atlas, ai-video-lab; config/aaron-studio.json blogWorkflow lists 8 steps and omits canon-alignment, prose-editor, asset-library while SKILL.md:12-23 pipeline has 10. .tessl/RULES.md referenced by AGENTS.md does not exist. The sync script shells out to python3 for relpath (:57-62).
- Recommendation: Make `config/aaron-studio.json.blogWorkflow` (or the new artifacts.json) the single list and have sync-agent-skills.sh and a tessl.json generator read it. Add a CI-style check script that fails when the three disagree. Remove the python3 dependency (bash `realpath --relative-to` or a bun one-liner) so the sync script runs on a clean machine.

### [medium/product/L] Productization blockers: prose-held state, host-specific branches, hard-coded paths, API key sprawl

- Evidence: Hard-coded: publish-to-blog/SKILL.md:17-20 (/Users/aaronguo/... despite config keys blogRepo/blogPostsEn at config/aaron-studio.json); blog-illustrate/SKILL.md:321 and scripts/blog-illustration-workflow.test.ts:144 assert a /Users/aaronguo/Work/lab/images-stock path in a test. Host-specific: SKILL.md:232 and blog-illustrate/SKILL.md:70,111,231,286 branch on 'In Codex ... built-in image_gen' vs 'in Claude Code AskUserQuestion'. External deps referenced across tiles: OPENAI_API_KEY (28), ELEVENLABS_API_KEY (13), KLING_SECRET (12), GOOGLE_API_KEY (8), TURSO_AUTH_TOKEN (7), YOUTUBE_CLIENT_SECRET (5). Aaron-specific assumptions baked into the orchestrator: voice profile default (:265), CTA rotation (config), taxonomy list duplicated in prose (:278-284) and code (:12-18), growth DB via scripts/blog-growth.mjs. 08-19 package: 135 files (217 with cache), 6 video mp4s and 3 full audio versions in the content dir with 0 git commits — no clean separation of source artifacts from renders.
- Recommendation: For a sellable skill stack: (1) every path and identity value comes from one config (blog repo, voice profile id, taxonomy, CTA rotation, tiers) — remove the table in publish-to-blog. (2) Replace 'In Codex / in Claude' prose with a capability check ('if a host image tool exists, else CLI') and record the backend in the manifest as already done. (3) Declare required env per phase in artifacts.json so `next` can report missing keys before a phase starts. (4) Move renders/audio/cache to a `renders/` subdir (or config renderRoot) ignored by git; keep manifests + sha256 in the content dir. (5) Delete the hard-coded path assertion from the illustration test.

### [medium/automation/S] Stale-asset detection relies on mtime; locks are not tied to content hashes

- Evidence: blog-package-quality.ts:513-525 compares statSync mtimeMs of youtube-script.md and the canonical video against the English article. mtime is reset by git checkout/clone and by any no-op save. editorial-system.md:71 demands 'identify every downstream asset that is stale' but only the video has a recorded sha256 (state schema :87). 08-19 had a post-Article-Lock v12 fact fix (scorecard line 61) that legitimately changed the article after audio v3 and video v3 were rendered; the validator surfaces this only as a warning.
- Recommendation: When a lock is recorded, store sha256 of the locked inputs (EN/ZH article at Article Lock; youtube-script + audio-transcript at audio approval; director-plan + storyboard at render). The validator marks downstream artifacts stale when the current hash differs from the hash at their lock, and `next` routes to the re-gate step. Keep mtime as fallback only.

### [low/measurement/S] The feedback/growth loop is the least-proven gate and the most instruction-heavy

- Evidence: SKILL.md:94-104, :216-230 require running scripts/blog-growth.mjs twice and naming 'one pattern to reuse/avoid, one caveat, next experiment'. 08-21 state records only 'prediction recorded ... rubric 64/100'. postmortem.md exists in all 9 packages but 08-19's is still 'pending' (package-state.postmortem.status) a week after publish. No validator reads postmortem.md.
- Recommendation: Either make the postmortem a real phase in the state machine (phase 'postmortem-pending' with a due date the `next` command surfaces) or demote the Reinforcement gate to an optional reference until the growth DB has enough lessons to change behavior. Do not keep two mandatory CLI invocations in the hot path for a signal that is self-described as sparse (:222).
