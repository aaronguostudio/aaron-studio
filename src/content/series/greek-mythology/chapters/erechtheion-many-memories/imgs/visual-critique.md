# Visual Critique — Erechtheion Memory Map

## Decision

**PASS — accepted as `artistic-interpretation`.**

## What works

- The diagonal cutaway makes the uneven site and multiple levels the first read.
- The olive, south porch, lower sacred opening, and interior chambers form five usable interaction regions.
- The image avoids tourists, text, map legends, modern blueprint styling, fantasy architecture, and a falsely symmetrical temple.
- Series materials remain coherent while the axonometric composition is distinct from the pediment and statue chapters.

## Accepted caveats

- The cutaway looks unusually resolved. Captions must explicitly deny that it is a measured or recovered floor plan.
- An upright trident appears beside the lower opening. This is a strong artistic shorthand; it is not the surviving rock outline described by Pausanias and must not be treated as an artifact depiction.
- The interior Athena-like figure, polychromy, roofs, partitions, and exact porches are supplied choices.
- Five solid Korai plus one ghosted support communicate displacement, but not the exact modern conservation history on their own.

## Rejection triggers for a future version

Reject any replacement that equates every irregularity with ritual meaning, claims exact room functions through realism, treats today's olive as the ancient individual, or assigns the Korai one certain mythic identity.
