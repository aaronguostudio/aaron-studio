## Editorial Contract

- **Reader:** A curious non-specialist or Greece traveler who recognizes the Caryatids but cannot yet explain the building around them.
- **Job:** Read the Erechtheion as a layered sacred complex while keeping architecture, cult, ancient tradition, reconstruction, and authorial interpretation distinct.
- **Promise:** The reader will understand why the building changes level and face, what its main cultic names mean, and which famous explanations remain uncertain.
- **Opening:** Walk from the recognizable south porch around a building whose levels, porches, and façades refuse one clean view.
- **Contribution:** Connect Athena Polias, Poseidon-Erechtheus, the Erechtheus/Erichthonius source problem, contest tokens, neighboring sanctuaries, and the Korai through one spatial journey.
- **Boundary:** Do not claim a complete ancient interior, one canonical genealogy, a proven identity for the Korai, biological continuity for the present olive, or an ancient program of tolerance and reconciliation.
- **Hypothesis:** The Caryatid recognition hook should earn the episode open; the reveal that the site figures are copies and that the building's names remain unsettled should carry qualified readers toward the evidence layers and series navigation.
