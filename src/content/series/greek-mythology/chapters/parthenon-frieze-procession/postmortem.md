## What Worked in Production

- The label-off opening converted an interpretive-method question into an immediate visual act.
- Six sources were sufficient to support the final public claim set; later administrative and very recent spatial research could remain in the dossier without burdening the article.
- Comparing explanatory scope prevented the sacrifice proposal from becoming sensational balance.
- The visual program and prose now share the same three stages.
- English and Chinese preserve the same confidence changes without sentence-by-sentence translation.
- The active growth lesson about concrete openings was useful; the unrelated operator framework was explicitly rejected.

## Lessons

- A consensus statement needs a mechanism in the article. “Most scholars think” is weaker than showing what the interpretation explains.
- One contested center can teach calibrated confidence more clearly than a catalogue of every uncertain identity.
- Digital reassembly is itself a provenance layer and should be dated even when it improves access.
- Rights must be decided independently from evidence quality; an authoritative image is not automatically reusable.
- A mythology chapter can retain Aaron's evidence discipline without translating the insight into AI or business.
