## Editorial Contract

- **Reader:** A curious non-specialist who has seen the Parthenon frieze or its museum display and assumes “Panathenaic procession” is the object's surviving name.
- **Job:** Learn to distinguish observable carving from a strong scholarly identification without falling into “anything goes.”
- **Promise:** The reader can explain why the Greater Panathenaia is the best overall reading and why the central cloth remains disputed.
- **Opening:** The reader turns off every label while the carved movement remains.
- **Contribution:** One interaction and one narrative sequence make interpretive weight visible: observation → leading reading → pressure test.
- **Boundary:** No claim that the relief records one real parade, shows all Athenians, has a settled cloth action, or supports one complete political ideology.
- **Hypothesis:** A visual contradiction should earn deeper reading than a survey opening. The primary channel is the mythology season page; the primary 7-day signal is completion of all three evidence states, supported by central-scene dwell and return-to-series clicks.
