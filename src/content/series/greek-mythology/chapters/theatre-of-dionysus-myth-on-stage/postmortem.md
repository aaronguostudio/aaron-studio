## What Worked in Production

- The marble-seat correction gave an architectural claim narrative force without inventing a personal visit.
- A single *Eumenides* passage carried the mechanism better than a survey of playwrights.
- The spectator/juror clarification stopped the central example from overpromising political participation.
- Agathon's invented plot provided an economical correction to “all tragedy retold myth.”
- Cutting exact capacity, full schedule, machinery, and contested reform politics kept the evidence budget aligned with the thesis.
- The active concrete-opening lesson transferred well; the unrelated AI operating frame was left out.

## Lessons

- A famous ruin should be treated as a dated stack before it becomes a scene.
- “Public” needs an operational definition whenever modern political meanings could rush in.
- One source-backed counterexample can repair a false universal without opening a second article.
- Performance reconstruction needs both a textual boundary and a visual chronology boundary.
- The missing event can provide stronger narrative tension than a claim to complete recovery.
- A season finale should complete a medium-level movement, not merely summarize earlier chapters.
