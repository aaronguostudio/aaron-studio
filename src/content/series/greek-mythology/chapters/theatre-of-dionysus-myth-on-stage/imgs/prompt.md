# Prompt — `theatre-palimpsest-v1`

## Role

Chapter hero and interaction ground for `theatre-of-dionysus-time-layers`.

## Visual predicate

Several chronological evidence layers share one theatrical place without becoming a recovered view of a single ancient performance.

## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: 4:5 vertical website chapter hero, no typography
Primary request: Create an archaeological editorial palimpsest image for a chapter about how myth became public argument in the Theatre of Dionysus. Use the reference only for the series' tactile stone, restrained ancient pigment, warm paper-marble, mineral blue, terracotta, weathered bronze, and quiet authored visual language; do not reuse its divine tableau.
Scene/backdrop: viewed obliquely from high above, the semicircular Theatre of Dionysus rests against the south slope of the Acropolis. Separate time layers visibly: surviving later stone seating and retaining walls are solid, weathered and quiet; above the orchestra, a ghosted fifth-century earthen floor and simple timber stage appear as translucent graphite-and-pigment reconstruction; a small chorus in varied masks forms an imperfect circle, while three actors occupy distinct positions around a central civic decision moment inspired by the Eumenides, with voting pebbles/urn shapes and Athena indicated only as an artistic performance silhouette, not a literal goddess. A festival inscription fragment, theatre mask statuette, and ivy/Dionysian trace sit at the frame edges as separate evidence objects. The audience is suggested by many small attentive silhouettes, not a modern mass crowd.
Style/medium: exquisite archaeological site model fused with archival stage sketch and layered paper theatre design; factual masonry solid, early stage ghosted, performance figures painterly silhouettes; contemporary editorial, no fantasy spectacle.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5; theatre bowl fills the frame, focus moves from solid stone seats at bottom through audience to ghosted orchestra/stage at center and sanctuary trace at top; calm zones for five mobile hotspots.

Color palette: warm limestone and paper, graphite, muted Aegean blue, mineral red, ivy green, weathered bronze, tiny restrained gold accents.
Materials/textures: worn stone seats, packed earth, timber grain, pigment dust, parchment, chipped terracotta mask.
Constraints: chronology must be visibly layered; performance staging marked as artistic, not exact; audience watching a jury scene must not look like the audience itself is voting; no claim that every tragedy retold myth; no text, letters, labels, UI, border, watermark, logo.

```

## Evidence caution

The orchestra, timber stage, masked chorus, actors, jury props, audience, sanctuary, and foreground objects come from different dates and evidence types. The image is designed as a palimpsest, not a synchronized historical instant.
