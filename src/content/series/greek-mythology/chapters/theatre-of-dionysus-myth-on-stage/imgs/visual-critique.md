# Visual Critique — Theatre Palimpsest

## Decision

**PASS — accepted as `artistic-interpretation`.**

## What works

- The theatre bowl establishes place, while graphite architecture and colored performance figures visibly add earlier and imaginative layers.
- The high view makes the audience distinct from the central decision scene.
- Foreground mask, ivy and statuette signal the distributed archive without generated labels.
- The image avoids the Colosseum, pristine Roman spectacle, modern proscenium, medieval scenery, fantasy magic, and tourism photography.

## Accepted caveats

- The chronological layers are aesthetically blended enough that a casual reader could read them as one moment. Caption and interaction statuses must carry the separation.
- The central arrangement resembles an actual jury vote more concretely than the evidence can reconstruct; exact blocking and props remain supplied.
- The full audience, chorus size, costumes, masks, timber stage and sanctuary architecture are artistic.
- The foreground objects are enlarged and relocated for explanation; they are not a documented assemblage from the premiere.

## Rejection triggers for a future version

Reject any replacement that labels the later stone auditorium as the 458 BCE premiere venue, makes the audience itself vote, claims universal attendance or a single democratic lesson, or presents exact masks, sound, blocking, and response as recovered facts.
