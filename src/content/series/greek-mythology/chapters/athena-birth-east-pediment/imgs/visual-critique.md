# Visual Critique — East Pediment Lost Center

## Decision

**PASS — accepted as `artistic-interpretation`.**

## What works

- The missing center is the strongest shape, so the image communicates loss before detail.
- Helios and Selene create a readable two-edge rhythm and provide useful mobile hotspot zones.
- Warm marble, faded blue/red pigment, graphite traces, and oblique light match the series world.
- No text, UI, watermark, medieval architecture, Roman costume shorthand, or fantasy effects appear.
- The complete center is not invented, and the image remains legible at mobile scale.

## Accepted caveats

- The surviving hand and body have become complete celestial figures. The interface must continue to say that the artifacts survive only fragmentarily and that their positions/actions are reconstructed.
- Several witness figures look unusually complete and individually plausible. They must stay unnamed; the image cannot support a definitive cast list.
- The geometry and pigmentation are editorial synthesis, not measured restoration or exact color evidence.

## Rejection triggers for a future version

Reject any replacement that fills the central void, fixes Hera rather than Eileithyia, presents the axe as sculpturally proven, adds fake labels, or makes the image look like an intact archaeological object.
