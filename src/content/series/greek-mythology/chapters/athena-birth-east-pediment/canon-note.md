# Canon Note — Born at Dawn

## Canonical Idea

The east pediment preserves Athena's birth most securely through a dawn frame and surrounding witnesses, not through the lost central choreography. The chapter's canonical judgment is: **an event can lose its center while every surviving edge continues to point toward it**.

This is an editorial reading grounded in the monument's condition, the Acropolis Museum's Helios/Selene interpretation, and the cosmic reaction in Hymn 28. It is not a recovered inscription or a claim that the pediment directly illustrated the hymn.

## Reusable Frame

For a lost central scene:

1. read the surviving edges for time and direction;
2. identify what an ancient observer securely names;
3. let each textual tradition add only its own details;
4. mark the exact point at which a scholar or artist must choose.

This is a viewing sequence for the mythology series, not a named framework or a transferable business model.
