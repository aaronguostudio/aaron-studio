# Content Plan: Athena Parthenos

## Voice check

**Positioning:** A curious visitor entering an empty ancient room and learning how description, accounting, and copying can give a lost figure partial form.

**Voice rule:** Let evidence carry the wonder. Use “I” only to frame the desire to see the absent statue and the final recognition that no complete image is neutral.

**Personal anchor:** Aaron's family trip to the Acropolis and the realization that the Parthenon's most dominant interior presence is also one of its largest absences.

## Hook

The Parthenon still stands, but the figure that once dominated its interior is gone. A second-century traveler can begin to return her: gold and ivory, helmet, Medusa, a towering Nike, spear, shield, serpent, and a base filled with another birth.

## Core thesis

Athena Parthenos survives as a distributed archive. Ancient words, public accounts, reduced Roman copies, and reproduced shield details recover a strong outline while preventing any honest modern reconstruction from claiming the lost original's complete presence.

## Story movement

### 1. The empty inner room

- Begin with absence inside the surviving temple.
- Establish that the original does not survive and avoid a speculative destruction story.
- Let the reader ask: how do we know what filled this space?

### 2. Pausanias builds a goddess from nouns

- Restore the high-confidence visual inventory in a deliberate sequence.
- Use the four-cubit Nike to make scale tangible without modern conversion.
- Mark the serpent/Erichthonius identification as Pausanias's.

### 3. Gold that could come off

- Move from appearance to construction accounts.
- Show EM 637 as public oversight and gold expenditure.
- Use Thucydides for forty removable talents and the obligation to replace them.
- Preserve the double identity: sacred image and recoverable public resource.

### 4. The goddess becomes small enough to hold in view

- Introduce the Varvakeion Athena and the fragmentary Acropolis copy.
- Contrast the lost twelve-metre figure with later reductions.
- Explain what repetition across copies can strengthen and what changed material/scale can distort.

### 5. A shield survives without surviving

- Use the Strangford Shield to isolate one component and show traces of paint.
- Briefly show how several shield reproductions allow motifs to be cross-checked.
- Do not resolve disputed portraits or every Amazonomachy figure.

### 6. Every complete Athena is a new Athena

- Explain why modern full-scale and digital versions are valuable as experiences but not exact replicas.
- Return to the empty room: evidence can give back stance, attributes, scale, and material tension, not Pheidias's face, surface, light, or total artistic effect.
- Close on the distributed archive as a form of survival with honest limits.

## Visual and interaction role

**Proposed interaction ID:** `athena-parthenos-evidence-assembly`

- Base state: empty naos plus scale marker, not a completed goddess.
- “Ancient description” layer adds named attributes.
- “Accounts” layer reveals gold as counted and removable without visualizing modern money.
- “Copies” layer compares silhouettes from Varvakeion, Ακρ. 1362, and shield evidence.
- “Artistic interpretation” layer adds face, colour, light, fine texture, and missing construction choices with a persistent label.
- Confidence should plateau below total certainty; avoid a progress meter that ends at 100%.
