## What Worked in Production

- The empty-room opening made an invisible interior object concrete without inventing entry into the naos.
- Each evidence class performed a different narrative action instead of forming a museum-source catalogue.
- The account inscription and Thucydides created the chapter's material turn while the prose preserved their limited scope.
- Whole-figure reductions and the isolated shield made the distributed-archive mechanism visible at two scales.
- Red-team revision stopped the gold fact from controlling the statue's entire interpretation.
- The bilingual versions preserve the same facts and source set while using natural language-specific rhythms.

## Lessons

- “Lost” is a condition, not a destruction narrative. Final-fate claims need a dedicated source pass.
- Later copies become more useful when their changes in scale, material, and date remain visible.
- A high-confidence outline should precede the uncertainty list; otherwise evidence discipline feels like refusal to know.
- Financial evidence can explain the status of a material without becoming the master metaphor for an artwork.
- Partial objects correct one another best when the interface lets their layers remain reversible.
- Proposed source IDs must be adopted into the shared registry before article integration.
