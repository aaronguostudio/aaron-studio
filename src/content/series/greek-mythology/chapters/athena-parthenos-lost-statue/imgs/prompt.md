# Prompt — `distributed-archive-v1`

## Role

Chapter hero and interaction ground for `athena-parthenos-evidence-assembly`.

## Visual predicate

Several unlike witnesses align around one lost colossal body, but their alignment never becomes the original.

## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: 4:5 vertical website chapter hero, no typography
Primary request: Create an archaeological editorial reconstruction image about Athena Parthenos, the colossal lost statue once inside the Parthenon, whose outline survives only through distributed evidence. Use the reference only for the series' tactile stone, restrained polychromy, warm paper-marble, mineral blue, terracotta, weathered bronze, restrained gold, and quiet authored visual language; do not reuse its tableau.
Scene/backdrop: inside a shadowed abstracted Parthenon cella, a monumental Athena-shaped absence rises at center. Build her from separated evidence layers rather than one seamless statue: a solid helmet fragment; a ghosted ivory-toned face and arms; removable overlapping gold garment plates; a small winged Nike indicated as a faint supported possibility; a large shield assembled from a surviving carved shield fragment and two differently scaled Roman-copy silhouettes; at the base, an inscribed accounting stone slab and small marble reductions on study plinths. Some layers align, others remain visibly incomplete and suspended by fine archival construction lines. The original statue itself must read as lost.
Style/medium: exquisite mixed-media archaeological maquette, carved marble and ivory-like material, hammered gold leaf plates, graphite reconstruction drawing, archival paper; refined museum-editorial art, not a fantasy idol.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5, towering central silhouette occupying upper two-thirds, evidence objects clearly separated around lower and side zones for mobile hotspots, strong scale contrast, generous breathing room.
Lighting/mood: a narrow warm shaft of temple light catching gold fragments, otherwise contemplative deep graphite shadow; awe created through scale and absence.
Color palette: warm Pentelic stone and paper, ivory, graphite, muted Aegean blue, mineral red, weathered bronze, restrained ancient gold; no glossy luxury shine.
Materials/textures: chipped marble, hammered removable gold sheets, aged ivory-like surface, incised stone accounts, pigment dust, paper grain.
Constraints: make uncertainty visible; no modern complete reconstruction presented as exact; no exact facial claim; no legible writing; no text, labels, UI, border, watermark, logo.
Avoid: seamless fully preserved Athena statue, Nashville replica look, superhero armor, fantasy magic, glowing eyes, Roman toga cliché, medieval interior, modern museum room, treasure-poster glamour, photoreal living person.
```

## Evidence caution

Although the prompt requests a monumental silhouette, every continuous join, facial feature, gold placement, copy alignment, shield composition, and cella atmosphere is artistic completion.
