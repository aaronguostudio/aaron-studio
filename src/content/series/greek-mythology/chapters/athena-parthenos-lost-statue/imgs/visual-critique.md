# Visual Critique — Distributed Archive

## Decision

**PASS — accepted as `artistic-interpretation`.**

## What works

- The towering silhouette restores awe while visible gaps, plates, wires, copies, shield layers, and the account keep the evidence distributed.
- Gold is materially important without turning the image into treasure-poster glamour.
- The lower evidence objects and upper body provide a strong scale contrast and five clear hotspot regions.
- The work contains no UI, labels, watermark, fantasy glow, modern room, or superhero armor.

## Accepted caveats

- The output is more visually complete than the prompt's phrase “Athena-shaped absence.” The interface disclaimer is therefore essential: this is an assembly metaphor, not a recovered form.
- Athena's rendered face and body can easily be mistaken for a confident reconstruction; exact face and proportions remain unsupported.
- The accounting slab contains fake ancient-looking marks. They must never be enlarged, transcribed, or described as the actual inscription.
- A complete spear, Nike and coherent gold costume are visual synthesis; later copies do not guarantee these exact joins or surfaces.

## Rejection triggers for a future version

Reject any replacement that advertises itself as “the original,” treats one Roman reduction as exact, invents a certain disappearance, converts forty talents into a modern value without research, or makes pseudo-writing legible as evidence.
