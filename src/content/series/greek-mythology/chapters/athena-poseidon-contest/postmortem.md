## What Worked in Production

- The artifact-first opening converted a fragmentary monument into a natural narrative hook.
- The evidence hierarchy made it possible to preserve wonder without presenting generated imagery as archaeological fact.
- Separating the Pseudo-Apollodorus sequence as one version prevented a smooth story from becoming false certainty.
- The Erechtheion supplied a spatial mechanism for the central thesis instead of leaving it as a free-floating metaphor.
- Chinese and English were developed as parallel reading experiences rather than a rigid sentence-by-sentence translation.
- The interactive comparison and long-form story share one visual and editorial language.

## Lessons

- A mythology chapter needs its claim ledger before prose; otherwise familiar retellings easily smuggle in unsupported voting, judging, gift, or naming details.
- Evidence labels should attach as closely as possible to the claim they qualify. A whole section can contain artifact evidence, textual tradition, reconstruction, and inference at once.
- The source registry must remain the stable identity layer; presentation-friendly source labels should not become a second ID system.
- Personal travel experience works best as an entrance and return point, not as proof.
- A strong interpretive sentence can carry the chapter, but only when its status as interpretation remains explicit.
- Local build and browser QA validate the package and experience, not publication or production provenance.
