# The desert has a voice

## [HOOK]

That low rumble is a sand dune. You're hearing a real recording from Great Sand Dunes National Park. Watch the surface: the sand is sliding.

## [SLIDE: A landscape becomes an instrument — eureka-nps.jpg]

On some dunes, an avalanche produces a deep, sustained note. It can resemble a distant airplane, or the lowest pipes of an organ. A whole hillside, making sound.

## [SLIDE: The conditions matter — grains-illustration.png]

But this isn't something every pile of sand can do. At Eureka Dunes, the sand needs to be dry. Add moisture, and the booming stops. The conditions matter as much as the movement.

## [SLIDE: Listen again — eureka-nps.jpg]

Researchers have proposed different explanations for how the sound is sustained. We can watch the avalanche. Explaining its voice is harder. Listen again. A landscape we think of as silent, making itself heard.
