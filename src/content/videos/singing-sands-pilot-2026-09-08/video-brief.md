# Singing sands — internal pilot
## Target Audience
Curious adults beyond technology; no geology knowledge assumed.
## Desired Emotion
Surprise, then attentive wonder. Conversational, never trailer hype.
## Core Promise
Hear a real dune, see an avalanche, and understand that booming depends on conditions.
## Title/Thumbnail Expectation
The desert has a voice / 沙丘真的会唱歌. Actual sand at frame zero; a small title rather than a title slide.
## High-Shock Facts
- Some avalanching dunes produce a sustained low note (NPS; Caltech).
- Moisture suppresses booming at Eureka Dunes (NPS).
## Hook Type
Sensory reveal: real sound precedes the first spoken identification.
## Story Structure
Listen -> reveal location -> observe motion -> compare conditions -> acknowledge mechanism limits -> listen again.
## Retention Beat Map
- 0s: real sand recording, no music.
- around 25s: wide dune gives way to a close explanation.
- around 50s: dry versus damp is revealed.
- ending: narration yields to the real recording.
## What The Video Adds
- An audible primary source.
- Moving footage of the sand surface.
- A labeled illustrative dry/damp comparison.
## Banned Phrases
- In today's video
- Let's dive in
- Scientists are baffled
- Mystery solved
## Ending
Return to the actual recording and leave room to listen. Fade to silence inside file.
## Audit Status
Source bounds reviewed; author listening and visual review pending. This is a 60–90s prototype, not an approved release.
