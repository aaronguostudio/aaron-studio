## Title
This Sand Sings — The Science Behind a Desert’s Voice

## Channel
Visual & Sound

## Description
A desert can make a sound that resembles a distant aircraft or a low organ note. Hear a real recording, follow the laboratory experiments, and explore what moving grains can teach us about sound and engineering.

The final chapter visits granular robot grippers: a related branch of granular physics, rather than a direct invention from singing-sand research.

English narration: approved American voice F (Brian). Visual explanations include original animation and Blender reconstructions.
