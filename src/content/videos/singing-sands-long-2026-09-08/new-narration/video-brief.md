# Expanded documentary
## Target Audience
Curious adults, no physics background required.
## Desired Emotion
Wonder followed by understanding and useful curiosity.
## Core Promise
Hear a real dune, follow experimental clues, and understand the connection to broader granular engineering without claiming direct technological spin-offs.
## Title/Thumbnail Expectation
The Desert Has a Voice. A real sand surface and a clearly identified field recording open the film.
## High-Shock Facts
- Singing-dune sand produced sound in a hard-bottom laboratory channel.
- Granular discharge can cause silo vibration and sound; jamming can enable a robotic gripper.
## Hook Type
Sensory reveal preserved from the author-approved pilot. This script contains only additional narration inserted into that full film.
## Story Structure
Listen; conditions; frequency; grain motion and pressure; synchronization; controlled experiments; size selection; competing mechanisms; silo engineering; jamming gripper; return to listening.
## Retention Beat Map
- Real sound opens the film.
- A crowd analogy makes synchronization visible.
- Laboratory control tests whether a whole dune is necessary.
- Silo and gripper examples widen the subject beyond a desert curiosity.
## What The Video Adds
- A source-backed long narrative and concrete explanatory animations.
- Distinct chapter music bridges and English-only captions and graphics.
- Independently reframed vertical excerpts.
## Banned Phrases
- Mystery solved
- Scientists are baffled
- This invention came from singing sand
- Guaranteed viral
- In today's video
- Let's dive in
## Ending
Return to the actual NPS recording with music silent, then a quiet completed author end card.
