# Pilot 01 — AI Passed QA. Human Hated It.

Series: `HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`

Status: treatment for selection; no media generation approved yet.

## One-Sentence Idea

An AI-made Codex video passed every machine quality check, then failed the only
test that mattered: a human watched it.

## Why It Works

This is not a hypothetical lesson or a takedown of somebody else's work. It is
a real, self-deprecating postmortem with visible receipts. The joke and the
operating principle are the same thing: technical validity is not taste, and a
disclosure label does not automatically create trust.

## Draft English Voiceover

> My AI passed every quality check. 1080p. Clean encode. Perfect loudness. Then
> Aaron watched it: “The music sucks. And it obviously looks fake.” New rule:
> if only the machine likes it, it doesn't ship.

The user feedback should be typeset as a translated quotation, not presented as
a screenshot of a private conversation.

## Visual Treatment

- Real encoded video frames and the actual QA report are the evidence.
- Editorial typography and grease-pencil annotations provide the entertainment.
- No simulated Codex window, fake cursor, or reconstructed chat UI.
- The original video's `RECREATED DEMO · SOURCE-BACKED` disclosure remains
  visible whenever its footage appears.
- AI-generated imagery is unnecessary for this pilot.
- Render 9:16 first, then recompose a 16:9 X cut rather than center-cropping.
