# Human Review Required #001 — Video Brief

## Target Audience

AI-native builders and product leaders who use automated checks but know that a technically valid output can still feel untrustworthy, tasteless, or simply bad.

## Desired Emotion

Recognition first, then a laugh, then respect for the honesty of showing a real miss.

## Core Promise

In 23 seconds, show why machine QA cannot replace human taste and trust by autopsying a real AI-made video that passed every technical check and still failed human review.

## Title/Thumbnail Expectation

`AI PASSED QA.` is visible at frame zero over the actual finished video. The reversal `HUMAN HATED IT.` arrives before the midpoint.

## High-Shock Facts

- The source video passed its technical production checklist.
- It is 1920×1080 at 60 fps and decodes without errors.
- Its encoded mix measured approximately -16.2 LUFS.
- The first human reaction was that the music was bad and the demo visibly looked fake.
- Passing measurable checks did not make the output tasteful or trustworthy.

## Hook Type

Contradiction. Target: AI builders who trust checklists. Transformation: from `all checks passed` to `human release gate required`. Stakes: shipping technically correct work that damages trust.

## Story Structure

Real receipt → technical confidence → blunt human reversal → forensic proof → operating rule.

## Retention Beat Map

- 0.0s: actual video plus `PASSED QA`.
- 1.4s: technical receipts accumulate.
- 7.8s: music and confidence collapse into the human-review pivot.
- 10.7s: the two approved translated feedback lines take over the frame.
- 14.8s: `QA: PASSED · SHIP: BLOCKED` becomes `HUMANLY WRONG`.
- 16.5s: the missing release criteria appear.
- 20.6s: one repeatable operating rule closes the film.

## What The Video Adds

It does not merely state that human judgment matters. It shows the real artifact, the real measurements, and the real feedback from the failed production.

## Banned Phrases

- game changer
- crazy, right
- AI is replacing humans
- perfect QA
- human in the loop as empty jargon

## Ending

`If only the machine likes it, it doesn't ship.` followed by the small `HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED` series identity.
