## Recommended Directions

### Direction A — Forensic Deadpan

- Visual spine: real footage, real QA receipts, grease-pencil evidence marks, white type, green pass signal, coral human-review tension.
- Scene families: source evidence, editorial statements, restrained brand end card.
- Signature opportunity: the green `QA: PASSED · SHIP: BLOCKED` state is interrupted by a coral `HUMANLY WRONG` verdict.
- Tradeoff: relies on precise typography and sound timing rather than visual spectacle.

### Direction B — Mock Reality Show

- Visual spine: scorecard, rejection stamp, exaggerated reaction timing.
- Scene families: progressive cards and result bands.
- Signature opportunity: the video receives a theatrical rejection.
- Tradeoff: funnier on first viewing but risks cheapening the real lesson.
