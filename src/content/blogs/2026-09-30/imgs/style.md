# Referendum Authority Cover Style

- **Thesis:** a mandate is real, but it does not create a safe option by itself.
- **Palette:** cream paper, deep teal cliffs, sage terrain, ochre road, small coral ballot mark.
- **Motifs:** a plain ballot sheet, one amber route, open horizon beyond constraint.
- **Rules:** no ballot text, party logos, flags, crowds, Greek columns, maps, charts, or heroic leaders.
