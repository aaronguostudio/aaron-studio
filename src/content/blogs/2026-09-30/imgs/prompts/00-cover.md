# Prompt: 00-cover

## Role

Clean blog cover for “61% Said No. Why Did Greece Sign Anyway?”

## Visual Mode

Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight

Loud

## Style Family

Field Signal Editorial / warm muted risograph collage

## Positive Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape blog cover, no text. Primary request: a humane editorial metaphor for a democratic mandate meeting a narrow set of viable options. Scene: a single blank cream ballot sheet in the foreground unfolds into one ochre road; the road moves through a narrowing pass between two quiet deep-teal paper-cut cliffs toward a pale Mediterranean horizon. A tiny abstract coral check mark can appear as a shape only, with no letters or numbers. Style: warm muted retro editorial collage, flat paper-cut forms, subtle risograph grain, sophisticated magazine illustration. Composition: wide 16:9, generous negative space, calm not dramatic. Palette: cream, sage, ochre, dusty coral, deep teal, charcoal. Text: none.

## Negative Prompt

No readable ballot text, numerals, political logos, flags, crowds, politicians, Greek columns, maps, charts, robot, UI, neon, glossy 3D, or stock photo.

## Mode Constraints

One paper-to-road relation, one narrowing pass, no other metaphor.

## Density Budget

Minimal.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Do not make the vote look meaningless or depict a trapped person.
