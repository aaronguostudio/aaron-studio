# Visual Strategy

## Article Character

Political-economic story about mandate versus available options. Reader emotion: recognition that a clear vote can meet an impossible choice. Risk: depicting voters as confused or making it an election poster.

## Concept Routes

1. **A ballot paper meets a narrowing mountain pass** — predicate: an open ballot sheet becomes a road that narrows between two cliffs. Selected.
2. **A loud megaphone against a closed door** — predicate: mandate meets constraint. Rejected: too accusatory.
3. **A voting box on a chessboard** — predicate: choice meets negotiation. Rejected: turns citizens into game pieces.

## Selection

**Agent-selected: Route 1.** It keeps the vote dignified while showing that authority and optionality are different.

## Revision 2 — Broad Public Mandate, Narrow Decision Exit

The v1 ballot-road was too gentle and too close to Part I. The selected v2 cover uses a broad civic table of voting disks that narrows into one torn-paper exit, with a locked financial gate on one side and a negotiation room on the other. It preserves the dignity of the vote while making the constraint concrete.
