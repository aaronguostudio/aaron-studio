Title: Why I Share DHH's Enthusiasm for AI

Description:
In my own team, some people use AI enthusiastically while others are not yet comfortable trusting it. DHH's interview with Lex Fridman made me think about how those different experiences can meet in real work.

Three ideas stood out: updating a judgment when tools change, making better product decisions, and keeping the joy of creating. I connect them to my own blog workflow, then work through a proposed shared writing exercise: agree on what good looks like, show the method, inspect a claim, and decide what to keep. This is a practice I want to try, not a claim that our team has already resolved its differences.
