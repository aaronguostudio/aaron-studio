# LinkedIn launch draft

AI can make a small product easier to start. It can also create a much larger maintenance queue.

That was the tension I kept coming back to after listening to DHH's conversation with Lex Fridman. I share his excitement about building. The pleasure, for me, is seeing an idea become something that makes a person's day work a little better.

My own writing workflow now has reusable steps for research, outlining, drafting, and review. That gives a loose thought a clearer path to something I can examine. It still doesn't decide whether the result deserves publication.

Software delivery has the same distinction. More generated work doesn't automatically give a team more capacity to understand, verify, or maintain it.

So I want to spend some of this new capacity on small, complete improvements. Before starting, I want three answers: who uses it, which part of their experience improves, and how they return to the old way if it fails.

How does your team decide which inexpensive AI experiments are worth maintaining?
