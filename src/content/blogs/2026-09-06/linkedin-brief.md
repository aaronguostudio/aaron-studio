# LinkedIn — draft

In my own team, some people don't yet trust AI enough to use it more widely. Others are already enthusiastic users. I am closer to the second group.

DHH's conversation with Lex Fridman resonated with me because he describes a change in what the tools could actually do. His experience is a useful reference for revisiting our judgments. His confidence cannot substitute for evidence in our projects.

I would like to bring those different views to one familiar task. Agree on what a useful result looks like, let AI participate, and examine the result together. People getting good results can make their methods visible. Colleagues with reservations can help identify what still needs checking.

This is a practice I want to develop, not a claim that we have already resolved the differences. I want more room to make useful products, and I want the rest of the team to be able to understand and benefit from the methods that work.

What kind of task helped your team build more specific trust in AI?
