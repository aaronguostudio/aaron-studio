# Visual strategy

Article: personal product-building reflection. Emotion: curious, capable, warm. The visual risk is turning an essay about joy into another corporate AI diagram.

## Three concepts compared before prompts

A — A pencil sketch becomes a working lamp: flat close composition, paper-to-object transformation; tangible usefulness is visible. SELECTED.
B — A person opens a door from crowded workspace to small workshop: human action, deep composition; rejected because door metaphor makes scope look like escape rather than useful creation.
C — One finished object among shelves of unfinished prototypes: shelf/elevation composition; rejected for cover because it emphasizes unfinished obligations over enthusiasm. Reuse its restraint for a maintenance body metaphor only.

Agent-selected under the user's request to continue production, applying the existing default visual baseline rather than introducing a new brand style. Sources serve ideas, never fake evidence of Aaron's actual workspace or DHH's product.

## Image jobs

Cover: give abstract creative freedom one tangible object. Body 1: show the small first-version threshold. Body 2: turn taste into choosing and removing. Body 3: show why beginning and sustaining differ. No dense mechanism image needed.

Reading loss without images: less tactile recognition of usefulness, selection, and maintenance. No image attempts to substantiate a measured result.
