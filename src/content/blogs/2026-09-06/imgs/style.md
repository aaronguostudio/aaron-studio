# Visual style: Small Things Worth Making

Baseline: Soft Glass Narrative from 2026-06-20; preserve human scale, negative space, restrained palette. Controlled Mix as defined in style-directions.md.
Thesis: an idea deserves a small real version when it improves someone's day.
Emotion: warm curiosity, useful agency, practical optimism.
Anchor: ordinary lamp, paper sketches, small workbench; illustration only, no likeness of Aaron or DHH.
Palette: warm ivory, graphite, amber human intent; cyan a subtle enabling trace, coral maintenance load.
Glass: one translucent paper edge on cover only; no glow or HUD.
Set: clean cover, exact English headline thumbnail, three body assets.
Anti-patterns: robots, brains, fake code, vendor logos, faux screenshots, charts without data, cinematic blue, extra hands, stock office workers.
Quality: one visible relation, mobile legibility, generous breathing room, no invented evidence.
