# Content Plan

## Reader and Job

Leader or builder whose agent can act; turn an abstract governance concern into an auditable design test.

## Story Arc

1. Socrates’ jury and the competence problem.
2. Plato’s challenge, stated fairly.
3. Modern democracy separates standing from expertise.
4. India’s deliberate universal-suffrage choice.
5. Agents as high-speed experts, not sovereigns.
6. Six governance properties with proportional controls.

## Evidence Boundary

Ancient Athens is not modern democracy; the six properties are an operational framework, not a political cure-all.

## Article Hypothesis

Target: AI/product leaders concerned with agent action. Channel: LinkedIn and newsletter. Primary signal: saves, substantive comments, and newsletter clicks. Lesson: a concrete historical tension should precede the frame.
