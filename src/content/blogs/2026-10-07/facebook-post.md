This series started with a family trip to Greece and a small financial-history question. It ended somewhere I did not expect: with Socrates, Plato, democracy, and the AI agents we are beginning to let act for us.

The thought I keep returning to is simple: being smarter does not give anyone—or any system—the right to decide without limits.

That is as true for experts as it is for software. The real work is designing who gives authority, who can question it, what can be undone, and who owns the consequences.
