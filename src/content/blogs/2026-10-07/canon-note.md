# Canon Note

## Canonical Idea

We are not simply building intelligent agents. We are implementing decision-making institutions in software, so intelligence must be constrained by a legible mandate and accountable process.

## Reusable Frame

Govern consequential agents through six properties: delegated, bounded, visible, challengeable, reversible, and accountable.
