# Agent Governance Cover Style

- **Thesis:** intelligence is allowed to act inside an institution; it is not self-authorising.
- **Palette:** cream, deep teal night, sage green gate, amber lantern, restrained coral boundary.
- **Motifs:** one lantern, one open green gate, a human-scale path, a few distant stars.
- **Rules:** no robots, brains, crowns, Greek statues, UI panels, labels, circuit boards, or generic glowing orb.

## Revision 3 — Contemporary Myth Engraving

Baseline: deliberately depart from the v2 editorial-workbench collage. Adapt the supplied shop-card references at the level of visual grammar only: a distinctive illustrated character, fine copperplate crosshatching, rich solid colour field, ghosted mythic pictograms, modern accessories, and one patterned clothing element.

- **Thesis:** intelligence receives authority only through legible constraints and a human-owned final seal.
- **Primary family:** Character Atlas Engraving; secondary test family: Mythic Operator Diagram.
- **Palette:** deep petrol teal or charcoal-blue ground; warm coral/terracotta skin and linework; bone paper highlights; one acidic green gate accent; one amber decision seal.
- **Motifs:** a green gate, decision disk/seal, one simple tool arm or key, sparse Greek icon field (owl, lyre, amphora, helmet) at very low contrast.
- **Pattern rule:** one garment or border uses a bold horizontal/Greek-key stripe; it supports character, never becomes wallpaper.
- **Do not copy:** any supplied subject pose, clothing, tattoo, icon placement, typography, or identifiable card layout.
- **Anti-patterns:** photoreal face, generic marble bust, movie-poster god, fantasy armour, robot face, tiny invented text, a wall of symbols, or full-screen ornamental Greek meander.

## Revision 3.1 — Bright Semi-Flat Civic Greek Editorial

- **Thesis:** a decision becomes legitimate and accountable through visible civic constraints, not through the charisma of a person or the intelligence of a machine.
- **Composition:** no central person. Prefer a place, a path, or a set of objects with one readable relationship.
- **Palette:** chalk cream, pale Aegean blue, sunlit turquoise, terracotta/coral, saffron, and sage; use deep ink only as a fine outline, never as the dominant field.
- **Rendering:** semi-flat screen-print or paper-cut editorial illustration. Use short crosshatch marks sparingly inside stone, cloth, or ceramic surfaces; no rendered skin, photographic lighting, or deep 3D space.
- **Greek vocabulary:** columns, an abstract Acropolis silhouette, amphora, owl, olive, lyre, thread, simple meander/stripe, and a civic gate. These carry mood and symbolic meaning only.
- **Pattern rule:** a small border, awning, or object may carry vivid stripes; the pattern must not become an infographic grid.
- **Anti-patterns:** human hero portrait, dark teal/charcoal ground, literal game map, circular node diagram, arrows, labels, fake Greek text, robot imagery, or historical overclaim.

## Revision 4 — Selected Reading Set

Baseline: adapt the F2 clean civic-print direction. Keep its restraint, object hierarchy, bright paper space, fine contour, and selective hatch texture; deliberately depart from the earlier dark, character-card rendering.

- **Cohesion:** Unified.
- **Thesis:** decision systems earn authority through an architecture of standing, boundary, evidence, challenge, repair, and accountable ownership.
- **Palette:** chalk cream and pale Aegean blue foundation; sage/teal for boundaries and capability; terracotta/coral for decision/action; muted olive and saffron as material accents.
- **Set rule:** one object relationship per frame; different compositions across the three bodies; no figures required.
- **Background:** empty or only a quiet Acropolis-like distant silhouette when it creates depth. No icon wallpaper.
- **Anti-patterns:** central portrait, generic robot, dark or dramatic backdrop, game-board paths, six-node radial diagrams, arrows, labels, fake Greek lettering, overly realistic architecture, or decorative motifs that do not carry the argument.
