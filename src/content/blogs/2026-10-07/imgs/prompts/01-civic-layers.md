# Prompt: 01-civic-layers

## Role

Explain the democracy section's four interacting layers without presenting a literal civic textbook or a history reconstruction.

## Visual Mode

Cutaway System Map

## Visual Weight

Medium

## Style Family

Bright Semi-Flat Civic Print

## Positive Prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 body illustration for an essay on AI agent governance
Primary request: a bright, semi-flat architectural cutaway showing four different civic layers that together support one public decision place
Scene/backdrop: chalk-cream sky with a thin pale-Aegean distant horizon; no wallpaper motifs
Subject: an open classical civic building seen straight on, made of four broad, visually distinct horizontal layers: an open mosaic public plaza at the base; a lighter assembly balcony above; a small calm archive/workroom with simple shelves above that; and a protective rule-like roof and lintel at the top. At the center of the composition, a small blank pale decision table is visibly held by all four layers.
Style/medium: original bright semi-flat paper-cut and screen-print editorial illustration, inspired only by the general bright printmaking grammar of the supplied Greek shop-card photos
Composition/framing: wide, symmetrical but airy; large readable shapes; generous negative space
Lighting/mood: sunlit, calm, thoughtful
Color palette: chalk cream, pale Aegean blue, sage, turquoise, terracotta, muted coral, small saffron details; fine deep-ink contours only
Materials/textures: short restrained hatch marks inside stone and mosaic; shallow two-layer depth only
Text (verbatim): none
Constraints: visual predicate is four civic layers holding one decision place; no people, no labels, no arrows

## Negative Prompt

No text, letters, numbers, logos, charts, UI, game board, node diagram, portraits, statues, trial reenactment, dark field, photorealism, dramatic 3D architecture, or decorative background icons.

## Mode Constraints

The image must make institutional layers intuitive in five seconds, without claiming a specific historical Athenian building or using Greek ornament as empty decoration.

## Density Budget

Four architectural bands, one decision table, one distant horizon only.
