# Prompt: 00-cover-v3-probe-a

## Role

Part IV cover-style probe: character-led governance poster.

## Visual Mode

Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight

Loud

## Style Family

Character Atlas Engraving

## Positive Prompt

An original contemporary Greek-philosopher gatekeeper in hand-engraved poster form: a thoughtful adult character with a modern striped vest and a small amber decision seal, standing before one green review gate. Use crosshatched copperplate linework, petrol-teal solid ground, faint Greek myth pictograms, and one physical boundary. The image must make the authority-through-constraint relation clear without words.

## Negative Prompt

No text, labels, direct character copies, marble bust, robot, crown, dashboard, frame/rack, fantasy armour, generic AI motifs, watermark.

## Mode Constraints

One figure and one gate; background pictograms remain low contrast.

## Density Budget

Narrative/metaphor.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Do not reduce governance to a lonely gate in an empty landscape.
