# Prompt: 00-cover

## Role

Clean blog cover for “Being Smarter Doesn't Give You the Right to Decide.”

## Visual Mode

Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight

Loud

## Style Family

Field Signal Editorial / warm muted risograph collage

## Positive Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape blog cover, no text. Primary request: an editorial metaphor for intelligence operating inside a legitimate institutional boundary. Scene: one small amber lantern on a quiet cream paper path; its warm light passes through a single open sage-green gate and continues toward a deep-teal evening horizon with a few tiny stars. Style: warm muted retro editorial collage, flat paper-cut shapes, restrained risograph grain, sophisticated reflective magazine illustration. Composition: wide 16:9, gate slightly right of centre, generous calm negative space. Palette: cream, sage green, amber ochre, dusty coral accent, deep teal, charcoal. Text: none. Constraints: human-scale, clear gate/lantern relation, no literal AI figure.

## Negative Prompt

No robots, brains, crowns, Greek statues, circuit boards, dashboards, text, logos, neon, glossy 3D, sci-fi HUD, stock photo, or decorative clutter.

## Mode Constraints

Exactly one gate and one lantern; no more than four stars.

## Density Budget

Minimal.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Avoid turning “governance” into a glowing lock icon or compliance diagram.
