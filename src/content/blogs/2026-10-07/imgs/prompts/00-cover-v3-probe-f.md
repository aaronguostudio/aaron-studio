# Probe F — Institution in Objects

Create a 16:9 blog-cover style probe for an essay about AI agent governance. No text, letters, labels, numbers, or logos.

Compose a bright semi-flat Greek civic still life: four playful architectural objects — a striped column, an open sage gateway, a small amphora, and an olive branch — surround a simple pale decision tablet resting safely on a coral civic plinth. In the distance is a very simplified light Acropolis silhouette and a faint field of owl, lyre, and vessel icons. The objects should feel like parts of one institution, not like a museum display. The image should communicate: structures and constraints hold authority in place.

Use the supplied shop-card photos only as a reference for colorful printmaking grammar: Mediterranean coral/saffron/turquoise/sage, selective fine hatch marks, and tiny stripe motifs. Bright cream or pale-blue daylight background, semi-flat screen-print/paper-cut illustration, shallow shadow only. No humans, character portraits, god figures, fantasy, dark teal field, game interface, infographic nodes/arrows, photorealism, or invented text.
