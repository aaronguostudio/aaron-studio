## Positive Prompt

An original engraved portrait of a contemporary guardian holding the final amber seal outside a subtle green gate, on a charcoal-blue ground filled with faint mythic line icons. Warm hand-drawn crosshatching and one patterned cuff convey personal responsibility without looking like a historical costume.

No text, no copied figure, no robot, no crown, no statue, no rack/frame, no watermark.

## Mode Constraints

One portrait, one seal, one threshold.

Narrative/metaphor.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
