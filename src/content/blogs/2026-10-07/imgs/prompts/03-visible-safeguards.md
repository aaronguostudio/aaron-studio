# Prompt: 03-visible-safeguards

## Role

Make the six governance properties feel like concrete safeguards without building a busy framework diagram.

## Visual Mode

Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight

Medium

## Style Family

Bright Semi-Flat Civic Print

## Positive Prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 body illustration for an essay on AI agent governance
Primary request: a bright, semi-flat civic still life of six tangible safeguards protecting one decision tablet
Scene/backdrop: clean chalk cream with a pale-Aegean-blue floor line; no background symbols or pattern
Subject: an asymmetric public worktable holding one blank pale decision tablet in the middle. Six clearly different simple objects make a loose protective arrangement around it: a terracotta delegation seal; a short sage boundary rail; a small turquoise window/frame; a coral override lever; a neatly coiled undo thread; and a weighty olive-green accountability plinth. The objects are close enough to read as one institutional arrangement, but not placed in a circle or connected by arrows.
Style/medium: original bright paper-cut / screen-print editorial illustration, closely consistent with F2's clean civic object language; restrained hatch marks and tiny stripe detail only on object surfaces
Composition/framing: wide asymmetric still life with generous negative space and an open top third
Lighting/mood: sunny, trustworthy, quietly practical
Color palette: cream, pale Aegean blue, sage, turquoise, terracotta, coral, olive, small saffron accent; dark ink only for fine contour
Materials/textures: shallow flat layers, limited engraved hatching, no 3D realism
Text (verbatim): none
Constraints: exactly six safeguard objects plus one decision tablet; the visual predicate is tangible safeguards making a decision challengeable and repairable

## Negative Prompt

No labels, text, letters, numbers, arrows, circular node diagram, game-board, people, faces, robot, generic AI visual, icon wallpaper, photorealism, dark background, or fantasy gods.

## Mode Constraints

Do not make a radial diagram. This must feel like an editorial still life that a reader can understand without a legend.

## Density Budget

Six objects, one tablet, one worktable, empty background.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Avoid F1's decorative wallpaper and v3 Probe B's game-guide feeling.
