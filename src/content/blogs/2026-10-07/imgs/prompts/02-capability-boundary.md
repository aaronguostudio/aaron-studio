# Prompt: 02-capability-boundary

## Role

Separate a capable agent from the authority to make the consequential decision.

## Visual Mode

Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight

Quiet

## Style Family

Bright Semi-Flat Civic Print

## Positive Prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 body illustration for an essay on AI agent governance
Primary request: a bright semi-flat metaphor for capability without mandate
Scene/backdrop: clean chalk-cream tabletop against pale Aegean-blue open space; no ornament wallpaper
Subject: a small, non-anthropomorphic olive-and-bronze tool arm inside a shallow sage-green work tray. It quietly sorts three small blank terracotta tiles and one tiny stylus. Outside the tray, clearly separated by a simple coral boundary line, lies one larger pale decision token on its own small stone plinth. The tool cannot reach the token.
Style/medium: original semi-flat editorial screen-print/paper-cut image in the same clean visual world as F2; fine hatch marks only inside the tool, tray, and tiles
Composition/framing: wide, low still-life composition; the bounded tray occupies left/center and the token sits calmly on the right with ample empty space between them
Lighting/mood: bright daylight, capable but restrained
Color palette: cream, Aegean blue, sage, muted turquoise, terracotta/coral, small bronze accent
Materials/textures: crisp contours, two shallow depth planes, subtle print texture
Text (verbatim): none
Constraints: one small tool, one clear boundary, one unreachable decision token; no characters or faces

## Negative Prompt

No robot mascot, humanoid figure, screen, code, circuit board, glowing AI graphics, arrows, labels, diagrams, background icons, dark ground, photorealism, or text.

## Mode Constraints

This is a quiet visual pause, not a warning poster. The tool is useful and orderly; the boundary is legitimate, not punitive.

## Density Budget

One tray, one tool, four small work objects, one token, one boundary line.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Avoid a central gate or repeated cover composition; avoid rendering the tool as a character.
