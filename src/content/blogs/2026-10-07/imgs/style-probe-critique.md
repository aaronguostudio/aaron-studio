# Revision 3 Style Probe Critique

## Reference Reading

The supplied shop cards are not frames. Their useful visual grammar is an original contemporary-character reinterpretation of Greek cultural figures: fine woodcut/copperplate hatch marks; saturated teal, charcoal, or brick-red grounds; faint mythic icon wallpaper; one vivid modern accessory; and small, personal storytelling details such as stripes, objects, or insignia.

The probe does not reproduce individual characters, clothing, lettering, or layouts from the supplied cards.

## Probes

| Probe | File | Reading | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Gatekeeper | `00-cover-v3-probe-a.png` | Strongest direct test of the contemporary mythology character-card language. The figure, striped vest, open gate, amber seal, and ghosted icon field read in seconds. | Candidate for a series-wide character-card cover system. |
| B — Six Thresholds | `00-cover-v3-probe-b.png` | Best explanation of the six governance properties, but it reads more like a collectible systems emblem than a personal story. | Candidate as a body/framework illustration, not the preferred cover direction. |
| C — Human Seal | `00-cover-v3-probe-c.png` | Most intimate and nuanced: the human keeps the seal outside the gate. It preserves the reference's engraving language without making the history decorative. | Candidate for Part IV's final cover or a closing image. |

## What We Learned

- The character-led engraving language can carry a serious argument when the character performs one governance relation rather than merely posing as a god.
- Low-contrast mythic icons make the Greek setting felt without turning the cover into a tourist postcard.
- The striping works best as one personal detail; repeated borders or all-over meanders would imitate surface styling without its character logic.
- V2 remains the clearer system composition. V3 is the stronger cultural and emotional series identity. Aaron should choose which priority governs the full four-cover set.

## Current State

No v3 asset is compressed, inserted into an article, or promoted. `00-cover-v2.webp` remains the active cover.

# Revision 3.1 Style Probe Critique

## Correction Applied

The first v3 trio adopted too much of the references' character-card format. It made the cover dark, portrait-led, and overly rendered. This set keeps only its useful graphic vocabulary, then lets the article's own themes choose the subject: civic space, visible paths, and institutional objects.

## Probes

| Probe | File | Reading | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| D — Sunlit Civic Threshold | `00-cover-v3-probe-d.png` | The bright semicircular public space, open gate, and central empty table make authority a shared civic architecture. It is still more spatially rendered than fully flat. | Strong cover candidate; a later refinement can flatten the stone depth if selected. |
| E — Threaded City | `00-cover-v3-probe-e.png` | The coral thread creates one immediately readable route across a luminous city and terminates at the open gate. It translates auditability into an image without nodes, labels, or arrows. | Strongest narrative candidate and a natural series visual language. |
| F — Institution in Objects | `00-cover-v3-probe-f.png` | Column, gate, tablet, amphora, and olive branch become a calm semi-flat public institution. It is the closest to the desired bright, graphic, non-character direction. | Strongest style-system candidate; likely best starting point for all four covers. |

## What We Learned

- Brightness changes the tone materially: cream and Aegean-blue fields leave the reader room to think, rather than presenting governance as a night-time warning.
- The Greek setting is strongest when architecture and everyday cultural objects carry the metaphor. Myth may appear as a thread, owl, or decorative echo, not as a literal god or a new subject for the series.
- F is clearest as a reusable visual system; E is strongest when a post has a single causal path to show; D is strongest when the post's emotional center is public legitimacy.

## Current State

No v3.1 asset is compressed, inserted into an article, or promoted. `00-cover-v2.webp` remains the active cover until Aaron selects a direction.

## F2 — Clean Variant

`00-cover-v3-probe-f2-clean.png` removes the faint owl, lyre, and vessel wallpaper behind F while retaining the pale distant Acropolis silhouette. The foreground now carries the complete argument, and the ample cream space makes it feel less like a style exercise. This is the preferred F variant for future selection.

# Revision 5 — Bright Material Style Probe Critique

## Question

Can the series regain material richness without returning to a dark, portrait-led character card or filling the clean background with decorative Greek motifs?

| Probe | File | Texture source | Reading | Use if selected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G — Sunlit Intaglio | `00-cover-v4-probe-g.png` | varied etched hatch marks and warm ink on paper | Most intellectually precise. It retains the best trait of the supplied cards—the etched material language—while the subject stays institutional. | Best primary system for the four serious essays; body images can simplify the hatch density. |
| H — Aegean Fresco | `00-cover-v4-probe-h.png` | lime plaster, mineral pigment, light brush wear | Most geographically felt. It is bright and warm, though slightly more decorative/atmospheric than the argument needs. | Strong for travel transition, opening, or a quieter reflection image. |
| I — Mediterranean Risograph | `00-cover-v4-probe-i.png` | bright spot inks, grain, halftone, mild misregistration | Most vivid and contemporary. It carries energy without darkness, but needs restraint so the series does not become a retro poster collection. | Strong for a hook, Part I's incentive story, or social crop; use less saturated body variants. |
| J — Paper and Textile Collage | `00-cover-v4-probe-j.png` | cotton fibre, cut edges, shallow physical shadow | Most tactile and premium, with a calm three-dimensional presence. It moves closest to physical craft. | Strong cover option, but may feel more like an art-object series than an argument series if used everywhere. |

## Recommendation

The strongest next experiment is not to choose one style absolutely. Use **G's sunlit engraved linework as the primary language**, add **J's shallow paper depth**, and borrow **I's very restrained ink grain**. Keep H as a situational accent rather than the default. That blend returns texture to the actual objects while preserving F2's clean background and readable thesis.

## Current State

No v4 probe is compressed, inserted, or promoted. `00-cover-v3-clean.webp` and the v3 body-image set remain active.
