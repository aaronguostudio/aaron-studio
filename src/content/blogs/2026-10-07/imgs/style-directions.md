# Style Directions

## Recommended Directions

1. **Field Signal Editorial** — paper-cut gate/lantern, tactile and reflective; selected.
2. **Soft Glass Narrative** — good body mode, but too familiar for the series conclusion.
3. **Abstract Mechanism** — could become an empty orb/gradient.

## Decision

**Agent-selected — Unified:** Field Signal Editorial, with the green gate as a quiet series motif.

## Revision 3 Style Probe Menu

| Probe | Style family | Role | Why it fits | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — Character Atlas Engraving | Contemporary myth character card; hand-engraved linework, saturated ground, icon field | loud cover | Closest to the supplied references and gives governance a memorable human face | Can turn into generic “Greek philosopher” décor if the gate/decision relation is vague |
| B — Mythic Operator Diagram | Printed systems emblem with one character and six icon thresholds | framework | Tests whether the six governance properties can become a collectible visual object | Could become a busy infographic |
| C — Quiet Guardian Portrait | Restrained engraved portrait with one seal and background pictograms | narrative cover/body direction | Tests the emotional side of accountability and works as an interior illustration | May be too quiet for the series conclusion |

**Decision: user-delegated Style Probe Mode.** These probes deliberately depart from v2's workbench collage. They borrow the supplied references' general visual grammar, not their exact figures, copied designs, or typography.

## Revision 3.1 Style Probe Menu

| Probe | Style family | Role | Why it fits | Risk controlled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D — Sunlit Civic Print | bright semi-flat architectural editorial | cover | lets the governance idea live in public space rather than a portrait | no claim about an exact ancient trial venue |
| E — Threaded City Print | symbolic architectural collage | cover/body direction | makes an auditable action path intuitive without literal diagrams | no nodes, arrows, labels, or game-map language |
| F — Civic Still Life | semi-flat cultural-object editorial | series-system candidate | uses Greek visual culture without making mythology the topic | no heroic god or central character |

Shared rules: chalk-cream/light-blue grounds; coral, saffron, cerulean, sage, and terracotta accents; shallow paper-cut depth only; selective print hatching inside objects; minimal stripe/meander as detail. Avoid deep night fields, photorealism, portraits, UI panels, arrows, and invented lettering.

## Revision 4 — Selected Body Direction

**User-selected / Agent-applied: Unified Bright Semi-Flat Civic Print.** F2 is the anchor. Body images use the same clean cream/Aegean background, restrained hatching, fine dark contours, and Mediterranean palette, but change composition and visual job:

- architectural cutaway for civic layers;
- bounded workbench metaphor for capability versus authority;
- civic still life for concrete safeguards.

No decorative background icon wallpaper. Pattern exists only where it describes an object or a boundary.

## Revision 5 — Material Style Probe Menu

| Probe | Style family | Source of texture | Best quality to judge | Risk |

| J — Paper and Textile Collage | crafted editorial collage | paper fibre, torn edge, woven stripe, shallow shadow | warmest material contrast to the clean source art | can become handmade-crafty rather than serious |

**Decision: user-delegated Style Probe Mode.** Keep F2's composition as a controlled comparison; vary material language, not the thesis or background clutter.
