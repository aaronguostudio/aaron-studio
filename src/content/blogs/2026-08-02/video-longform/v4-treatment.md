# V4 Treatment — Cover-Led, Chaptered Score

## Selected Direction

Keep the approved Clean Indigo editorial film and narration unchanged. Improve
the product opening and sound arc only.

## Opening

- Start on `imgs/00-cover-thumbnail-youtube-v2-c.png` for 2.8 seconds.
- The complete title promise, both illustrated programmers, and Aaron identity
  are visible at frame zero.
- A quiet cello phrase begins with the cover. Narration starts only after the
  cover releases into the existing opening scene.

## Music Strategy

`chaptered`, not continuous.

Reuse the project-owned score from the approved and published FDE film because
Aaron explicitly selected its restrained low-cello, piano, and gradual language
as the reference. Use four bounded cues from that score, separated by three
meaningful narration-only passages.

1. **Opening disagreement** — score first, then duck beneath the first claims.
2. **Common ground and community** — quiet reflective cello and piano.
3. **Industry evidence to critical systems** — a restrained analytical chapter
   that darkens without becoming busy.
4. **Questions, ownership, and end card** — return to the closing cello cadence.

The base narration-bed gain rises from the V3 range of roughly `0.065–0.085` to
roughly `0.095–0.12`, using the calmer source score. The opening may briefly own
the mix before narration; the end card may lift after speech stops.

## Silence

Silence is intentional around:

- the two programmers' detailed quality gates;
- the learning-cost turn before industry evidence;
- the objection and moving-boundary section before the final questions.

These gaps prevent the film from feeling continuously scored and give the voice
more authority.

## Prototype Gate

Before the full V4 render, review a representative reel containing the cover-led
opening, one middle chapter cue, one narration-only passage, and the closing
cadence. Check on headphones and phone-scale speakers.
