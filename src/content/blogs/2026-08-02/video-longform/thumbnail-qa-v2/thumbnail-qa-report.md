# YouTube Thumbnail QA V2

## Candidates

- C: `../../imgs/00-cover-thumbnail-youtube-v2-c.png`
- D: `../../imgs/00-cover-thumbnail-youtube-v2-d.png`

## Review Sizes

- Mobile: 320 × 180
- YouTube Studio list: 226 × 127

## Decision

Select candidate C.

It preserves the video’s porcelain, indigo, and editorial-portrait language,
while giving the package a clear focal promise: `READ THE CODE?`. At both review
sizes the headline remains legible, both programmers remain recognizable, and
the `READ.` / `DON’T.` conflict reads without requiring the title or description.

Candidate D is a valid alternate, but its central headline becomes smaller at
list scale and the composition has three equally weighted vertical zones.

## Result

PASS — dedicated YouTube cover, not a reused slide frame.
