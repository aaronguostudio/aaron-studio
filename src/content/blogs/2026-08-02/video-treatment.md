# Video Treatment: The AI Code Debate

## Product Promise

Turn the Mitchell Hashimoto versus Robert C. Martin argument into a useful
operating rule: the right review depth depends on what the code is authorized
to change.

## Editorial Arc

1. Open with the two apparently opposite answers.
2. Reconstruct each person's real quality gate instead of treating the debate
   as a slogan contest.
3. Identify the common ground: a human still owns the judgment.
4. Explain why the community split around understanding, evidence, and
   maintainability.
5. Test the debate against current OpenAI, Cloudflare, and Godot experiments.
6. Draw Aaron's boundary around critical systems and systems of record.
7. End with a three-level operating rule and four questions for any AI change.

## Visual Direction

The selected direction is **Clean Indigo Editorial**.

- Solid warm porcelain background; no grid, ruling, texture, glow, gradient,
  green accent, or persistent dark field.
- Deep indigo carries the argument; neutral grey carries secondary evidence.
- Georgia carries claims, monospace carries labels, and sans-serif carries
  phrase-level captions.
- Header contains only `AARON GUO` plus a small chapter marker.
- Nineteen scenes use distinct visual structures without looking like separate
  slide decks.

## Motion Direction

- Every scene preserves a visible element before the next scene takes over.
- Crossfades last about 0.42 seconds and use restrained vertical continuity.
- Motion explains sequence, ownership, comparison, or authority. It never
  decorates empty space.
- No bounce, overshoot, page flip, infinite loop, per-character effect, or
  arbitrary camera motion.

## Music Direction

Use one continuous original score, composed to the film's chapter arc rather
than looped beneath it. Low cello represents consequence and authority; sparse
felt piano represents human judgment; a very quiet analog pulse represents the
AI workflow. The pulse recedes for common ground and critical systems, returns
slightly for industry evidence and the operating rule, then leaves a held chord
for the brand card. Narration remains roughly 20 dB above the score during
speech; the score never uses drums, stings, or trailer-style lifts.

## Asset Decision

Three restrained image-rich moments interrupt the typography without changing
the film's visual spine:

- two generated editorial portraits humanize the opening disagreement;
- one generated code-folio and verification-instrument still makes the two
  community costs tangible;
- one indigo ledger briefly makes “system of record” concrete.

The portraits and object study are scene media. The ledger is the film's only
semantic sprite and appears once. All four images are visibly illustrative,
project-owned, provenance-logged, removable without changing the argument, and
never presented as documentary evidence. There are still no fake social
screenshots or fake product UIs.
