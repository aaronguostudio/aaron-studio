# Prompt: 02-review-depth-ladder

## Role

Reusable framework diagram for the four cumulative levels of review.

## Visual Mode

Operator Diagram

## Visual Weight

Medium

## Style Family

Executive Brief inside the Field Signal Control system.

## Positive Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram  
Asset type: 16:9 blog framework graphic  
Primary request: create a clean operator-grade diagram showing review depth increasing from reversible output to authoritative state.  
Scene/backdrop: warm-white paper field with crisp graphite grid alignment and generous margins.  
Subject: four large ascending horizontal steps or depth bands ordered strictly from left to right: `OUTCOME`, `EVIDENCE`, `SYSTEM MODEL`, `CRITICAL PATH`. A single simple arrow beneath them runs left to right from `REVERSIBLE` to `AUTHORITATIVE`. The final step has one restrained muted-coral boundary edge; the Evidence step may have one tiny cyan trace; the Critical Path step may have one soft-green verification point.  
Style/medium: Executive Brief, large simple shapes, modern editorial diagram, tactile paper material, precise hierarchy, not a software dashboard.  
Composition/framing: landscape 16:9, four steps centered, labels large enough to read at 320 px width, one direction arrow only.  
Text (verbatim, the only permitted words): `OUTCOME`, `EVIDENCE`, `SYSTEM MODEL`, `CRITICAL PATH`, `REVERSIBLE`, `AUTHORITATIVE`.  
Typography: uppercase geometric sans serif, bold, crisp, high contrast, no tiny type.  
Color palette: graphite and warm white with limited cyan, green, and muted coral signals.  
Constraints: exact left-to-right order; exact spelling; exactly six labels; one arrow from REVERSIBLE toward AUTHORITATIVE; no other text, numbers, icons, legends, captions, logos, or watermark.

## Negative Prompt

Wrong order, backward arrow, circular loop, pyramid, dense flowchart, nested boxes, tiny icons, extra labels, explanatory paragraphs, misspellings, decorative numbers, fake UI, dashboard, chart wall, glowing glass cards, neon gradients, code symbols, robot imagery.

## Mode Constraints

Semantic correctness is a hard gate. The four levels are cumulative and must visibly deepen left to right.

## Density Budget

Light diagram: four bands, six labels, one arrow, at most three small color accents.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Do not make attractive but semantically wrong paths. No arrow may skip, reverse, branch, or loop between levels.
