# Prompt: 00-thumbnail-candidate-a

## Role

Mobile-readable YouTube and social thumbnail derived from the selected clean cover.

## Visual Mode

Editorial Minimal + typography

## Visual Weight

Loud

## Style Family

Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: 16:9 YouTube thumbnail  
Input images: Image 1 is the selected clean cover and must remain the visual-world reference.  
Primary request: adapt the selected Authority Gate cover into a high-contrast thumbnail. Preserve the gate, code-strip transformation, palette, tactile paper material, and overall editorial identity. Create one calm graphite copy zone on the left covering about 42% of the canvas; keep the authority-gate scene simplified on the right.  
Text (verbatim, the only permitted text): `AI WROTE IT` on line 1, `WHAT CAN IT` on line 2, `CHANGE?` on line 3.  
Typography: uppercase geometric sans serif, very bold, crisp, large, mobile readable; line 1 small soft cyan; line 2 large warm white; line 3 largest warm amber or muted coral. Keep all letters fully inside the copy zone with generous margins.  
Composition/framing: 16:9, clean left text zone and right visual world, strong separation, no extra badges.  
Constraints: render the three lines exactly, spelled correctly and in this order; no other text, letters, numbers, logos, captions, signatures, or watermark; preserve the selected cover's central metaphor.

## Negative Prompt

Extra words, duplicate words, misspellings, tiny copy, condensed illegible lettering, text over the hand or gate, badges, arrows, emojis, logos, fake UI, code, neon glow, clutter, dark-blue AI look.

## Mode Constraints

The headline must dominate at 320 px width. The visual is subordinate but still recognizable as the Authority Gate.

## Density Budget

Narrative / metaphor plus exactly three headline lines.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Image models often invent captions or alter punctuation. Permit only the exact three lines and preserve the question mark in `CHANGE?`.
