# Mode Mix

## Set-Level Pacing

The set uses two loud entry assets and two medium body assets. The cover and thumbnail share one high-contrast visual world but are never shown together inside the article. The authority-ripple image provides tactile narrative relief; the review ladder shifts into a precise operator artifact. No body image is loud, and no image repeats the same composition.

## Image Plan

| Image | Role | Visual mode | Style family | Visual weight | Reason | Reuse candidate? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00-cover | Cover | Editorial Minimal | Field Signal Editorial | loud | A single human-controlled authority gate earns the title without explaining the whole article. | no |

## Density Budget

- Narrative / metaphor body images: 1
- Light diagrams: 1
- Dense mechanism diagrams: 0
- Cover and thumbnail: 2 entry assets, excluded from the body density ratio

The two-body-image set intentionally approximates the 70 / 20 / 10 rule at small scale without adding a decorative third image.

## Style Fatigue Guardrail

Glass / Fluent effects may appear only as one small cyan or green evidence accent in the review ladder, at roughly 10% of that image. No glowing route line spans the set. Paper texture stays subtle and clean; only the cover and ripple image may use tactile cut-paper depth.
