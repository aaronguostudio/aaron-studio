# Style Directions

Concept is locked to **Authority Gate** in `visual-strategy.md`. The options below change how that predicate is rendered; they do not replace it with a different metaphor.

## Article Fit Summary

This is a concise, high-consequence engineering argument. The images need to feel authored, tactile, and operationally credible. The strongest set will use one coherent cover/body world plus a more functional diagram variant. The main visual failure modes are generic AI glow, legal/compliance heaviness, and consulting-slide clutter.

| Candidate E | Soft Glass Narrative | restrained accent only | Maintains continuity with Aaron's visual brand through soft depth and a small cyan evidence layer | Reusing glass as the primary style would make this post look too similar to prior AI workflow essays |

## Style Cohesion Decision

| Cohesion model | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unified | Not recommended | A single style would either weaken the framework or make the body too diagrammatic. |
| Controlled Mix | **Recommended** | One primary cover/body family plus Executive Brief or Cutaway System Map where the image job changes. |
| Experimental Mix | Not recommended | The short article does not have enough visual length to justify three unrelated worlds. |

## Recommendation

Choose **Direction A — Field Signal Control** with a **Controlled Mix**:

- Field Signal Editorial for cover, thumbnail world, and authority-ripple metaphor.
- Executive Brief for the four-level ladder.
- Cutaway System Map used only as a compositional influence inside the ripple image.
- Soft Glass Narrative limited to small cyan/green evidence accents, not the base style.

This direction departs from the Soft Glass Narrative baseline while preserving Aaron's restraint, human scale, negative space, and recognizable cyan / amber / green signals.

## Prompt Implications

- **Direction A:** prompts emphasize clean paper-cut shapes, graphite structure, tactile warm-white surfaces, one muted-coral authority boundary, and restrained cyan / green evidence signals.
- **Direction B:** prompts emphasize documentary material realism, top-down composition, paper and ledger texture, natural light, and one restrained graphic overlay.
- **Direction C:** prompts emphasize large geometric zones, strong typographic negative space, high mobile readability, minimal labels, and no decorative dashboard components.
- All directions ban robots, glowing brains, code rain, sci-fi HUDs, database cylinders, fake UI, and unreadable text.
