## Description

Mitchell Hashimoto reads the code produced by his AI agents. Robert C. Martin
says he does not read the implementation at all. The disagreement sounds
fundamental—but both positions still depend on human judgment.

This video examines what the two arguments actually say, why the software
community split around them, what current industry experiments reveal, and why
critical systems and systems of record require a stricter boundary.

My conclusion: review depth should follow authority. The more consequential a
change is, the stronger the evidence—and the clearer the human ownership—must
be.
