# Director Memo: Review Depth Follows Authority

## Central Idea

The debate is not really “read versus don't read.” It is a disagreement about
where trustworthy understanding should live: in direct implementation reading,
or in specifications, tests, tooling, and independent verification. The film
makes that distinction visible before introducing Aaron's authority boundary.

## Rhythm

The 09:44 film moves through four acts:

- `00:00–02:33` — the two positions and their shared accountability;
- `02:33–06:00` — community concerns and real industry experiments;
- `06:00–08:57` — critical systems, systems of record, and proportional review;
- `08:57–09:44` — four decision questions and the ownership conclusion.

The opening conflict is fast and symmetrical, with two visibly illustrated
portraits giving the disagreement a human face. A tactile read-versus-verify
still resets the medium during the community section, and one ledger appears
only when “system of record” needs a concrete mnemonic. Evidence scenes slow
down enough to distinguish reported facts from Aaron's interpretation. The
final third becomes simpler and more spacious as the argument resolves into a
rule.

## Sound

The first music decision was silence: narration seemed rhythmically sufficient
and no cleared library track justified its presence. That decision became too
conservative once the film gained portraits and tactile editorial media. The
final direction uses an original low cello, sparse piano, and nearly invisible
analog pulse written to the same chapter turns as the picture. Volume changes
are broad and gradual rather than phrase-by-phrase ducking.

## Evidence Boundary

Public statements are paraphrased and attributed. Company metrics are presented
as the organizations' own reports, not universal proof. Hacker News discussions
inform only recurring themes; they are not described as representative polling.
Preprints and surveys remain contextual evidence rather than verdicts.

## Explicit Exclusions

- No rejected `paused → inactive` case or line-by-line implementation example.
- No tutorial-level CI, test, rollback, or database walkthrough.
- No fake X post, customer record, incident screen, photorealistic reenactment,
  robot, code rain, cyber lock, neon dashboard, or database cylinder.
- No `FIELD JOURNAL` label, ruled background, green success color, dark theme,
  rounded-card dashboard, or full-page hard cut.
- No decorative sprite. The single ledger is a bounded mnemonic for “system of
  record”; the portraits and object still are scene media, not sprites.

## Final Lock

Composition: `AuthorityBoundaryLongformFinal`. Narration timing, evidence,
scene order, captions, and thumbnail direction are locked. The enhanced master
is rendered as a new scored sibling file and passed visual, audio, decode, and
commercial music-rights review.
