## Post Copy

Should engineers read AI-generated code?

Mitchell Hashimoto says yes. Uncle Bob says he doesn't read the implementation; he relies on specifications, tests, and verification.

I think the binary question misses the real risk.

A disposable prototype and a system-of-record write should not receive the same review. Five lines that change a permission, balance, or customer status may deserve more scrutiny than 1,000 lines of UI.

My rule: review depth should follow authority.

Outcome → Evidence → System model → Critical path

The more power code has to change authoritative state, the more human understanding it requires.

AI lowers implementation cost. It does not lower the cost of consequences.

Where does your team draw that boundary?
