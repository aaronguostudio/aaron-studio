As AI takes on more coding, I want engineers to get closer to the business.

Years ago at Amazon, I had already reached a judgment: a company’s ability to fund a large organization does not tell us how much of that work its customers need. Stronger execution tools make that question harder to avoid.

For the next five to ten years, I would invest in five things:

- Learn an industry deeply.
- Spend time with customers.
- Practice product decisions.
- Test commercial value.
- Follow a project into actual use.

One project can develop all five. Watch where a customer gets stuck. Try a small alternative. Find out what would make adoption worthwhile. Return after delivery and see what changed.

That is the opportunity I find exciting: using stronger tools to make things we care about that someone else actually needs.
