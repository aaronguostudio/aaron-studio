# Prompt: 01-watch-the-work-v1

## Role
body; customer section.
## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor
## Visual Weight
quiet
## Style Family
Soft Glass Narrative / tactile flat editorial
## Positive Prompt
Create a premium editorial illustration, landscape 16:9. Soft Glass Narrative adapted as Work that reaches people: clean tactile paper-cut shapes, subtle print texture, adult human proportions, warm ivory, deep graphite, muted teal, restrained amber. Sophisticated magazine composition, generous negative space, no stock-photo look. An engineer observes where a customer pauses in an existing paper workflow. Side view, two adult people, one desk with a short row of blank documents; customer holds one document while engineer listens with a notebook closed. Absolutely no text, lettering, logos, numbers, watermarks or fake UI. Exactly one visual idea. No robots, glowing brains, neon, dashboard grids, business suits, stock smiles, trophies or tiny decorative symbols. Simplified anatomically coherent hands, natural body proportions. Clean broad forms.
## Negative Prompt
No collage of mechanisms, microtext, extra limbs, uncanny faces, employer branding or layoffs imagery.
## Mode Constraints
One predicate: An engineer observes where a customer pauses in an existing paper workflow.
## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor, maximum three main visual groups.
## Prior Failure To Avoid
No full-frame dashboard or generic office scene; the human action must be readable.
