# Prompt: 00-cover-v1-a

## Role
cover; near top.
## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal
## Visual Weight
medium
## Style Family
Soft Glass Narrative / tactile flat editorial
## Positive Prompt
Create a premium editorial illustration, landscape 16:9. Soft Glass Narrative adapted as Work that reaches people: clean tactile paper-cut shapes, subtle print texture, adult human proportions, warm ivory, deep graphite, muted teal, restrained amber. Sophisticated magazine composition, generous negative space, no stock-photo look. An engineer hands a small working prototype to a customer across one shared table. Wide side-view scene; engineer left, customer right, simple tabletop linking them; generous clear ivory upper half. Absolutely no text, lettering, logos, numbers, watermarks or fake UI. Exactly one visual idea. No robots, glowing brains, neon, dashboard grids, business suits, stock smiles, trophies or tiny decorative symbols. Simplified anatomically coherent hands, natural body proportions. Clean broad forms.
## Negative Prompt
No collage of mechanisms, microtext, extra limbs, uncanny faces, employer branding or layoffs imagery.
## Mode Constraints
One predicate: An engineer hands a small working prototype to a customer across one shared table.
## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor, maximum three main visual groups.
## Prior Failure To Avoid
No full-frame dashboard or generic office scene; the human action must be readable.
