# Prompt: v2-00-cover-a

## Role
Cover / thumbnail

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight
medium

## Style Family
Tactile paper editorial; existing author-liked style

## Positive Prompt
Use case: style-transfer. Create a new 16:9 clean blog cover, using the attached image ONLY for its tactile paper texture, restrained painted editorial shapes, warm ivory / graphite / muted teal / amber palette, and quality of illustration. Do not preserve the people, couple composition, pottery, apron, kitchen or handoff.
Theme: As AI handles implementation, an engineer's most valuable attention moves toward the customer's business problem.
Scene: a clearly professional order-operations office, viewed in a wide three-quarter angle. ONE engineer, short dark hair and simple graphite work clothes, seen mostly from behind, stands slightly right of centre at a large physical operations board. The board has just three large document silhouettes in a horizontal sequence; the engineer places one amber exception note at the missing handoff between the first and second document. The engineer's hand and the blocked business step are the focus.
A small unattended open laptop in the lower-left foreground is a quiet secondary layer: three clean software task tiles and a simple code-bracket symbol imply delegated implementation continuing. No readable words, no tiny code, no charts. A couple of archive boxes on a low professional shelf give business context without clutter.
Composition: one person, one dominant work artifact, one small software workbench. Clear long diagonal connects laptop and business board; generous ivory negative space upper-left; confident flat forms, print grain, soft dimensional shadows, calm light. The board must feel like business work under examination, not school, domestic art, a romantic scene or a generic meeting. No humanoid agent/robot, no people representing AI, no floating HUD, no arrows everywhere, no logo, no title text, no watermark. Actual imagined business illustration, not documentary photography.

## Negative Prompt
Explicit avoidance constraints are included verbatim in the submitted prompt above.

## Mode Constraints
One work action or comparison; business context and human roles visible in the image.

## Density Budget
At most three main visual groups; sparse task artifacts, no dense mechanism.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Ambiguous couple-like relationship and domestic setting that obscure the article's work/agent topic.
