# Prompt: v2-00-thumbnail-a

## Role
Cover / thumbnail

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight
loud

## Style Family
Tactile paper editorial; existing author-liked style

## Positive Prompt
Use case: compositing. Make a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail using the reference clean cover as the entire visual world: ONE engineer studies a business workflow at a physical board, small unattended software workbench secondary. Keep the tactile ivory paper, graphite and muted teal illustration. Preserve the meaningful business scene, no extra people or objects.
Composition: left 44 percent is a confident near-black graphite typography field; right 56 percent shows a reframed crop of the engineer's hand locating the amber missing step on the business board. Keep the person and the amber note visibly distinct. The scene should remain legible at 320 pixels wide. A small partial laptop may sit at the lower dividing edge if there is room, but never under the headline.
Exact allowed text only:
small eyebrow: "AI & ENGINEERS"
large line 1: "BUILD WHAT"
largest line 2: "MATTERS"
Set eyebrow in muted teal, first large line in warm ivory and MATTERS in warm amber. Bold clean condensed editorial sans serif, immaculate spelling, strong spacing, generous margins, not distressed. No additional text anywhere, no labels on illustration, no dates, logos, watermark, subtitle, faces looking at the viewer, robot or decorative arrows. This is a thumbnail about using AI execution to address real business needs.

## Negative Prompt
Explicit avoidance constraints are included verbatim in the submitted prompt above.

## Mode Constraints
One work action or comparison; business context and human roles visible in the image.

## Density Budget
At most three main visual groups; sparse task artifacts, no dense mechanism.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Ambiguous couple-like relationship and domestic setting that obscure the article's work/agent topic.
