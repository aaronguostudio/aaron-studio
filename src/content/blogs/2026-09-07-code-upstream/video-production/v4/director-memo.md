# Why V4 changes the viewing experience

The prior film changed supporting claims more often than it changed the viewer's mental map. V4 lets the chapter heading persist, while a three-item agenda gives each example an address. This is the reason to animate: show the next chapter arriving, then move attention within it.

Music begins on that visible change. It has a short response in the breathing space and recedes after the first sentence begins. Arbitrary mid-scene entrances are removed. The closing has one continuous arc instead of two isolated rises.

No new factual claim or personal experience is introduced. All illustration crops retain the accepted professional-business meaning.
