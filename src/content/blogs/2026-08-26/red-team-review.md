# Red-Team Review

## Draft reviewed

`ai-made-process-cheaper-judgment-is-still-expensive.md` — English first draft, 2026-08-16.

## Issues found

### 1. The economic frame risks remaining intellectual rather than commercial

The J-curve section explains complementary organizational capital, but it does not yet tell a leader what changes in an AI budget or capability strategy. Add the distinction between rented model access and the cumulative asset built around it.

### 2. The evidence base is unusually selected

OpenAI, Anthropic, and DeepSeek are frontier or highly agent-native cases. “Reported” language is present, but the article should make the selection boundary explicit before deriving the broader mechanism.

### 3. The examples remain too software-centric

The target reader includes product leaders and founders. Add one compact non-code example showing how a domain rule becomes reusable capital while domain judgment remains human.

### 4. The second Process Capital question sounds too deterministic

“Can the system apply or check it?” could imply that every valuable rule must compile into a linter. Clarify that an artifact can also automatically surface evidence, deviations, or escalation; product and business decisions often remain probabilistic.

### 5. The article names retirement but does not show a management signal for retirement

Add an observable condition: a rule that produces more exceptions and interpretation than avoided judgment is becoming a liability.

### 6. The final section partly summarizes instead of changing the reader’s next decision

Replace part of the recap with an operating cadence: inspect where review repeatedly stops, encode recurring judgment, and retire rules whose exception traffic exceeds their value.

### 7. The personal authority must remain proportional

The fourteen-agent teardown is valid operator context, but it should not be framed as independent proof of the economic thesis. Keep it as the reason Aaron noticed the mechanism; keep external and repository evidence responsible for supporting it.

## Required revisions

- Add the rented capability versus owned organizational capital distinction.
- Add an explicit extreme-sample caveat.
- Add a non-software example without introducing a second framework.
- Expand “executable” to include automatic evidence and escalation.
- Sharpen the ending into a management operating rule.
