# Canon Note

## Canonical Idea

AI does not eliminate process. It changes process economics: reusable constraints, evaluations, decision history, and feedback loops can become organizational capital when agent execution scales faster than human attention.

## Reusable Frame

**Process Capital Test**

1. Does it capture a recurring, expensive judgment?
2. Can the system apply it, check it, surface evidence, or route the exception?
3. Who owns its revision or retirement as it depreciates?

Failure modes: bureaucracy, hidden toil, institutional fossil.

## Claims Added

- A harness is organizational capital in executable form.
- Model access is rented capability; acceptance criteria, evals, operating rules, and decision memory can become owned organizational assets.
- AI makes bad process easier to multiply as well as good process cheaper to execute.

## Claims Updated

- “Machines check the checkable; humans keep judgment” now includes depreciation: encoded judgment remains an asset only while it reduces more human interpretation than it creates.
- “AI amplifies the system it enters” now has a more specific mechanism: cheaper execution increases the return on good constraints and the spread of bad patterns.
