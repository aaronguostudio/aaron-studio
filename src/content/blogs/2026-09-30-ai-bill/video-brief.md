# Video Brief

Channel: ai-native-builder (Google account arronstudio@gmail.com). Language: English. Target length: 7-8 minutes.

**Angle:** This video is not "the blog in narration form." It is about the moment a $200 AI subscription got an exchange rate, told through Aaron opening his own 30-day bill line by line, so the viewer leaves knowing that the real price of agentic coding is the re-read, and how to price their own.

## Target Audience

Developers and technical leads paying $100-$500/month for Codex, Claude Code or both. They believe "Pro 200 got nerfed" and are deciding whether to stay, upgrade to Pro 500, or switch to Claude Max. They have never priced their own usage.

## Desired Emotion

Clarity after irritation: "I'm not guessing anymore; I can read this bill."

## Core Promise

In eight minutes: what actually changed (the unit, not just the quota), one heavy user's real bill priced at list, which plan wins for that workload and why, and how to price your own before October 29.

## Title/Thumbnail Expectation

Title: "OpenAI Halved My $200 Plan. So I Priced My Own AI Bill." Thumbnail promise: a real bill with a surprising line. Viewers expect real numbers and a verdict within the first minute.

## High-Shock Facts

- The Codex weekly meter on Aaron's Pro plan read 99-100% in five of six September windows. (lived, 9/10)
- OpenAI: new Pro 200 nets out at "half the dollar in API spend." (source, 8/10)
- After Oct 30 every OpenAI tier costs $20 per 1× Plus; the old Pro 200 was the only bulk discount. (math, 8/10)
- 30 days priced at list: Claude Code ≈ $9,400 vs Codex Pro ≈ $3,600, each on a $200 plan. (lived, 9/10)
- Output tokens were a small slice; cache reads were 58-78% of cost. (lived, 9/10)
- Astra's cache-read price is 5× Opus 5.5's ($1.00 vs $0.20). (source, 7/10)
- Same Astra tokens at GPT-6.1 Sol ≈ 12% of the cost. (lived+source, 8/10)
- "Simply say 'we're cutting limits in half.' Five words." (community, 6/10)

## Hook Type

Result-first contradiction. Target: a Pro 200 subscriber. Transformation: from "is it nerfed?" to "I know my unit cost." Stakes: a decision due by October 29 and a halved budget on the tool that already capped out.

## Story Structure

Investigation: cold open (meter at 100% + email) → promise → the unit changed → the room reacts → open the bill → the reveal (the re-read) → the verdict and the bigger lever → what I'm doing → what comes next → how to price yours → payoff. Chosen because the viewer should feel the bill being opened, not hear an essay.
