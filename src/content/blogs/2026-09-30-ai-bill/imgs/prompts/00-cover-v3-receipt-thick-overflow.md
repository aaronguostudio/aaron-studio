# Prompt: 00-cover-v3-receipt-thick-overflow

## Role
Cover (refined candidate 3 of selected Route A). Regeneration of v2 with a narrower contract: v2 read well but its amber line was too thin to dominate at mobile size.

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight
loud

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
High-end editorial still life seen from directly above. A long white thermal-paper receipt lies vertically on a calm warm-white paper surface, positioned in the left third of the frame, with its top edge slightly curled and a finely torn bottom edge. It is printed with many thin graphite line items, each an abstract bar of similar length with no characters, evenly spaced from top to bottom. One single line item in the lower half is different: it is printed as a very thick, solid warm-amber band, about five times taller than the graphite rows and as tall as a finger is wide, that starts inside the receipt like any other row and then runs horizontally off the right edge of the receipt, continuing across the empty paper surface for most of the frame width, many times longer than the receipt is wide. Flat printed ink, crisp edges. Soft daylight from the upper left, gentle paper shadow. Generous empty warm-white space above the amber bar on the right. Palette: warm white, ink graphite, one warm amber. Quiet, exact, editorial. 16:9 landscape.

## Negative Prompt
No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no barcodes, no logos, no hands, no people, no robots, no glowing AI, no neon, no screens, no dashboards, no 3D gloss, no gradients, no extra objects, no clutter.

## Mode Constraints
One relation: one line item overflows the bill. Three elements only.

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
v2 rendered the amber item as a hairline; it must be a heavy band that visibly outweighs the whole receipt. Do not render it as tape or ribbon.
