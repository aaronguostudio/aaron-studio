# Prompt: 00-cover-probe-c-reread-stack

## Role
Cover concept probe C.

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight
loud

## Style Family

## Positive Prompt
A high-end editorial still life on a warm-white desk surface. A very tall, neat stack of identical printed pages, hundreds of copies of the same page, rises from the desk like a tower; the page edges show as fine graphite lines. A thin warm-amber paper ribbon loops from the top of the stack, arcs around, and feeds back into the bottom of the same stack, forming one continuous loop, suggesting the same pages are read again and again. Beside the stack sits a small vintage mechanical counter with rolling number wheels shown blank. Soft daylight, crisp shadows, generous negative space on the right. Restrained palette: warm white, ink graphite, one warm amber accent. Flat editorial print feel, calm and precise. 16:9 landscape.

## Negative Prompt
No readable text on pages, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no robots, no glowing AI, no neon, no screens, no dashboards, no people, no hands, no clutter, no extra objects.

## Mode Constraints
One relation: the same context is re-read in a loop. Max three major elements.

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Pages must not carry legible fake copy; counter wheels must be blank.
