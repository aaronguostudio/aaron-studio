# Prompt: 00-cover-v1-receipt-spill

## Role
Cover (refined candidate 1 of selected Route A: the itemized receipt where one line dominates). Article: "OpenAI Halved My $200 Plan, So I Priced My Own AI Bill". Thesis: AI subscriptions are becoming cloud bills; for agentic coding the line item that matters is the re-read (cache reads).

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight
loud

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial (warm-white print, graphite structure, paper objects, one amber signal)

## Positive Prompt
High-end editorial still life, viewed from directly above at a slight angle. One long white thermal-paper receipt lies on a calm warm-white paper surface in the left third of the frame, its top end gently curled. The receipt is printed with many thin, evenly spaced graphite rows, nearly identical to each other, like the same entry repeated again and again; every row is an abstract printed bar with no characters. On the right side of the receipt a narrow column of short graphite amount marks runs down the page. Partway down, one amount is not a short mark but a thick, solid warm-amber bar that starts in the amount column and keeps going, running past the torn bottom edge of the receipt and continuing straight across the surface until it exits the bottom of the frame, far longer than the entire receipt. The amber bar is flat printed ink on paper, not a ribbon. Soft daylight from the upper left, subtle paper shadow, crisp focus. The right half of the frame is calm empty warm-white paper. Palette: warm white, ink graphite, one warm amber. Quiet, precise, magazine-cover quality. 16:9 landscape.

## Negative Prompt
No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no barcodes, no logos, no brand marks, no hands, no people, no robots, no glowing AI, no neon, no screens, no dashboards, no 3D gloss, no gradients, no extra objects, no clutter.

## Mode Constraints
One relation: a single line item dwarfs the whole bill. Three elements only: receipt, amber line, surface.

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor. No diagramming.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Probe A placed the receipt near center-left with the amber bar reading slightly like tape; keep it printed ink and leave the right half empty for thumbnail text.
