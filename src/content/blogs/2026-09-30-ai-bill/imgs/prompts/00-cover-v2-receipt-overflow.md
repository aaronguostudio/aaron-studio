# Prompt: 00-cover-v2-receipt-overflow

## Role
Cover (refined candidate 2 of selected Route A). Same thesis as v1; tests a horizontal overflow so the dominant item reads as a line item rather than a column.

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight
loud

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
High-end editorial still life seen from directly above. A long white thermal-paper receipt lies vertically on a calm warm-white paper surface, positioned in the left third of the frame, with its top edge slightly curled and a finely torn bottom edge. It is printed with many thin graphite line items, each an abstract bar of similar length with no characters, evenly spaced from top to bottom. One single line item in the lower half is different: it is printed as a thick, solid warm-amber bar that starts inside the receipt like any other row and then runs horizontally off the right edge of the receipt, continuing across the empty paper surface for most of the frame width, many times longer than the receipt is wide. Flat printed ink, crisp edges. Soft daylight from the upper left, gentle paper shadow. Generous empty warm-white space above the amber bar on the right. Palette: warm white, ink graphite, one warm amber. Quiet, exact, editorial. 16:9 landscape.

## Negative Prompt
No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no barcodes, no logos, no hands, no people, no robots, no glowing AI, no neon, no screens, no dashboards, no 3D gloss, no gradients, no extra objects, no clutter.

## Mode Constraints
One relation: one line item overflows the bill. Three elements only.

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Do not render the amber bar as tape or ribbon; it is printed ink continuing past the paper edge.
