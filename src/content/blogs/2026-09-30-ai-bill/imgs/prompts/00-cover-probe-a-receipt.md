# Prompt: 00-cover-probe-a-receipt

## Role
Cover concept probe A. Article thesis: AI subscriptions are becoming cloud bills; for agentic coding the line item that matters is the re-read (cache reads).

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight
loud

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial (warm-white print, graphite structure, paper objects, one safety-color signal)

## Positive Prompt
A high-end editorial still life, seen from slightly above. One long white thermal-paper receipt lies diagonally across a calm warm-white paper surface, its top end still slightly curled. The receipt is printed with many short, thin, evenly spaced graphite rows, all abstract marks with no legible characters. Exactly one row is different: a single thick warm-amber bar that is enormous compared with every other row, stretching almost the full length of the receipt and running off its bottom edge onto the surface below, like one line item swallowing the whole bill. Soft natural daylight from the upper left, gentle paper shadows, crisp focus, generous negative space on the right third of the frame. Restrained palette: warm white, ink graphite, one warm amber accent. Flat editorial print texture, calm and precise, magazine cover quality. 16:9 landscape.

## Negative Prompt
No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no logos, no brand marks, no robots, no glowing AI, no circuit boards, no neon, no dashboards, no hands, no people, no 3D render gloss, no gradients, no clutter, no extra objects.

## Mode Constraints
One metaphor, at most three visual elements (receipt, amber line, surface). Implies the thesis without explaining it.

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor. No diagramming.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Image models invent fake receipt text; all rows must be abstract bars, not words.
