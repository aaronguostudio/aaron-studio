## Positive Prompt
A high-end editorial still life on a calm warm-white wall. A plain matte subscription card, completely blank, graphite-colored with no printing, is half inserted into the slot of a classic analog utility meter: a round glass-domed electricity meter with a thin spinning metal disc and a row of small dials. Where the card enters the slot, the meter's dial needles are swung hard toward the top of their range and a single warm-amber indicator flag has popped up. The idea: a flat-rate pass being turned into a metered utility. Soft side daylight, precise shadows, generous negative space on the left third. Restrained palette: warm white, ink graphite, brushed steel, one warm amber accent. Flat editorial product photography feel, quiet and exact. 16:9 landscape.

## Negative Prompt
No readable text, no numbers on the dials, no letters, no logos, no brand marks, no credit card numbers, no chip, no robots, no glowing AI, no neon, no dashboards, no people, no hands, no clutter.

## Mode Constraints
One relation: flat card becomes metered usage. Max three major elements.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
