# Style Brief: Itemized Receipt

- **Base visual language:** `src/content/strategy/visual-language.md` (palette and restraint), not Soft Glass.
- **Baseline:** Depart from Soft Glass Narrative for this post; keep its restraint, negative space and one-idea-per-image rule, using Field Signal Editorial for the cover and a deterministic editorial chart system for the body.
- **Cohesion model:** Controlled Mix (see `style-directions.md`).
- **Article thesis:** AI subscriptions are becoming cloud bills; for agentic coding the line item that matters is the re-read (cache reads).
- **Reader emotion:** recognition, then numerate calm.
- **Real-world anchor:** a printed receipt / bill.
- **Data layer meaning:** amber = the dominant or decisive number. Neutral warm grey = everything else.
- **Palette:** warm white (~#F6F4EF), graphite ink #1E2124, secondary #565A60, footnote #8B8E93, neutral bar #CBC6BC, amber accent #D2701C; chart 04 only: muted slate #71808E (OpenAI) and muted clay #B08F6E (Anthropic).
- **Type:** Helvetica Neue (charts); Helvetica Neue Condensed Black (thumbnail headline).
- **Recurring motif:** one amber bar that outweighs everything around it.
- **Image set rules:** one message per image; charts use direct labels, title states the claim, subtitle one line, footnote cites source; generated images carry no text.
- **Anti-patterns:** vendor logos, robots, glowing AI, dashboards, coins/credit cards, generated numbers, gradients, 3D, account names, file paths.
- **Quality checklist:** exact numbers from the brief; nothing clipped at 1920x1080; readable when scaled to ~800px wide; thumbnail readable at 320px.
