## Density Budget

0 narrative body images, 5 light data charts, 0 dense mechanism diagrams. This deliberately breaks the 70/20/10 default: the article is a priced receipt, and the charts double as video slides. Mitigation: charts 01 and 04 duplicate existing Markdown tables, so the blog can use either the table or the chart there (see `outline.md`).

## Style Fatigue Guardrail

Zero glass, zero glow, zero route lines. Amber appears once per image as the single signal.
