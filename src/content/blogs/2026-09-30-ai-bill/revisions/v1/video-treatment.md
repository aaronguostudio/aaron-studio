## Directions Considered

### A. Receipt Ledger Editorial (selected)

- **Spine:** the film is the bill. Chapters append as `LINE 00–11`; plan terms, prices, totals, decisions and steps are typeset receipt rows with dotted leaders; strikes mark what is going away; one amber accent means cache reads only.
- **Scene mix:** about 75% typeset structured scenes, about 15% evidence exhibits (meter, bill-by-model, model-vs-vendor charts), about 10% stills (cover bookends, one earlier-post callback).
- **Intensity:** calm exhibits and quotes, structured ledger scenes, one ~11 s signature beat.
- **Signature:** at "The big number was cache reads", the CACHE READS row turns amber and its bar runs off the receipt to the frame edge (the cover, made literal), then 78 / 76 / 58% append.
- **Music:** none unless an approved, rights-cleared bed exists (see Music below).
- **Tradeoff:** typography-led; relies on pacing of row appends rather than imagery. Cheapest and most coherent with the baseline.

### B. Chart Exhibit Film

- **Spine:** the five verified charts carry most chapters as framed exhibits, with typography only for quotes and decisions.
- **Tradeoff:** fast to build and very evidence-forward, but drifts toward the rejected legacy pattern (chart after chart), and the charts' orange means four different things (discount, capped week, cache reads, repriced), which breaks the one-accent rule.

### C. Terminal Log Investigation

- **Spine:** the investigation staged as a stylized terminal: session files scrolling, a pricing script summing tokens.
- **Tradeoff:** strong "I opened my logs" energy, but it invites fake UI and risks exposing paths, account IDs or client project names (privacy rule), and it is the dashboard/terminal collage the baseline rejects.

## Visual Direction (A)

- Porcelain `#F6F4EF` (the charts' own ground, so exhibits sit flush), graphite ink `#1E2124`, warm greys, raised receipt paper.
- **One accent, one meaning:** receipt amber `#D2701C` = cache reads / the re-read (cache-read column, the overflowing line, "re-reading it", "cheaper cache reads", "for me: the re-read"). The lost $10 discount, the capped meter and the repricing are carried in ink (strike, box, bold), and the two charts whose orange means something else (meter, repricing) are shown desaturated as graphite exhibits.
- Serif (Georgia) for claims and quotes; sans for explanation and captions; monospace for ledger rows, labels and honesty tags.
- Honesty tags on every personal number: "API LIST PRICE, NOT WHAT I PAID · ONE MACHINE". Community numbers labeled "ONE X USER'S ESTIMATE · UNVERIFIED". Forecasts labeled "MY INFERENCES".
- Privacy: no file paths, account IDs, client/project names or second Codex account on screen; the step-01 walkthrough names folders only in words ("session files", "project logs").

## Motion Direction

- Every scene opens on a meaningful state (title, scaffold, source row) and changes within ~0.3 s (8-frame fade-up from porcelain after a clean cut; no crossfade between two dense layouts).
- Rows append on the narration cue (opacity + 10 px settle); strikes draw left to right; connectors draw before their arrowheads; the 5x bracket label appears only when the bracket completes.
- s16 → s17 is one continuous receipt (no cut) so the reveal is the same object changing.
- Signature budget: one beat (s17, 10.7 s, about 2% of runtime).
