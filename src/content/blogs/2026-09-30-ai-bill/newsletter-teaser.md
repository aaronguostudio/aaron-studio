# Newsletter Teaser

Subject: I priced my own AI bill

OpenAI is halving what the $200 Pro plan buys on October 30, and said the new plan "nets out at half the dollar in API spend." That sentence turns a subscription into a prepaid API budget.

So I priced 30 days of my Codex and Claude Code logs at list price. Three findings changed my plans: both $200 plans are heavily subsidized, but the Codex meter was the one that capped out; 58-78% of my cost was the agent re-reading its own context; and switching models moved the bill more than switching vendors.
