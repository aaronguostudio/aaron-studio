## Counterarguments

- "Still a great deal": even halved, $200 buys thousands in API-equivalent for heavy users. True, and the article should say so. The point is the unit and the trend, not that OpenAI is stingy.
- "Your numbers are n=1": yes; the article offers them as a receipt and a method, not a benchmark.
- "Cheaper models aren't as good": the Sol switch is untested for Aaron's work; present as a test.
- "Claude may do the same": Anthropic already meters Fable on Max as usage credits at API rates; don't frame Claude as the permanent good guy.
