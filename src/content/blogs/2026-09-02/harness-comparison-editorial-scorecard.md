# Editorial Scorecard — Your Prompt Is Not the Request

Status: **Argument Lock passed. Article Lock pending Aaron's review and replicated H1 evidence. Package Lock not started.**

## Editorial Contract

- **Reader:** A builder or engineering/product lead who uses coding agents daily and is tempted to compare prompts or token counts without inspecting the harness.
- **Job:** Decide what can be fairly measured about an agent's default context, cache reuse, and capability surface before optimizing it.
- **Promise:** Reader leaves with a four-question audit and knows why raw usage receipts must precede a vendor comparison.
- **Opening:** Three installed coding agents are asked for `OK`; their receipts expose an incompatible accounting problem before any benchmark result exists.
- **Contribution:** Upgrades the prior DSH series from a source teardown to a cross-harness field method, while preserving strict evidence boundaries.
- **Boundary:** No model-quality ranking, dollar-cost claim, live DSH token measurement, private trace disclosure, or numerical four-harness leaderboard.
- **Hypothesis:** The counterintuitive operational claim — “your prompt is the last thing that entered the room” — plus an explicit method should earn qualified builder reading and reproducible replies.
