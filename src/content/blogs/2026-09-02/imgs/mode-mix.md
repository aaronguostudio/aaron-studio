# Mode Mix

## Set-Level Pacing

One loud cover/thumbnail pair followed by two medium, calm evidence cards. No decorative body art.

## Image Plan

| Image | Role | Visual mode | Style family | Visual weight | Reason | Reuse candidate? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00-cover | Cover | Editorial Minimal | Editorial Workbench | loud | Turns the shipping incident into a human-scale entrance to the hidden runtime. | yes |
| 00-cover-thumbnail | Thumbnail | Editorial Minimal + typography | Editorial Workbench + Executive Brief | loud | Needs a concise distribution headline. | no |
| 01-tool-sequences | Comparison evidence | Operator Diagram | Executive Brief + Field Signal Editorial | medium | Shows actual first-turn paths without prose. | yes |
| 02-follow-up-receipts | Cache evidence | Operator Diagram | Executive Brief + Field Signal Editorial | medium | Separates session, cache, and outcome. | yes |

## Density Budget

- narrative/metaphor: 1 cover world;
- light diagrams: 2;
- dense mechanism diagrams: 0.

## Style Fatigue Guardrail

Glass is limited to one cover gate and subtle panel depth. No glowing route lines in the evidence cards.
