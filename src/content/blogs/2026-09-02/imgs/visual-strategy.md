# Visual Strategy

## Article Character

Technical framework / know-how, anchored in one concrete shipping incident.

## Reader Emotion

Curious first, then convinced by evidence. The set should feel like a field report from a real workbench, not a vendor comparison deck.

## Visual Risk

- generic glowing AI imagery;
- fake terminal output or invented metrics;
- four-logo comparison energy;
- unreadable screenshot text;
- too many diagrams competing with the prose.

## Argument Beats That Need Images

| Beat | Faster visual understanding | Loss if removed |
|---|---|---|
| Same incident, different paths | Four harnesses can reach the same diagnosis through visibly different tool sequences. | The comparison remains an assertion instead of an observed trace. |
| Follow-up reuse | Session persistence, provider cache, and successful output are separate receipts. | The central cache insight is easy to miss in prose. |
| Cover | A tiny checkout receipt opens a larger hidden runtime. | The article loses its story anchor and becomes abstract. |

## Concept Routes

| Route | Central predicate | Composition | Best role | Why it fits | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | One shipping receipt enters a transparent layered inspection machine. | Human-scale workbench, receipt and small parcel foreground, layered mechanism behind. | Cover | Connects the real incident to the hidden harness. | Can drift into sci-fi machinery. |
| B | Four investigators leave different evidence trails around the same parcel. | Top-down workbench with four distinct tool trails converging on one marked line. | Cover | Makes comparison immediate. | Too many objects and accidental logos. |
| C | Five receipts bridge Prompt to Answer. | Minimal paper bridge with five stamped checkpoints. | Framework/closing | Encodes the final operator checklist. | Too conceptual for the opening. |

## Selected Concept Route

User-directed evidence-first set; Agent-selected Route A. It gives the article a concrete story without turning the cover into the comparison table.

## Recommended Mode Mix

- Editorial Minimal for cover and thumbnail.
- Operator Diagram for the two evidence cards.
- Limited Glass / Fluent Artifact only for the translucent hidden-runtime layer.

## Cover Direction

A $79.99 shipping receipt and small parcel on a warm editorial workbench. The receipt passes under one translucent layered inspection gate before an answer card emerges. No text, logos, robots, dashboards, or neon.

## Body Image Direction

Deterministic terminal-style evidence cards using exact sanitized test results and raw product field names. Large type, few rows, readable at mobile width.

## Framework / Diagram Direction

Two light diagrams only: observed tool sequence and follow-up receipts. No extra conceptual chart.

## What To Avoid

Vendor logos, decorative token totals, fake code, private paths, session identifiers, invented cache normalization, sci-fi HUDs, and dense chart walls.
