# Prompt: 00-cover-candidate-a

## Role

Clean article cover, concept Route A.

## Visual Mode

Editorial Minimal.

## Visual Weight

Loud.

## Style Family

Editorial Workbench with restrained Soft Glass Narrative accent.

## Positive Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 16:9 blog header image
Primary request: a small Canadian ecommerce shipping incident reveals the hidden machinery between a user prompt and an AI answer
Scene/backdrop: premium human-scale editorial workbench, warm white paper surface, quiet graphite background
Subject: one small cardboard parcel and one order receipt showing only an abstract $79.99 line, passing beneath a single translucent inspection gate; a clean answer card emerges on the other side
Style/medium: high-end flat editorial illustration with tactile paper and softly dimensional objects, restrained liquid-glass accent only on the inspection gate
Composition/framing: wide landscape, parcel and receipt in the lower middle, generous negative space, no more than four major objects
Lighting/mood: calm studio daylight, curious, evidence-led, premium but not luxurious
Color palette: warm white, ink graphite, soft cyan, amber, one muted coral error mark
Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no vendor marks, no robot, no human figures, no watermark

## Negative Prompt

Avoid neon, sci-fi HUD, dashboard, server racks, brains, glowing orbs, isometric SaaS diagrams, dense machinery, extra receipts, and fake labels.

## Mode Constraints

One visual predicate: the receipt passes through a hidden inspection layer before becoming an answer.

## Density Budget

Narrative/metaphor; four major objects maximum.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Do not explain all four harnesses in the cover.
