# Style Directions

## Article Fit Summary

The article needs technical credibility and scanability while retaining a human incident anchor. Exact evidence must remain deterministic; generated art is limited to the cover world.

## Style Library Candidates

| Candidate | Source style family | Best role | Why it deserves consideration | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate A | Editorial Workbench | cover | Gives the shipping incident physical reality and authored texture. | Can become dark or overly luxurious. |
| Candidate B | Executive Brief | evidence cards / thumbnail | Handles exact fields and compact comparisons cleanly. | Can resemble generic slides. |
| Candidate C | Field Signal Editorial | connective accents | Adds warm paper, graphite, coral failure, and green verification signals. | Too much texture can hurt small text. |

## Style Direction Menu

| Direction | Visual character | Best use in this article | Why it fits | Risks | Whole post or accent? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workbench Field Report | Tactile desk scene plus crisp evidence artifacts. | cover + evidence cards | Best match for a real test story. | Needs strict texture restraint. | whole post primary |
| Executive Cache Brief | Clean dark grid, monospace data, strong highlights. | evidence cards + thumbnail | Maximizes scanability. | Too corporate if used for cover. | functional accent |
| Paper System Sketch | Pencil paths and receipt fragments. | cover/body metaphor | Feels like learning in public. | Exact data becomes less readable. | accent only |

## Style Probe Menu

Not invoked. Aaron requested test output and screenshots rather than open-ended style exploration.

## Style Cohesion Decision

| Cohesion model | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unified | no | Exact evidence and narrative cover need different modes. |
| Controlled Mix | yes | Editorial Workbench cover; Executive Brief evidence cards; Field Signal accents bind them. |
| Experimental Mix | no | Would weaken the field-report credibility. |

## Recommendation

Controlled Mix. Use one warm graphite/cyan/amber/coral palette across a tactile cover and deterministic evidence cards.

## Aaron's Decision

User-directed evidence-first visual requirement; Agent-selected Controlled Mix inside that constraint.

## Prompt Implications

Cover prompts use Editorial Workbench with no text. Evidence assets are rendered from exact HTML, not generated. Thumbnail reuses the selected cover world with deterministic typography.
