# Prompt: 00-cover

## Role

Clean blog cover for “When a Shared Currency Meets a National Failure.”

## Visual Mode

Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight

Loud

## Style Family

Field Signal Editorial / warm muted risograph collage

## Positive Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape blog cover, no text. Primary request: a calm editorial metaphor for a shared system whose weak failure ownership becomes visible at one local failure. Scene: one long paper-cut bridge crossing a quiet deep-teal sea toward a distant Mediterranean horizon; one narrow central span has a small muted-coral structural crack, while the rest of the bridge remains composed. Style: warm muted retro editorial collage, flat geometric paper-cut shapes, subtle risograph grain, magazine-quality. Composition: wide 16:9, bridge extends horizontally with generous cream sky and negative space. Palette: cream, sage, ochre, dusty coral, deep teal, charcoal. Text: none. Constraints: one bridge, one crack, no drama beyond the relation.

## Negative Prompt

No text, flags, euro signs, bank logos, crowds, maps, charts, broken glass, fire, robots, UI, neon, glossy 3D, or stock photo.

## Mode Constraints

Keep the crack small and structurally credible; no more than three major shape groups.

## Density Budget

Minimal.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Avoid “Europe falling apart” imagery; the article concerns responsibility, not spectacle.
