# Visual Strategy

## Article Character

European crisis/history essay with an operator resilience payoff. Reader emotion: shared-system unease; risk: a generic “broken euro” symbol.

## Concept Routes

1. **A shared bridge, one failing plank** — predicate: a small national section of a long shared bridge cracks while other sections remain intact. Selected.
2. **Currency as a building foundation** — predicate: a common floor cracks beneath different rooms. Rejected: too architectural and static.
3. **Spilled coins through a bank door** — predicate: liquidity leaves. Rejected: too literal and sensational.

## Selection

**Agent-selected: Route 1.** It visualises a shared runtime and local failure without blaming a country or turning the article into a chart.

## Revision 2 — Shared Structure With an Unowned Rescue

The original bridge became a near-empty landscape. The v2 concept keeps the shared structure but adds the missing system relationship: five distinct districts on one aqueduct, a dark cutaway below it, and a rescue boat tied to no district. The predicate is still one thing—failure ownership is ambiguous—but the composition makes the institutional problem visible rather than merely decorative.
