# Eurozone Failure Cover Style

Keep the series paper-collage language but use a structural rather than financial-symbol image.

- **Thesis:** happy paths hide unowned failure domains.
- **Palette:** cream, deep teal, sage, ochre; a small muted coral fracture.
- **Motifs:** a long bridge, a thin amber review line, distant Mediterranean horizon.
- **Rules:** no text, flags, currency symbols, bank buildings, crowds, maps, charts, or broken-glass spectacle.
