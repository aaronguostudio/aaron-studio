# Idea

## Topic

**When a Shared Currency Meets a National Failure.** The Greek debt crisis as an architecture story: a shared monetary runtime with national fiscal systems, unclear failure ownership, and a rescue that was dangerous precisely because no rescue could be worse.

## Why Now

Teams are moving agents from isolated chats into shared production systems. Happy-path demos are cheap; recovery, escalation, and blast-radius design are the hard part.

## Target Reader

Builders and operators designing a shared platform, multi-team workflow, or AI agent system with real consequences.

## Reader Pain / Curiosity

The reader can describe what their system does when it works, but may not have assigned who acts, who pays, and who can stop it when a dependent component fails.

## Initial Thesis

A system is not defined only by its happy path. Its real architecture becomes visible when something fails and no one clearly owns the failure domain.

## Why This Should Exist

The Eurozone turns an abstract reliability question into a human story about trust, refinancing, solidarity, moral hazard, and a country that could not simply reach for its own monetary backstop.

## Kill Criteria

Stop if the article says Greece had no lender of last resort at all, makes austerity the sole cause of the recession, or lets the engineering metaphor flatten the political and social stakes.
