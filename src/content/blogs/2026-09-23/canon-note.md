# Canon Note

## Canonical Idea

A shared system is defined by its failure path: who absorbs the loss, who can stop the damage, and who has authority to restart it.

## Reusable Frame

Map the happy path, then name the failure domain, owner, escalation boundary, rollback option, and residual blast radius.
