## One-Sentence Promise
I ran my full blog production pipeline on a fully local stack — DSH 0.1.0-rc.8 + Qwen3.8-27B on my M5 Max — and this post is the receipt: what local bought, what it gave up, and where quality now has to come from.

## Structural Spine (6 sections, 1500-2000 words EN)
1. **Opening — the invoice and the meter** (bottleneck): yesterday's work arrived with an invoice line I couldn't audit; today the same work runs on my own chips. Version-pinned receipt in the first 150 words: DSH rc.8, Qwen3.8-27B Q8_0, M5 Max 128GB, Ollama, no billing surface.
2. **The crossover just became real — market timing**: 27B crossed the daily-task bar (1M downloads/2 days, HF #1, Cline top local in 4 days, AA Index 52 — all with outlets+dates). At the same moment the cloud raised the line: DeepSeek V4-Pro 6->27 yuan peak + peak/off-peak billing. Two curves, one crossing.
3. **What zero marginal cost reveals — the harness takes over the invoice's job**: when the meter disappears, the costs the invoice hid resurface — quality costs. Model margin is smaller locally (Terminal-Bench/HLE gaps, the 21-minute pelican), so enforced gates are the difference between output and slop. Receipt: this session's gates fired for real (style scanner, claim exclusion, fact-check date correction), and the audit is the event log, not an invoice.
4. **Self-referential, on purpose — the conflict of interest in the body**: the machine writing this IS the subject; state it plainly. Receipts are version-pinned; the gates are adversarial (the scanner flags, the ledger excludes, the fact-check corrects my date). This is the only honest writing position for this topic.
5. **What it gives up — the kill line is per work class**: high-difficulty reasoning band, long-horizon repo-level refactors, one-shot highest-ceiling tasks don't transfer. Hardware is not free (128GB has a purchase price) — that's the point: recurring invoice vs amortized purchase, the crossover is a cost-structure decision.
6. **The local crossover test — decision lens + operating rule**: three questions (capability on your check / recurrence / audit), one-hour validation protocol, investment-order shift (harness first, model is a shelf product). Ending sharpens: at zero marginal cost, the system around the model is the only place quality responsibility lives.
