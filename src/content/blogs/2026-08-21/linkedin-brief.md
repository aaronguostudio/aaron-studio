# LinkedIn Post (draft — direct-link-with-preview format)

Your token bill is hiding a cost-structure decision.

I ran my full blog production pipeline — idea, bilingual drafts, quality gates, cover image — on a fully local stack last week: a 27B open-weights model on my own workstation, through an open agent harness. No cloud tokens, no meter, no billing surface at all.

What I found is not that local models are ready to replace frontier models. They are not. The hard band is still the hard band.

What I found is what changes when a 27B-class model clears the bar for the work classes you actually run: the per-token invoice stops being a detail and becomes a structural cost, and the quality responsibility it was quietly covering moves to the machine around the model — the gates, the skills, the memory, the audit log.

That's why I'd ask three questions before moving any recurring agent workload: does the local model clear the bar on your own checks, is the metering structural cost or rounding error, and can your harness's log give you what the vendor's invoice used to?
