# Post-Specific Visual Direction

- Paper field, graphite structure, exactly one safety color (orange 224/92/40) carried by the stop line and seal.
- Three major elements max: receipt card, stop line + seal, die. Secondary dither field is texture, not a fourth object.
- No text in the image (title belongs to the blog shell / thumbnail).
- No glow, glass, fake UI, charts, or photorealism.
- The stop line is the only orange stroke; orange elsewhere appears only as drift specks (death of the invoice), never as UI accent.
- Margins generous; content lives in the upper-left two-thirds, dissolving toward the lower-right die.
