# Style Directions (recommended menu)

## Primary (selected)

**Field Signal Editorial** — warm paper white, graphite structure, one safety color (orange 224/92/40). Matches the DSH-series covers and the essay's arithmetic character. Recommended for the cover; body images (none this post) would use the same family at lower density.

## Rejected For This Post

- **Data Poster**: the cost-curve claim could ride it, but a chart wall on a blog header reads as a stock infographic and would pre-argue a point the text makes better.
- **Soft Glass Narrative**: default body family; would put glass glow on a post about killing the glow economy of cloud compute. Wrong temperature.
- **Editorial Workbench**: business credibility is right, but two-scene (cloud desk vs local) = two predicates = rejected by concept rules.

## Cohesion Decision

Unified (single family, one color signal). No controlled mix — a solo cover post does not need pacing variation.

## Probe Note

No style-probe mode: the article has one strong metaphor and one family fits it; probes would add noise, not information.
