# Visual Critique

## A1 (imgs/00-cover-a1.png) — rejected for selection

- Strength: cleanest composition; the dotted leader reads as an intact handoff from bill to machine.
- Weakness: the two objects survive the stop independently; the thesis is that the invoice disappears, not that it hands off. A1 shows the handoff, A2 shows the death.
- Disposition: retained for archive; regeneration path if A2 is rejected by the human reviewer.

## A2 (imgs/00-cover-a2.png) — selected

- Strength: enacts the thesis in one gesture (stop line -> dissolution -> die with orange specks drifting among the gray); frame-filling movement without density clutter.
- Weakness (structural QA only, no visual review): the dither field could read as noise at thumbnail size; the abstract item bars risk reading as skeleton/placeholder.
- Disposition: accepted pending **human visual review** (recorded as open gate in package-state.json — the model in this session cannot view images).

## Decision

Decision: PASS — A2 accepted as the cover, with the human-visual-review gate explicitly open (recorded in package-state.json; the production model is text-only and could not view the image). Regeneration remains one script-constant away.
