# Editorial Brief

## Reader Pain

Teams keep choosing metrics because they are visible, then discover that the metric has become the work. The gap between “green dashboard” and “better reality” is hard to explain without a concrete story.

## Reader Job To Be Done

Recognize when a proxy is replacing the outcome, and redesign the measurement before delegating it to people or agents.

## One-Sentence Promise

By the end, the reader can tell the difference between reducing a reported liability and reducing the obligation—and apply the same distinction to an agent's success metric.

## Sharp Thesis

Metrics fail less because people are irrational than because intelligent participants adapt to the incentives the system makes legible. A number is useful only while it remains answerable to the reality behind it.

## Concrete Opening

On a family trip to Greece, a question followed me home: Goldman Sachs helped structure a transaction that reduced the reported euro value of part of Greece's foreign-currency debt by €2.367bn. What, exactly, had disappeared?

Name one scene, contradiction, bottleneck, result, or decision. Do not open with the topic in general.

## Original Contribution

The article makes the mechanism understandable without using the swap as a villain shortcut, then treats Goodhart's Law as an engineering and evaluation-design problem rather than a slogan.

State what this article adds beyond the source material and Aaron's prior posts.

## Why Aaron Can Write This

Aaron is not an accountant or historian. He is an operator who designs systems in which proxies, incentives, authority, and verification matter; the travel question is real, and historical facts are sourced.

## Authority And Scope Boundary

The article explains reported treatment at a high level. It does not adjudicate the transactions' legality, calculate the swaps independently, or claim they caused Greece's euro entry or debt crisis by themselves.

State what Aaron knows directly, what is inferred, and what the article will not claim.

## Evidence Needed

- ECB evidence that Greece adopted the euro on 1 January 2001 and ECOFIN had qualified it in June 2000.
- Goldman disclosure/reporting on the €2.367bn and 105.3% to 103.7% figures.
- Eurostat evidence on off-market-swap reporting uncertainty.
- European Commission explanation that public-finance reporting is member-state responsibility.

## Counterargument

Every metric is imperfect; replacing metrics with intuition can create less accountability. The point is to pair targets with reality checks and leading evidence, not to stop measuring.

## Reusable Frame

**Metric → behavior → reality check.** Before rewarding a number, name the behavior it induces and the independent evidence that would show whether the real outcome improved.

## Distribution Hook

“The debt didn't disappear. It moved into the future.” The hook is accessible to founders, PMs, and agent builders who have watched a KPI turn into theatre.

## Success Hypothesis

Concrete finance-story opening plus a single useful evaluation frame should earn qualified reading and replies from product/AI operators; apply the current growth lesson: open with the bottleneck before the abstract framework.

## Kill Criteria

Kill or redraft if the title is not earned in the first 150 words, the financial explanation cannot be made clear, or the agent connection becomes more prominent than the Greek story.
