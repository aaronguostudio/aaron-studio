# Visual Strategy

## Article Character

Reflective business-history essay; reader emotion is curiosity followed by unease. Visual risk: turning Greek finance into a villain cartoon or generic AI art.

## Concept Routes

1. **Paper balance sheet folds into a long road** — predicate: a neat debt sheet unfolds into a path disappearing toward a future horizon. Clear, human-scale, selected.
2. **A balance scale with numbers falling through** — predicate: a ratio balances while coins escape beneath it. Rejected: too literal and moralising.
3. **Two coastline maps joined by a swap ribbon** — predicate: an exchange line alters a map. Rejected: map complexity would overwhelm the metric idea.

## Selection

**Agent-selected: Route 1.** It makes “moved into the future” visible without claiming debt vanished, leaves room for the Greece/travel atmosphere, and works at thumbnail size.

## Revision 2 — Editorial Workbench Collage

User feedback correctly identified that the original route had collapsed the reference language into a sparse landscape. Three probes tested a ledger workbench, a subterranean cutaway, and a torn-paper editorial spread. The selected v2 cover uses the first: a blank ledger, metric objects, arch, map fragment, sea, and island form one connected visual argument. Its predicate remains singular—an obligation is moved onward—but the evidence is allowed to have foreground, middle ground, and distance.

## Revision 3 — Content-Driven Bright Print Probe

The v2 cover had the right instinct about content density: Greece was present as a place; the financial story had tangible materials; a temporal movement could be investigated. The later bright style probes solved lightness and print character but became generic civic still lifes.

This set combines them: use the luminous Mediterranean Risograph language from Part IV Probe I, but derive subjects directly from Part I's verified argument.

1. **Euro Horizon Ledger** — Santorini-like Aegean island, a small Acropolis silhouette, an EU twelve-star blue field, foreign-currency tokens, and a ledger whose coloured contract ribbon moves from the present page into a distinct future ledger; predicate: a euro-reported figure changes while the obligation travels onward.
2. **Swap Across Time** — two reporting horizons in a treasury worktable scene joined by a routed swap ribbon; predicate: classification can move an obligation from today's balance sheet into tomorrow's cash flow.
3. **The Proxy Machine** — a debt-ratio measuring instrument gets a cleaner reading while the larger Greek fiscal landscape behind it remains materially unchanged; predicate: an indicator can improve without the underlying condition improving.
4. **No Whole Owner** — four distinct institutional stations each touch a different segment of one common ledger; predicate: complex systems diffuse accountability.

All are content probes. Avoid invented precise numerical text, bank logos, claims about a single cause of Greece's crisis, and generic Greek decoration.
