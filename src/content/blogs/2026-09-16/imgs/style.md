# Greece Metric Cover Style

Baseline: the warm muted retro editorial collage reference in `2026-06-08`; use its restraint, not its business-dashboard composition.

- **Thesis:** reported improvement can differ from economic reality.
- **Palette:** cream paper, sage, ochre, dusty coral, deep teal, charcoal.
- **Materials:** subtle risograph grain and paper fibres.
- **Motifs:** one folded ledger sheet, one amber road, a small Greek coastal horizon.
- **Rules:** no text, logos, real people, charts, robot, euro symbols, glowing UI, or more than three major shapes.

## Revision 3 — Bright Evidence Risograph

Baseline: retain v2's foreground/middle/distance evidence collage, but replace its dark photographic-workbench rendering with Part IV Probe I's luminous Mediterranean risograph language.

- **Thesis:** a reported debt measure can change while the obligation remains and shifts across time.
- **Cohesion:** Controlled Mix — rich narrative collage for the cover and accountability scene; cleaner explanatory compositions for the swap and proxy images.
- **Visual anchors:** Aegean island / white coastal buildings, distant Acropolis-like silhouette, EU twelve-star blue field, two currency materials, ledger, contract ribbon, debt-ratio measuring instrument, institutional desks.
- **Palette:** warm cream, Aegean blue, vivid teal/sage, terracotta/coral, yellow-gold, black outline; bright throughout.
- **Material language:** risograph ink grain, light halftone, small overprint and registration shifts, sparse engraved contour; avoid photographic lighting.
- **Information rule:** one causal relation per image; historical/civic anchors provide evidence, never empty decoration.
- **Data rule:** do not generate numeric text. The prose retains exact dates and values; imagery shows relation and scale only.
- **Anti-patterns:** generic island postcard, villain bank imagery, financial dashboard, text/labels/logos, robot/AI motifs, dark boardroom, unexplained symbol clutter, or a visual claim that the swap caused euro entry or the entire later crisis.
