# Prompt: 00-cover

## Role

Clean blog cover for “The Debt Didn't Disappear. Greece Moved It Into the Future.”

## Visual Mode

Editorial Minimal

## Visual Weight

Loud

## Style Family

Field Signal Editorial / warm muted risograph collage

## Positive Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape blog cover, no text. Primary request: an elegant editorial metaphor for a financial obligation whose reported number looks smaller because part of the burden has been shifted into the future. Scene: an off-white paper ledger sheet in the foreground folds seamlessly into one long amber road that runs toward a calm distant Greek coastal horizon; one small sun and layered hills. Style: premium warm muted retro editorial collage, flat geometric paper-cut forms, subtle risograph grain, sophisticated magazine illustration. Composition: wide 16:9, ledger left foreground and future road leading toward centre-right, generous negative space. Palette: cream paper, muted sage, ochre, dusty coral, deep teal, charcoal. Text: none. Constraints: one visual idea only; no literal people, no brand marks, no euro signs, no readable numbers.

## Negative Prompt

No text, logos, charts, maps, flags, robots, AI interface, neon, glossy 3D, stock photography, dramatic crisis imagery, or clutter.

## Mode Constraints

No more than three major shapes; the road must be the only directional cue.

## Density Budget

Minimal.

## Prior Failure To Avoid

Do not imply money physically disappeared or turn the cover into a dashboard.
