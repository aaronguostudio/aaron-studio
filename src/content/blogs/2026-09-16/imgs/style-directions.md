# Style Directions

## Recommended Directions

1. **Field Signal Editorial** — tactile warm-white print, paper-cut road and ledger; best fit for cover.
2. **Paper System Sketch** — notebook-like finance metaphor; too quiet for this cover.
3. **Infographic Editorial** — clear metric relation; risks explaining too much.

## Decision

**Agent-selected — Unified:** Field Signal Editorial, adapted from the documented warm muted retro editorial collage reference. User already delegated a similar visual direction.

## Revision 3 — Content-Driven Bright Print Direction

**User-delegated: Controlled Mix.** Use `Mediterranean Risograph Evidence Collage` for the content-rich cover and accountability scene; use `Bright Print Explainer` for the swap and proxy sections. They share cream/Aegean fields, coral/teal/gold inks, fine contour, and printed grain.

| Direction | Role | Why it fits | Risk controlled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mediterranean Risograph Evidence Collage | cover, accountability | Carries Greece, euro architecture, financial materials, and temporal movement in one readable scene | Every object must correspond to an article claim |
| Bright Print Explainer | swap, proxy | Makes the mechanism legible with fewer objects and no fake numerical text | No flowchart, nodes, or game-guide treatment |
