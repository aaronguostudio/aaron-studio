# Editorial Scorecard

## Editorial Contract

- Reader: Builders and curious general readers who remember the Greek crisis but have not seen it as a measurement-design story.
- Reader's job to be done: Recognize when a reported success is being optimized instead of the reality it proxies.
- One-sentence promise: Greece's famous swap explains why an apparently improved score can leave the underlying obligation intact—and why agents need better success criteria.
- Opening scene or bottleneck: The question left by a Greek trip: how can €2.367B appear to shrink without disappearing?
- Original contribution: A narrow, careful historical mechanism becomes a practical evaluation lens rather than a morality tale about Goldman.
- Scope boundary: No claim that the swap got Greece into the euro or caused the whole crisis.
- Success hypothesis: Readers save the mortgage analogy and carry its question into their own dashboards and agents.
