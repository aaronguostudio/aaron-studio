# Director Memo: What I Learned From DeepSeek's Harness (v2)

## The Central Visual Idea

The film is a session log. The essay's core claim — an append-only ledger is the only truth, and the model sees exactly what the ledger allows — becomes the film's own operating structure. Chapters are appended as mono ledger lines (`SEQ 03 · FOLLOW THE MONEY FIRST`); nothing is erased, prior context compresses upward; the single cyan accent means one thing everywhere: what the model is allowed to see, what you are allowed to keep. The viewer doesn't need this explained. By the time narration reaches "every session is an append-only log," the film has been quietly behaving that way for four minutes — the reveal lands as recognition.

## Reference Lessons Applied

- From the 08-02 Clean Indigo film: one visual spine, typography does the arguing, images interrupt rarely and deliberately, 0.42s crossfade continuity, phrase-level captions, brand card with a held chord.
- From Guizang's method: finite layout vocabulary chosen before animation; rhythm from materially different page states (porcelain reading pages, one rare ink page as chapter punctuation, ledger slips as evidence rows); rendered frames inspected, never trusted.
- From The Shader's Gambit: sound establishes the world before the first claim; one spatial peak, earned, not sustained.

## Scene Rhythm

Nine chapters, ~20 scenes, ~603s narration. The arc alternates reading pages (calm) with structured explanation, placing the single signature scene (the 44/3 ledger cascade) at the film's centre — the one moment the film's own grammar becomes the subject. Numbers open cold (47/67 count-up against porcelain, no branding delay — the cover-hero identity beat follows the hook, not precedes it... correction: frame zero carries the cover-hero identity per the first-frame contract, with the 47/67 statement as the first change inside it). Evidence stills (contract, machine, fleet, shelves) arrive roughly every 90-120 seconds as medium resets, each with a mono margin label. The catch chapter drops to near-silence and the film's quietest page. The hour chapter resolves into the two-column close, then the brand card.
