# Prompt: 02-metaphor-contract-reread

## Role
Finding 3 body — the article's own cache metaphor: change one early word, re-read everything after.

## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight
medium

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial (workbench props allowed)

## Positive Prompt
A warm editorial illustration of a long paper contract scroll unrolled across a plain wooden desk, seen from a slight angle. The early portion of the scroll lies perfectly flat and calm, its paragraphs rendered as quiet graphite bars, each early section carrying one small round soft-green stamp — the feeling of "already remembered." Near the beginning, one single word-sized muted-coral mark interrupts a graphite bar. From that point on, every page of the scroll lifts and fans upward, caught mid-re-read, pages curling in the air toward the right edge. A small human figure leans over the desk, one hand holding the fanned pages. In the far corner of the desk sits a small graphite mechanical counter, mid-tick. Warm white paper, ink graphite, soft green, one muted-coral accent only. Generous negative space above the desk. No legible text — all writing is abstract graphite bars, all stamps are plain shapes.

## Negative Prompt
legible text, letters, words, numbers, legal-office stock photo energy, leather, gavels, scales of justice, suits, screens, dashboards, neon, glass ribbons, clutter, more than one coral element

## Mode Constraints
One ordinary anchor (the scroll on the desk); the counter is the only secondary object; the human figure stays small and quiet.

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor. Zero words.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
The lawyer metaphor must not become a literal law office; no courtroom props, no briefcase, no second figure.
