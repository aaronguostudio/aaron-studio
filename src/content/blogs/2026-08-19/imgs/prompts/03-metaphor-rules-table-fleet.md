# Prompt: 03-metaphor-rules-table-fleet

## Role
Finding 4 body — humans set the rules; the fleet executes behind their gate.

## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight
quiet

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
A quiet, warm editorial illustration in a paper world. Foreground, lower left: three small human figures around a plain table, unhurried — one placing a graphite rule-card onto a small stack, one pressing a simple soft-green stamp, one reading a card held up to the light. Behind and above them stands a wide, calm soft-green gate frame. Beyond the gate, receding into soft depth, a long orderly line of identical simplified paper worker figures — flat, silhouette-like, clearly many but individually unremarkable — each carrying one small bright warm-white card, walking a gentle path marked by a single soft-cyan thread that disappears toward the horizon. Warm white and graphite dominate; green and cyan are the only accents. Lots of air and negative space; the foreground table is the emotional center, the fleet is atmosphere.

## Negative Prompt
text, letters, numbers, labels, faces with detail, robots, metal machines, screens, dashboards, network graphs, neon, crowd chaos, marching-army menace, more than two accent colors, clutter

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor. Zero words.

## Mode Constraints
The humans are few and specific; the fleet is many and uniform; the gate separates them visually. AI never appears as machines — only as the uniform paper figures and the cyan thread.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Do not let this become a workflow map or org chart; no arrows, no lanes, no panels.
