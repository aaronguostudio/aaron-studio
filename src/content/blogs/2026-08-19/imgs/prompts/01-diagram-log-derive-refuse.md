# Prompt: 01-diagram-log-derive-refuse

## Role
Finding 1 framework — the set's one mechanism diagram: log → derive → gate.

## Visual Mode
Operator Diagram

## Visual Weight
medium

## Style Family
Neo Diagram Minimal (variant)

## Positive Prompt
A clean operator-grade diagram in a warm-white paper world, five large elements on generous white space, reading left to right: (1) a tall spine of stacked graphite paper ledger strips, bound like a ledger book; (2) one smooth graphite arrow flowing right, splitting into (3) a neat vertical stack of exactly three bright warm-white cards with thin soft-cyan edges; (4) the cards passing through a simple soft-green gate frame; then a final short arrow toward (5) a plain warm-white envelope leaving the frame. Above the gate, one single stray graphite card approaches on its own dotted path and stops flat against a short muted-coral horizontal bar — it does not pass. Large simple paper-cut shapes, soft shadows, zero labels, zero letters, zero numbers. Readable at mobile width.

## Negative Prompt
text, labels, letters, numbers, icons, tiny elements, dense arrows, nested boxes, chart walls, dashboards, screens, code, gradients, neon, 3D, sci-fi, more than five major elements plus the one stray card

## Mode Constraints
Semantic order is a hard constraint: ledger → derived three cards → green gate → envelope; the stray card is blocked BEFORE the gate by the coral bar and never touches the envelope path.

## Density Budget
Light diagram. Five major elements + one stray card. Zero words.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Workflow-3 v7 lesson: a diagram can be beautiful and still wrong — the blocked path must clearly fail, not merge; no invented labels.
