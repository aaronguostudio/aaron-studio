## Positive Prompt
A high-end editorial illustration in a warm-white paper world. A tall, calm wall of small graphite-grey paper ledger strips arranged in quiet even rows fills the left two-thirds — the strips read as soft texture, like a library card catalog seen from a distance. In the middle stands one narrow vertical pane of glass: a slim aperture. Exactly three ledger strips pass through the aperture and emerge transformed — clean, bright, warm-white cards with a thin soft-cyan edge — drifting in a gentle arc toward a small human figure reading at a simple desk in the lower right. A single warm-amber line runs along the floor from the desk toward the viewer. Flat editorial composition, soft paper shadows, generous negative space, palette strictly warm white / ink graphite / soft cyan / warm amber. The feeling: of everything recorded, only three things are allowed through. No text of any kind.
