# Prompt: 04-metaphor-two-shelves

## Role
Closing image — the two-column asset inventory made physical: what travels vs what is bolted down.

## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight
quiet

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
A warm, calm closing illustration in a paper world, split softly into two zones by negative space (no hard divider). Left zone: a small open carry case on the floor, holding just a few warm-white cards, a small plain notebook, and one spool of soft-cyan thread; a single warm-amber line runs from the case toward the left edge of the frame, out of the room. A lone human figure crouches, placing the last card into the case. Right zone: a handsome wall shelf holding beautiful, well-made fixtures — a compact paper machine, a fine instrument, an elegant box — each visibly clamped to the shelf with simple graphite bolts and brackets. Soft even light, warm white and graphite base, amber and cyan as the only accents. The mood is unhurried and honest, not sad: you take what travels; you respect what stays.

## Negative Prompt
text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, screens, dashboards, robots, chains, prison imagery, dramatic lighting, clutter, more than two accent colors, second human figure

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor. Zero words.

## Mode Constraints
Two zones, few objects each; bolts read as matter-of-fact hardware, not restraints; the amber line is the only path element.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Do not moralize the locked side into villainy — no dark palette shift, no broken objects.
