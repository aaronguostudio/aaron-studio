# Prompt: 00-cover-thumbnail

## Role
YouTube thumbnail — cover world + exact-text headline. Never inserted into the article.

## Visual Mode
Editorial Minimal + typography

## Visual Weight
loud

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
A YouTube thumbnail in the same warm-white paper world as the cover, simplified: on the right third, a compact version of the ledger-strip wall with the narrow glass aperture and three bright cards passing through (halve the strip count; keep it calm). The left two-thirds is a deep ink-graphite panel with generous padding, carrying exactly three lines of headline text, left-aligned, in a clean modern sans-serif:
Line 1 (small, soft cyan): "What I Learned"
Line 2 (large, warm white, bold): "From DeepSeek's"
Line 3 (largest, warm amber, extra bold): "Harness"
The only text in the entire image is exactly these words: What I Learned From DeepSeek's Harness. Every other surface is text-free. High contrast, readable at 320 pixels wide.

## Negative Prompt
any text other than the permitted headline words, subtitles, captions, labels, watermarks, logos, screens, code, dashboards, robots, network graphs, neon, sci-fi, clutter, busy background behind the text zone

## Mode Constraints
Permitted words verbatim and nothing else: "What I Learned" / "From DeepSeek's" / "Harness". Dark text zone must stay calm; background element count halved vs cover.

## Density Budget
Narrative/metaphor + typography. Exactly 6 words of text.

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Image models invent captions and engrave words on objects — explicitly no text on ledger strips, cards, desk, or floor.
