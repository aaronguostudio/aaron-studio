# Visual Strategy

## Article Character

Technical framework / know-how — a verified teardown with an operator-strategy tail. Default mode mix for this character: Operator Diagram + Human-Scale Metaphor + limited Glass / Fluent Artifact.

## Reader Emotion

"Someone finally opened the machine and showed me the parts" — earned trust, curiosity, and a quiet urge to go apply the three adoptables. Not awe, not hype, not sci-fi.

## Visual Risk

- The subject is a codebase: strongest pull toward exactly the banned imagery (glowing dashboards, terminal screenshots, network graphs, isometric SaaS explosions).
- Numbers everywhere (44/3, 12,293, 27, 209) invite chart-wall or fake-legible-text failures; the plan.md v4-era scatter-plot ideas are formally superseded by this file.
- Four findings tempt four diagrams — would fail the 70/20/10 budget and editorial rhythm; most body images must stay metaphor-first.
- The lawyer metaphor risks stock-photo legal-office energy if rendered literally.

## Argument Beats That Need Images

| Beat | What the image lets the reader grasp faster | Lost without it |
|---|---|---|
| Cover — the article's signature fact | The post's promise: the machine is opened, and its rules are visible | Scroll-stop; the teardown identity |
| Finding 1 — log → derive → refuse | The one mechanism worth a diagram: context is recomputed from the log, mismatches refuse to send | The article's most adoptable idea stays abstract |
| Finding 3 — the lawyer re-reads the contract | Why one changed word re-prices everything after it | The cache economics stays a money paragraph with no felt cost |
| Finding 4 — the fleet and its rules | 37 humans set rules and judge evidence; the fleet executes | The "management document" reading stays statistical |
| Closing — two-column asset inventory | Portable vs locked-in, the reader's one同-week action | The CTA loses its object |

Cut candidates: a finding-2 body image (one-row switch) is optional — if the cover uses Route B it is covered; otherwise it competes with the finding-1 diagram for the light-diagram slot and the weaker one is cut.

## Selected Concept Route

**Route A — The 44/3 gate** (Aaron's choice, 2026-08-15 in session). Route C stays as the finding-3 body image per the menu's standing note; Route B is not used (finding 2 has no body image at Balanced density).

## Recommended Mode Mix

- Cover: Editorial Minimal (loud) in the selected route's world.
- Thumbnail: same world + typography, dark text zone (loud).
- Body 1 (finding 1): Neo Diagram Minimal / Operator Diagram — the article's one allowed mechanism diagram (medium).
- Body 2 (finding 3): Human-Scale Metaphor — the contract scene (medium).
- Body 3 (finding 4): Human-Scale Metaphor — small human figures at a rules-table in the foreground, a calm fleet of identical worker shapes receding behind a green gate (quiet).
- Body 4 (closing): Human-Scale Metaphor — two shelves/columns: a small carry-case of portable items vs bolted-down fixtures (quiet).
- Optional Body 5 (finding 2, only at Rich density): Operator Diagram — identical config stacks, one extra row appended, the whole surface changed (medium).

Budget check at Balanced density (4 body images): 3 metaphor / 1 light diagram / 0-1 dense — inside 70/20/10; exactly one quiet-or-less image guaranteed; at most the cover and thumbnail are loud.

## Cover Direction

One scroll-stopping fact rendered as a physical scene, no text, no screens: the selected route's predicate carried by paper, glass aperture, thread, or contract — an "opened machine" feeling built from documents, because this repo's own texture is documents (markdown outnumbers TypeScript).

## Body Image Direction

Metaphor-first, one predicate each, generous negative space; the AI layer appears only as the established motifs (soft cyan thread for delegated work, green gate for review, warm white memory objects for the log). Each body image is a pause, not a second argument.

## Framework / Diagram Direction

Exactly one mechanism diagram in the set (finding 1): three large shapes — log spine, derived context, gate that refuses — no fake text, no more than five elements, readable at mobile width.

## What To Avoid

- Terminal windows, code screenshots, glowing dashboards, network-node graphs, robot/brain/orb imagery.
- Fake readable text anywhere except the approved thumbnail headline.
- More than one diagram-weight image; four-findings-four-diagrams symmetry.
- Countable-clutter in the 44/3 scene; chart-wall energy around the repo statistics.
- Legal-office stock photography energy in the contract scene.
