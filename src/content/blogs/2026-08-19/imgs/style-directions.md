# Style Directions

Concept routes are recorded in `visual-strategy.md` (selection pending in the same session as this menu). The families below were chosen to serve any of the three routes; the "best use" column notes route-specific fit where it differs.

## Article Fit Summary

A verified technical teardown that must feel evidence-led and tactile, not futuristic: the subject repo's own texture is paper (markdown outnumbers TypeScript, 683 design notes, a rejected/ freezer). Reader emotion: earned trust, opened-machine curiosity. Risk level: high pull toward banned dashboard/code imagery; needs one coherent world with functional variants, not style-per-finding.

## Recommendation

Controlled Mix on Direction 1 (Field Signal Editorial primary + Neo Diagram Minimal for the one diagram). Baseline statement: **depart from Soft Glass Narrative as primary for this post; keep its restraint, human scale, palette discipline, and motif vocabulary (cyan thread / green gate / amber intent / warm-white memory objects) while the material language moves from glass to paper — the subject is a machine made of documents.**
