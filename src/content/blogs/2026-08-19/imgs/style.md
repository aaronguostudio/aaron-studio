# Visual Style Brief: What I Learned From DeepSeek's Harness

## Base Language

Soft Glass Narrative (brand continuity layer)

Baseline: **Depart from Soft Glass Narrative as the primary family for this post; keep its restraint, human scale, negative space, palette discipline, and motif vocabulary while the material language moves from glass to paper — the subject is a machine made of documents.** Glass survives only as the cover's narrow aperture.

## Post-Specific Style Name

Paper Machine Teardown

## Selected Style Cohesion Model

Controlled Mix — Field Signal Editorial primary; Neo Diagram Minimal variant for image 01 only (per style-directions.md, Aaron's decision 2026-08-15).

## Selected Style Families And Roles

- Field Signal Editorial: cover, thumbnail, bodies 02/03/04.
- Neo Diagram Minimal: image 01 (the set's one mechanism diagram).

## Article Thesis

Inside DeepSeek's harness are four verified designs with their costs — and the durable layer for builders is the one every harness reads.

## Reader Should Feel

Trusted, curious, oriented, quietly equipped — "someone opened the machine and the parts are legible."

## Human-Scale Anchor

Paper as machinery: ledger strips, contract scrolls, stamps, tags, index cards — documents doing mechanical work, with small human figures reading, stamping, and judging.

## AI / Glass Layer

AI never appears as screens or robots. Delegated work is the soft cyan thread; review/verification is the green gate or green stamp; human intent is the warm amber line; the log/memory is warm-white paper objects. Glass appears exactly once: the cover's narrow aperture that 3 of 44 records pass through.

## Palette

Warm white paper, ink graphite structure; accents strictly from the brand set — soft cyan (model-visible/delegated), soft green (review/remembered), warm amber (human intent/hero), muted coral (the one re-pricing mark in image 02), used one or two per image.

## Image Mix

4 body images: 3 narrative/metaphor + 1 light mechanism diagram + 0 dense (Balanced; 70/20/10 ✓). Cover + thumbnail additional, loud.

## Recurring Motifs

Amber road line, soft cyan thread, green gate/stamp, warm-white memory paper — plus post-specific: the graphite ledger strip (a logged event) and the narrow aperture (model visibility). The ledger strip may recur in B/C/D posts as series identity.

## Image Set Rules

- Cover: no text of any kind; 44 strips must read as calm texture (rows of quiet graphite paper), exactly three bright cards passing the aperture; one small reading figure for scale.
- Thumbnail: same world, left/top dark zone, headline only (permitted words listed in the prompt; all other text banned).
- Bodies: one predicate each; one or two accent colors; high negative space; no fake readable text (stamps are shapes, not words; contract lines are graphite bars).
- Diagram (01): max five major elements, no labels, large shapes, mobile-readable.

## Anti-Patterns

Terminal windows, code screenshots, dashboards, network graphs, robots/brains/orbs, isometric SaaS explosions, halftone grime, legible fake microcopy, legal-office stock energy, glass ribbons outside the cover aperture, chart walls around the repo numbers.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The image can be described in one sentence.
- [ ] It has a human-scale anchor or strong paper-mechanical metaphor.
- [ ] The AI layer is secondary (motif-level) unless it is image 01.
- [ ] It has enough negative space.
- [ ] It avoids fake UI text and chart clutter; no readable text outside the thumbnail headline.
- [ ] It belongs to the Paper Machine Teardown series (graphite paper world, brand accents).
