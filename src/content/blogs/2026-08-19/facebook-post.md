## Post Copy

I spent the past few days doing something I haven't done in years: reading a stranger's codebase end to end. DeepSeek open-sourced the system that runs their coding agents, and instead of reading the hot takes I went through the repo itself — a few hundred claims, checked one by one against a pinned commit.

What stayed with me isn't the technology. The repo is a record of 37 people supervising a fleet of AI agents for two months — twelve thousand commits, most of the code not typed by humans — and it holds together because of rules, not magic: what the AI is allowed to see, what gets checked by machines, and which judgment calls deliberately stay with people.

I wrote up the four designs worth learning — three of them I'm taking into my own work.

If you're using AI for real work: what's one rule you've had to write down for it?
