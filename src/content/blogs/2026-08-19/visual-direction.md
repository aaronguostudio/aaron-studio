# Visual Direction: Graphite Ledger Editorial (ledger-editorial-v0.1)

## Guizang Influence

This film uses the Guizang hybrid pass (tiles/aaron-video-gen/references/guizang-hybrid.md): Style A "Editorial Magazine × Electronic Ink" method, re-grounded in this article's own material — the ledger. What Guizang contributes is method, not skin: a finite layout vocabulary chosen before animation; one visual spine with one dominant reading hierarchy and one anchor accent; rhythm from materially different page states rather than more cards; image proportions bound to the selected layout; dependencies revealed in order; rendered frames inspected rather than trusted.

## What The Film Borrows

- The magazine page as the base metaphor: porcelain paper field, generous margins, serif display claims, mono marginalia.
- Ledger slips, seq markers, proof rows, and running heads as the structural vocabulary (replacing UI panels and cards).
- Chapter punctuation through page-state changes (a rare near-ink frame is punctuation, never the default canvas).
- The discipline of static keyframes per layout family before any motion version.

## What The Film Intentionally Excludes

- The blog illustration set as full-frame slides (editorially rejected for video 2026-08-03; stills survive only as bounded scene media).
- Green as an accent (blog-set semantics stay in the blog); the film's ceiling is two accents: ledger cyan (Signal) and muted coral (Tension).
- Dark dashboard canvas, bordered card grids, persistent chrome beyond the header rail, fake UI, fake social frames, decorative sprites (semantic budget starts at 0).
- Guizang's AGPL implementation: principles are reimplemented in Remotion's editorial layout engine; no HTML/CSS/shader/assets are copied.
- Clean Indigo's palette identity: the method continues, the system is new and belongs to the DSH series (B/C/D posts may reuse `ledger-editorial-v0.1` and amortize it).

## Relationship To The Baseline Visual System

`aaron-editorial-visual-system.md`'s geometry, grid, layout engine, dependency rules, transform envelopes, and preflight contracts all apply unchanged. The `signal-editorial-v0.1` dark palette is superseded for this film by the `ledger-editorial-v0.1` tokens in layout-manifest.json (the reference file itself says the baseline is a versioned candidate, not a frozen brand).
