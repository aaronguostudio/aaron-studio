## Body

Two days after Composio showed the same model swinging from 46.7% to 66.7% task success depending on which harness ran it, DeepSeek open-sourced its harness — the layer that decides those swings, complete rulebook included.

So I spent a few days inside the repo, every claim pinned to one commit. Four designs stood out: a session log the model cannot escape (44 event types, 3 visible to it), an agent loop that is literally one row of config, cache discipline enforced by a CI test, and the clearest public record yet of AI building a complex system — including the management structure that makes it work.
