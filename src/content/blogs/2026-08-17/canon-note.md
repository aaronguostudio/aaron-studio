# Canon Note

## Canonical Idea

AI becomes most useful in everyday life after a plan has stopped being enough. Its practical role is often context recovery: helping a person locate the next useful document, question, translation, product detail, or writing thread without pretending to own the final decision.

## Reusable Frame

Use AI to find the next step. Identify who owns the final call.

This is a bounded decision rule, not an AI-travel framework. It applies when the AI can search, translate, organize, or compare, while a carrier, official source, product owner, or the person themselves remains accountable for the consequential choice.

## Claims Added

- A personal travel record can show AI value without becoming an itinerary guide or a productivity sermon.
- “No Google Search” can describe a change in the entry point for small context questions while preserving Maps and primary-source research as distinct tools.
- A product release can be a credible personal-story receipt when it is scoped to observable work rather than customer-outcome claims.

## Claims Updated

- Earlier “AI operating system” writing is updated with a smaller lived expression: the system is visible when it helps someone re-enter context under real-world friction.
- AI-assisted work does not imply constant availability. The relevant choice is whether work can be resumed in a bounded window and put away again.
