# Editorial Scorecard

## Editorial Contract

- Reader: Already-AI-native builders, product operators, and independent creators who travel but still occasionally need work to move.
- Reader's job to be done: Decide how to use AI in a live, imperfect travel context without surrendering judgment or turning a holiday into always-on work.
- One-sentence promise: A firsthand Greek travel record that shows AI handling research, language, code context, product work, and writing—plus the checks it cannot replace.
- Opening scene or bottleneck: A time-sensitive boarding-document problem shortly before embarkation.
- Original contribution: A non-theoretical, on-the-ground account of AI after the itinerary has already failed to cover reality; DrumNext supplies a real product receipt.
- Scope boundary: No legal or immigration advice; no claim that AI resolved the boarding decision; no universal remote-work or connectivity claim.
- Success hypothesis: The concrete scenes earn deeper reading than a generic AI-travel guide, and the DrumNext update makes the story credible to builders without becoming a product pitch.
