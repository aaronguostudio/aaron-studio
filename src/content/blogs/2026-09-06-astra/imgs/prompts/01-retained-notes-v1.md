## Positive Prompt
Use case: illustration-story. Create one landscape 16:9 editorial illustration for Aaron Guo's essay about GPT-6 Astra in real work. Refined magazine cartoon, warm ivory paper, clean graphite outlines, softly textured flat shapes, muted teal and small ochre accents. Mature, restrained, calm, generous negative space. No photorealism, no robots, brains, glowing AI orbs, neon, vendor logos, dashboards, fake UI or readable text. No borders, captions, watermark. Quiet body illustration, preferably 3:2 within landscape canvas. A large open paper folio lies on an uncluttered desk, seen at a gentle three-quarter angle. Its earlier pages fold back into a short accordion, each bearing only a few abstract graphite strokes and one small crossed-out route; a single teal bookmark visibly connects one earlier note to the current open page. A modest pencil rests alongside. Predicate: earlier discoveries remain findable as long work continues. Two main objects only: folio and pencil. No hands, no person, no digital screen, no legible letters or numbers. Light intimate paper-system-sketch mood, most of the scene is quiet ivory space.
## Negative Prompt
No fake evidence, labels, vendor logos, neon or clutter.
