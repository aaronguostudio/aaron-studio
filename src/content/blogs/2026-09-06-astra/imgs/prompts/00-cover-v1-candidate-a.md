## Role

## Visual Weight

## Style Family

## Positive Prompt
Use case: illustration-story. Create one landscape 16:9 editorial illustration for Aaron Guo's essay about GPT-6 Astra in real work. Refined magazine cartoon, warm ivory paper, clean graphite outlines, softly textured flat shapes, muted teal and small ochre accents. Mature, restrained, calm, generous negative space. No photorealism, no robots, brains, glowing AI orbs, neon, vendor logos, dashboards, fake UI or readable text. No borders, captions, watermark. Cover candidate A. One continuous long paper work-order bends across three neighboring physical work surfaces in a horizontal panoramic composition: a small open laptop on a desk at the left, a simple dark equipment cabinet on a slightly lower workbench in the middle, and a neatly finished paper document on a bright right-hand desk. A simplified adult builder in teal stands near the left edge calmly setting the task in motion, not posed at camera. The continuous paper sheet visibly bridges the gaps so the whole piece reads as one job continuing across different working environments. Keep the cabinet and laptop subsidiary; three primary zones maximum. Image has no words at all. High clarity and a strong silhouette at mobile thumbnail size. Upper left 30 percent remains mostly ivory for a separate title treatment, but do not draw text.
## Negative Prompt
No fake evidence, labels, vendor logos, neon or clutter.
## Mode Constraints
One image, one visual predicate; symbolic illustration.
## Density Budget

## Prior Failure To Avoid
