# 03-review-lamp-v1

## Role
Body illustration
## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor
## Visual Weight
quiet
## Style Family
Paper editorial / Cartoon Briefing
## Positive Prompt
Use case: illustration-story. A quiet 16:9 editorial illustration in clean graphite ink and soft flat colors on warm ivory paper. A simplified adult in a muted teal sweater sits in profile at the far right of a desk and thoughtfully examines a finished paper document under one warm ochre desk lamp. Face is small and dignified, natural hands, no pose toward camera. The finished document is the focal point in the pool of lamplight. A tidy stack of completed pages rests nearby. The left half is mostly empty warm ivory, useful for native video text. Predicate: completed delegated work receives thoughtful verification. Calm and optimistic, not anxious or surveillance-like. No readable text, laptop, dashboard, red warning marks, magnifying glass, robot, neon, brand or watermark. Soft textured flat editorial cartoon, restrained composition, no photorealism.
## Negative Prompt
No extra labels, logos, artificial evidence or visual clutter.
## Mode Constraints
One narrative predicate. Reference is illustrative, not documentary.
## Density Budget
Narrative metaphor.
## Prior Failure To Avoid
Tiny unreadable thumbnail titles and overly dense desks.
