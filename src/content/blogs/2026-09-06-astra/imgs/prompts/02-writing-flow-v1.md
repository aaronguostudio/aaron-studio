# 02-writing-flow-v1

## Role
Body illustration
## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor
## Visual Weight
quiet
## Style Family
Paper editorial / Cartoon Briefing
## Positive Prompt
Use case: illustration-story. One restrained landscape 16:9 editorial illustration. On a warm ivory table seen from above, three small loosely scattered paper fragments on the left gently become one open, coherent manuscript on the right. The fragments have only abstract graphite marks, and the manuscript has long flowing grey lines arranged in calm paragraphs, NO actual letters, words or numbers. A single muted-teal ribbon lightly connects the fragments to the manuscript's spine. Rich tactile paper, clean mature magazine illustration, subtle pencil texture and graphite silhouettes, a small warm ochre corner-tab. Most of the frame is quiet negative space. Predicate: scattered thoughts become naturally connected prose. No red pen, no correction marks, no computer, no person, no charts, no neon, no logos, no watermark. Different composition from an open notebook: overhead asymmetrical still life, left fragments, right unified sheet.
## Negative Prompt
No extra labels, logos, artificial evidence or visual clutter.
## Mode Constraints
One narrative predicate. Reference is illustrative, not documentary.
## Density Budget
Narrative metaphor.
## Prior Failure To Avoid
Tiny unreadable thumbnail titles and overly dense desks.
