# Astra visual strategy
Character: timely product analysis with first-person operator evidence. Emotion: recognition and useful possibility. Risk: generic AI launch imagery and fabricated performance comparisons.
## Three concept routes
A. A red pencil set down beside a work card: less repeated correction. Human-scale cover; one action, low density.
B. A finished PDF held just before an empty attachment pocket: output is one checkpoint. Strong narrative cover, but needs context.
C. Two clocks on one desk, one beside a working hand: waiting and active attention differ. Strong body metaphor; weaker recognition of Astra on its own.
Recommendation: A cover, B narrative body, C cost section. This is a concept recommendation, not a selected final image set.
No generated source screenshots, brand imitation or decorative benchmark numbers. Formal prompts wait for style selection.
