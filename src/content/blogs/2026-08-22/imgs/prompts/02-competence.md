# Prompt: 02-competence

## Role
Body narrative — the "competence" gift: a skill is a file you load, not a phrase you retype.

## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight
quiet

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
Warm-white editorial print illustration. A human hand places a single blank paper card into a small slot reader on a calm desk; a soft green checkpoint light passes through the card as it slots in. A few other blank cards fan nearby, still unloaded. Ink-graphite line work, flat editorial composition, lots of negative space, warm and quiet. No text.

## Negative Prompt
No text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no logos. No glowing card, no screens with readable UI, no robot, no dashboard, no clutter.

## Mode Constraints
One metaphor, one human anchor (the hand + card), AI layer = one green checkpoint light, secondary.

## Density Budget
narrative/metaphor

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Do not print fake text on the card; the card stays blank. Keep the green light as a single checkpoint, not a glow field.
