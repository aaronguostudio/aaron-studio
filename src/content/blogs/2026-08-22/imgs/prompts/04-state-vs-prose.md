# Prompt: 04-state-vs-prose

## Role
Body (frame) — "watch the state, not the prose": the hand checks the ledger, not the polished page.

## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight
medium

## Style Family
Paper System Sketch

## Positive Prompt
Warm paper system-sketch illustration, hand-drawn pencil line work on warm-white paper. A human hand points at a simple open ledger with three blank columns and a check mark, while a neat stack of finished, polished pages sits off to the side, untouched. A soft green check accent on the ledger, one cyan thread leading from the ledger. Low-tech thinking-in-public feel, calm, generous negative space. No readable text.

## Negative Prompt
No readable text, no words, no letters, no numbers. No chart wall, no spreadsheet with tiny cells, no dashboard, no robot, no clutter, no messy scribbles, no unreadable handwriting.

## Mode Constraints
One metaphor (state vs prose), keep the ledger simple with blank columns, no fake labels.

## Density Budget
narrative/metaphor

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Do not fill the ledger with readable text or tiny grid cells; three blank columns and one check mark only.
