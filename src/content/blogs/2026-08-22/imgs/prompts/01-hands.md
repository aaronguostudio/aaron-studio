# Prompt: 01-hands

## Role
Body narrative — the "hands" gift: the tool surface becomes a programming language.

## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight
medium

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
Warm-white editorial print illustration. One human hand writes a single line of marks on a sheet of paper with a pencil; from the written line, one soft cyan thread branches to four simple everyday tool objects — a document, a terminal window, a folder, and a magnifying glass — that all move in quiet sync. One hand moving many tools at once, orchestration instead of reaching. Ink-graphite line work, flat editorial composition, generous negative space, calm and warm. No text.

## Negative Prompt
No text, no words, no letters, no numbers. No robot arm, no screens with readable UI, no dashboard, no clutter, no extra objects, no neon, no HUD.

## Mode Constraints
One metaphor, one human anchor (the writing hand), AI layer = one cyan thread, secondary.

## Density Budget
narrative/metaphor

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Do not turn the four tools into four separate glowing panels; keep them simple quiet objects on one thread.
