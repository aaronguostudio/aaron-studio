# Prompt: 03-continuity

## Role
Body narrative — the "continuity" gift: the job carries its own state after you walk away.

## Visual Mode
Human-Scale Metaphor

## Visual Weight
quiet

## Style Family
Field Signal Editorial

## Positive Prompt
Warm-white editorial print illustration. A calm desk seen from a slight distance, chair empty and pushed back; on the desk a single cyan thread of work keeps moving on its own — a small checklist with a check mark and a few papers advancing by themselves. Warm window light, ink-graphite line work, flat editorial composition, generous negative space, quiet and calm. The person is absent; the work continues. No text.

## Negative Prompt
No text, no words, no letters, no numbers. No visible person, no robot, no screens with readable UI, no glowing orb, no dashboard, no clutter, no heavy motion blur.

## Mode Constraints
One metaphor, the empty chair as the human anchor (by absence), AI layer = one cyan thread, secondary.

## Density Budget
narrative/metaphor

## Prior Failure To Avoid
Do not make the papers fly chaotically; keep the movement calm and linear. Keep the chair clearly empty.
