# Long films, standalone shorts, and separate language editions

Use this workflow when an author-approved video is expanded into a longer documentary and shorter distribution assets. The singing-sands package at `src/content/videos/singing-sands-long-2026-09-08` is the first implementation, not an author-approved universal baseline.

## Expand through questions

Measure the narration with the production voice before locking runtime. Add evidence-bearing questions, experiments, limitations, and carefully bounded related applications. Do not extend holds or slow the voice merely to meet a duration. Keep primary sources attached to each claim. State explicitly when an application belongs to related science rather than being a direct spin-off of the featured discovery.

## Score chapters

Generate or select music after searching the asset library. Give each chapter a brief musical entrance before narration begins; duck the music bus from the voice signal without processing the approved voice unnecessarily. Leave genuine field recordings unscored where listening is the point. Save source music, cue offsets, fades, gain, provenance, rights status, and the exact final mix. Verify both visuals and the final encoded soundtrack; technical loudness measurements do not constitute listening approval.

## Derive independent shorts

Select start and end positions at measured word boundaries. Each short needs an immediate hook, enough context, and a completed takeaway. Reuse the exact master mix range when possible; keep a short ending tail. Re-layout for portrait with large captions and room for platform controls, then inspect actual rendered frames. A center crop of the long film is insufficient when it cuts explanatory diagrams or source labels. Record the master hash, source in/out, portrait composition, final duration and output hash. Preserve the source and rights chain.

## Separate language editions

Share the research, approved imagery, conceptual scenes and rights records. Keep English and Chinese scripts, narration files, word timings, captions, on-screen labels, chapter titles, and renders separate. Rebuild timing from each language's actual voice track. Do not reuse English timing for Chinese narration or mix bilingual labels by default. A request to consider a future edition does not require producing it immediately.

## Gates

Review new scene capabilities in a short rendered prototype before full export. Validate source evidence and production preflight, decode final outputs, inspect chapter transitions and portrait safe areas, and probe audio/video duration and loudness. Serve local review files with HTTP byte ranges so chapter seeking works. Keep author feedback, technical QA, commercial rights verification and publication status distinct. Do not imply that longer runtime or more shorts guarantees reach; distribution results require later measurement.
