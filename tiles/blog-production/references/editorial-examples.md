# Editorial Examples

Read only an example matching the current problem. These come from author
feedback on drafts in September 2026; they are calibration examples,
not proof that later skill changes produced the accepted versions.

## Preserve the center: DHH

- Before: “听完 DHH 和 Lex Fridman 的这次采访，我很想去试试 Linux。”
- Author correction: the point is agreement with his attitude to AI; Omarchy can
  be admired but should remain peripheral. Include the team's differing trust.
- Accepted opening: “最打动我的是他谈到 AI 时的兴奋。” The next paragraph connects
  that response to colleagues who trust AI differently.
- Lesson: an authentic-sounding personal detail can still be the wrong center.
  Choose the detail by the author's intent, not by its novelty.

## Context before components: Astra

- Before: introducing Windows Worker before the reader understood the task.
- Author correction: explain the complex deployment across Linux, Windows,
  Azure resources, network configuration and SQL certificates, without exposing
  project identities.
- Lesson: technical specificity becomes useful after the reader knows what was
  being achieved. Use only verified details; do not manufacture a dramatic failure.

## Preserve the experience: Astra writing

- Before: writing improvement explained mostly through fewer correction rounds.
- Author correction: emphasize smoother, more natural writing and reading.
- Accepted judgment: “我更喜欢和它一起写出来的文字了。” It remains a personal
  experience, not a quantified benchmark of the model.
- Lesson: an operational proxy may fail to express what the author values.

## Let the ending land: Astra

- Before: a final deployment plan followed by “一件原本需要我在几个工具之间来回处理的事，终于往前走了。”
- Author correction: bring together increased trust in long, complex work,
  verification and audit challenges, price, and anticipation of further progress.
- Accepted final sentence: “下一次进步，我希望继续在真实工作里感受到它。”
- Lesson: the ending earns that expectation by first gathering the experience
  and acknowledging the costs. A compact synthesis can help; neither a fixed
  number of paragraphs nor an obligatory call to action is the rule.

## A clear topic can hide an undeveloped view: upstream essay

- Draft headings included “走近真实工作” and “把结果说清”. The model passed
  its editorial review and the wording scanner found no issues.
- Author feedback, 2026-09-07: the article was bland, kept moving between
  positive and negative qualifications, and left little worth remembering.
  It should explain how to prepare for work over the next five to ten years
  and convey why the author cares.
- Diagnosis: familiar good practices were listed without developing why AI
  changes their priority or feasibility. Source details became endorsements;
  the author's ambition was reduced to a few small workflow improvements.
- Revised direction: recommend deeper business involvement and responsibility
  for whole projects; show what changed in the work and which capabilities
  repeated projects could build. The full revision uses five distinct choices:
  industry knowledge, customer contact, product decisions, commercial value,
  and following a project into use.
- Subsequent author feedback: “新的这版还不错，有一定的提高”, followed by
  authorization to continue the blog workflow. This is an author-accepted
  improvement, not a claim of perfection or a controlled test of the skill.
- Accepted opening: “很多年前在亚马逊工作时，我就有这样一个判断”. The
  author explicitly rejected “不太舒服的感受”; the later layoff discussion
  follows with “我并不惊讶”. Keep a considered judgment as a judgment.
- Example weighting: the cross-system reporting task shrank to one sentence
  with a link and its development-environment boundary. Repeated Linux/Windows
  details distracted from the career argument. Conversely, the earlier Astra
  case needed those components after its task context. Detail follows purpose.
- Lesson: strengthen the reasoning and author choice before sentence polish.
  Distinguish necessary factual limits from automatic balancing. Preserve this
  model-PASS/author-rejection example alongside the accepted revision. Author
  evaluation and mechanical checks answer different questions.

  the rejected draft, the intermediate probes, the full revision, and the
  author's acceptance. Use this historical comparison for calibration; a later
  matched-input run is needed to evaluate the changed skill itself.
